Lithia occupies a unique position in south Hillsborough County as a community defined by two distinct property types. The western portion is dominated by FishHawk Ranch, one of Tampa Bay's largest and most amenity-rich master-planned communities, spanning over 3,000 acres with multiple villages, resort-style pools, fitness centers, miles of walking trails, and A-rated schools. FishHawk homes were built by national and regional builders (David Weekley, Ashton Woods, Homes by WestBay) primarily between 2000 and 2020, featuring CBS/stucco construction with tile roofs from 1,400 to over 4,000 square feet. The Encore village offers 55+ living with 240 single-family homes.

Eastern Lithia transitions into rural Florida with larger acreage properties, horse farms, custom-built homes, and estates along the Alafia River corridor. These properties often include pole barns, detached workshops, fencing, and other structures that expand painting scope beyond a typical suburban home. The contrast between FishHawk's HOA-governed communities and Lithia's rural properties means a Lithia painting contractor must be equally comfortable with subdivision-standard work and custom estate projects.

Local Climate

Lithia-Specific Painting Considerations

Lithia shares Tampa Bay's subtropical climate but has distinctive micro-conditions driven by its geography. Properties near the Alafia River and nature preserves experience higher ambient humidity than developments further from water features, promoting faster mold and mildew growth on exterior surfaces. FishHawk's extensive tree preservation areas and nature corridors create shade patterns that vary significantly from lot to lot within the same neighborhood — a south-facing home on a preserved lot may face very different conditions than its sun-exposed neighbor across the street.

Rural Lithia properties with large open land exposure face more direct UV radiation than shaded subdivision homes but less moisture concern. We assess each Lithia property individually and select products and prep techniques based on its specific micro-environment, not a one-size-fits-all approach.
