Local Climate

How Riverview's Climate Affects Your Paint

Riverview sits in the heart of Tampa Bay's subtropical climate zone, subject to some of the most demanding conditions for exterior coatings in the entire United States. Average summer temperatures exceed 90°F with humidity regularly above 80%, creating ideal conditions for mold and mildew growth on north-facing and shaded exterior walls. The area receives roughly 50 inches of annual rainfall concentrated in afternoon thunderstorms from June through September, meaning exterior surfaces endure repeated wet-dry cycles that stress paint adhesion.

UV radiation is intense year-round, with Riverview receiving over 240 sunny days annually. South- and west-facing walls experience the most UV degradation, typically showing fading and chalking 1–2 years before north-facing walls. These conditions make proper surface preparation, moisture-resistant primers, and UV-stabilized topcoats essential — not optional — for any exterior painting project in Riverview.
