Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions About Painting Services in Riverview

Interior painting costs in Riverview typically range from $2,500 to $5,500 for a standard 3-bedroom home, depending on room count, ceiling height, wall condition, and the number of coats required. This includes all preparation, primer, two coats of premium paint, and complete cleanup. Cabinet painting and specialty finishes are priced separately. Contact Ayala Pro Painting for a free, detailed estimate specific to your home.
A typical exterior painting project for a single-family home in the Riverview area takes 3 to 5 days, depending on the home’s size, condition, and weather. Our process includes a full day of preparation (power washing, scraping, caulking, priming) before any painting begins. Florida’s afternoon storms can occasionally extend timelines, which is why we schedule exterior work during the drier months whenever possible.
For Florida exteriors, we recommend 100% acrylic latex paints with UV-resistant and mildew-inhibiting properties, such as Sherwin-Williams Duration or Benjamin Moore Aura Exterior. These products are specifically formulated to resist the cracking, peeling, and fading caused by Tampa Bay’s intense UV exposure and high humidity. For interiors, we use low-VOC formulations that resist moisture and mildew in bathrooms and kitchens.
For exterior painting, you do not need to be home. We coordinate access to your property and provide daily progress updates via text or phone. For interior painting, we recommend being present on the first morning so we can walk through the project scope together, but you’re welcome to go about your day after that. We treat every home with the same respect we’d give our own.
Yes. Every residential painting project is backed by a written 2-year workmanship warranty. If any area we painted shows premature peeling, bubbling, cracking, or fading within the warranty period, we will return and repair it at no additional cost. Commercial projects carry a 1-year workmanship warranty. Our warranty is in addition to the manufacturer’s warranty on the paint products themselves.
Absolutely. Ayala Pro Painting is a Florida-licensed painting contractor with full general liability insurance and workers’ compensation coverage. We carry a minimum of $1 million in general liability coverage to protect your property. Our license and insurance information is available upon request, and we are happy to provide a certificate of insurance to your HOA or property management company.
