Why Refinish Instead of Replace

Love Your Layout, Hate Your Cabinet Color?

If your cabinets are structurally sound but dated, refinishing delivers a dramatic transformation without the cost and disruption of replacement. Cabinet painting is the right choice when:

  • Your cabinet boxes and doors are solid but the color or finish is dated
  • You want a modern white, gray, navy, or custom color kitchen
  • Wood-tone or oak cabinets feel dark and out of style
  • Replacement quotes ($20,000+) are far beyond your budget
  • You’re refreshing a kitchen or bath before selling your home
  • You want a high-end, factory-smooth finish — not a brushed DIY look

How We Work

Our Cabinet Refinishing Process

1

Free Estimate & Color Help

Eliseo evaluates your cabinets, discusses color and finish options, and delivers a detailed, itemized estimate within 24-48 hours.

2

Remove & Label

We remove and carefully label every door, drawer front, and piece of hardware so everything goes back exactly where it belongs.

3

Clean, Degrease & Sand

We thoroughly degrease and sand all surfaces — the critical step that lets the new finish bond and last in a hard-working kitchen.

4

Bonding Primer

We apply a high-adhesion bonding primer so the enamel grips slick or previously finished surfaces and resists chipping.

5

Spray-Applied Enamel

We apply multiple coats of premium cabinet-grade urethane enamel for a smooth, durable, factory-like finish with no brush marks.

6

Reassembly & Walkthrough

Once cured, we reinstall doors, drawers, and hardware, then walk the finished kitchen with you to confirm you love the result.
