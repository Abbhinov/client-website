Signs You Need This Service

Overwhelmed by Paint Color Choices?

Choosing paint colors is one of the most stressful parts of a painting project for most homeowners. Common challenges include:

  • Thousands of color options creating decision paralysis
  • Colors looking different in the store versus on your walls at home
  • Undertones (pink, green, yellow) appearing unexpectedly in certain lighting
  • Difficulty visualizing a color covering an entire room versus a small swatch
  • Coordinating colors between rooms, trim, ceilings, and exterior elements
  • Wanting to update your look but unsure what styles suit your home’s architecture

How We Work

How Our Color Consultation Works

1

In-Home Assessment

We visit your home to evaluate natural and artificial lighting, existing furniture and decor, flooring, countertops, and architectural features that influence color selection.

2

Style Discussion

We discuss your preferences: warm or cool tones, bold or subtle, modern or traditional. We look at inspiration images and discuss the mood you want each room to convey.

3

Palette Development

Based on our assessment, we develop 2-3 coordinated color palette options using Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore color systems. Each palette includes wall, trim, ceiling, and accent colors.

4

Large Samples

We apply large-format paint samples (12x12 inch minimum) directly on your walls so you can see each color in your actual lighting conditions throughout the day.

5

Final Selection

After living with the samples for 24-48 hours and seeing them in different light, we finalize your color choices and confirm the complete palette for your project.

Why Choose Us

Why Professional Color Consultation?

We See Undertones

Every white has an undertone (pink, blue, green, yellow). We identify which undertones work with your fixed elements like flooring, countertops, and furniture.

Lighting Matters

A color that looks perfect in a south-facing room may look completely different in a north-facing room. We evaluate every room’s lighting conditions.

Cohesive Flow

Open floor plans need colors that flow naturally from room to room. We create palettes that maintain visual harmony throughout your home.

Save Money on Mistakes

Repainting because you chose the wrong color costs time and money. Getting it right the first time is what consultation delivers.

Florida-Specific Knowledge

Riverview’s abundant natural light affects how colors read. We know which colors thrive in Florida’s bright, warm light versus looking washed out.
