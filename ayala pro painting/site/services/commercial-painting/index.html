How We Work

Our Commercial Painting Process

1

Site Assessment & Scope

We visit your property, evaluate surfaces, discuss timeline constraints, and document access requirements. Estimate delivered within 48 hours.

2

Schedule Coordination

We build a project schedule around your business operations. After-hours, weekends, phased approaches — whatever minimizes disruption.

3

Preparation & Protection

Protect equipment, inventory, fixtures, and flooring. Prep surfaces per commercial standards. Coordinate with building management for access.

4

Professional Application

Apply commercial-grade coatings with appropriate techniques for each surface type. Maintain clean work areas throughout.

5

Inspection & Handover

Final walkthrough with your designated contact. Address any concerns on-site. Provide warranty documentation and maintenance recommendations.
