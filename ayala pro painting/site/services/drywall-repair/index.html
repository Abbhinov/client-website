Signs You Need This Service

Does Your Drywall Need Professional Attention?

Drywall damage ranges from minor cosmetic issues to structural concerns. Professional repair ensures invisible results that DIY patches rarely achieve:

  • Nail or screw pops creating small bumps or circles visible through paint
  • Holes from doorknobs, anchors, accidents, or removed fixtures
  • Cracks along seams, corners, or around windows and doors from settling
  • Water stains or soft/bubbling drywall from leaks or humidity damage
  • Mismatched texture from previous amateur repairs that stand out
  • Popcorn ceiling removal leaving uneven surfaces needing finishing

How We Work

How We Repair Your Drywall

1

Damage Assessment

Evaluate the type and extent of damage. Identify the cause (settling, moisture, impact) to ensure the repair addresses the root issue, not just the symptom.

2

Removal & Prep

Cut out damaged drywall, remove loose material, and prepare the area for patching. For water damage, we ensure the moisture source is resolved before proceeding.

3

Patching & Taping

Install new drywall sections or apply appropriate patch materials. Tape all joints with paper or mesh tape and apply initial coats of joint compound.

4

Mudding & Sanding

Apply multiple thin coats of joint compound, sanding between each coat for a smooth, flat surface. This step requires patience and skill for invisible results.

5

Texture Matching

Match the existing wall or ceiling texture (orange peel, knockdown, smooth, skip trowel, etc.) so the repair blends seamlessly with the surrounding surface.

6

Prime & Paint

Prime the repaired area and paint to match the surrounding wall. We color-match the existing paint to ensure a uniform finish.
