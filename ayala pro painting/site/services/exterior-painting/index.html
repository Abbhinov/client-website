Signs It's Time to Repaint

Is Florida Weather Wearing Down Your Home's Exterior?

Florida’s climate degrades exterior paint faster than almost anywhere in the country. If your home shows any of these signs, it’s time for a professional repaint:

  • Fading, chalking, or a powdery residue on painted surfaces
  • Peeling, cracking, or blistering paint, especially on sun-facing walls
  • Mildew, mold, or green/black streaks on stucco and siding
  • Bare wood, exposed stucco, or visible primer showing through
  • Cracked or failing caulk around windows, doors, and trim
  • You’re preparing to sell, or your HOA has flagged your home’s appearance

How We Work

Our 7-Step Exterior Painting Process

1

Free Estimate & Assessment

Eliseo inspects your home’s surfaces, identifies repairs, discusses colors, and delivers a detailed, itemized estimate within 24-48 hours.

2

Pressure Wash

We pressure wash the entire exterior to remove dirt, chalk, mildew, and loose paint — included in every exterior project at no extra charge.

3

Scrape, Sand & Repair

We scrape and sand failing paint, repair damaged wood or stucco, and address the root causes of paint failure.

4

Caulk & Prime

We re-caulk gaps and cracks around trim, windows, and doors, and prime all bare and repaired surfaces for maximum adhesion.

5

Protect

We mask and cover windows, fixtures, landscaping, and hardscapes so only the surfaces meant to be painted get painted.

6

Two-Coat Application

We apply two coats of premium, UV- and weather-rated paint using the right technique for each surface, maintaining clean lines throughout.

7

Final Inspection & Warranty

We walk the entire exterior with you, touch up any detail on the spot, and back the work with a written warranty.
