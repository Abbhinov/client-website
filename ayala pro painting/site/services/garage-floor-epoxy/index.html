Signs You Need This Service

Is Your Garage Floor Cracked, Stained, or Embarrassing?

Most Florida garage floors are uncoated concrete that degrades over time. If your garage floor shows any of these conditions, a professional epoxy coating can transform it:

  • Oil stains, rust marks, and chemical spills that won’t clean up
  • Cracks, spalling, or pitting from concrete degradation and moisture
  • Dusty surface that tracks concrete dust into your home
  • Hot tire marks from parked vehicles lifting the concrete surface
  • Previous DIY epoxy that is peeling, bubbling, or flaking off
  • You want to convert your garage into a functional workspace, gym, or showroom

How We Work

How We Install Your Epoxy Floor

1

Concrete Assessment

Evaluate slab condition, moisture levels (calcium chloride test), cracks, and previous coatings. Determine if the concrete can accept epoxy or needs repair first.

2

Diamond Grinding

Mechanically grind the entire surface with diamond-tipped equipment to create the profile needed for epoxy adhesion. This is the critical step that separates professional installs from DIY failures.

3

Crack & Spall Repair

Fill cracks with flexible polyurea filler, repair spalled areas with concrete patch, and level any uneven spots. These repairs are invisible under the final coating.

4

Primer / Moisture Barrier

Apply a penetrating primer that bonds to the concrete and blocks moisture vapor transmission. Florida’s high water table makes this step essential for long-term adhesion.

5

Base Coat Application

Apply the colored epoxy base coat across the entire surface. For flake systems, broadcast decorative chips into the wet base coat for a textured, multi-color finish.

6

Clear Topcoat

Apply a UV-resistant polyaspartic or polyurethane clear topcoat for abrasion resistance, chemical protection, and a glossy or satin finish. This is what makes the floor durable for 10-15 years.

Why Choose Us

Why Choose Professional Epoxy Over DIY Kits?

Diamond Grinding vs. Acid Etch

DIY kits use acid etching which provides weak adhesion. We diamond grind for a mechanical bond 3-5x stronger.

100% Solids vs. Water-Based

Our epoxy is 100% solids — 3-5x thicker than water-based hardware store kits. More material means more durability.

Moisture Testing

Florida’s high water table causes DIY epoxy to peel within months. We test moisture levels and apply vapor barriers when needed.

UV-Resistant Topcoat

Our polyaspartic topcoat won’t yellow from UV exposure through garage windows or open doors. DIY kits yellow within 1-2 years.

10-15 Year Lifespan

Professional systems last 10-15 years. DIY kits typically last 2-4 years before peeling. The math favors professional installation.
