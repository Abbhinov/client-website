How We Work

Our Painting Process, Start to Finish

1

Free Estimate & Consultation

Eliseo visits your property, listens to your goals, evaluates surfaces, and answers your questions. You receive a detailed, itemized estimate within 24–48 hours.

2

Detailed Proposal & Scheduling

We confirm scope, colors, products, and timeline in writing — no surprises — then schedule your project around the weather and your calendar.

3

Thorough Surface Preparation

Power wash, scrape, sand, fill, caulk, prime, and protect. Proper prep is the single biggest reason our finishes last years longer in Florida’s climate.

4

Professional Application

Premium Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore products applied with the right technique for each surface, with clean, tidy work areas every day.

5

Final Walkthrough & Warranty

We walk the finished project with you, address any detail on the spot, and back the work with a written workmanship warranty.
