What We Paint

Transform Every Room in Your Home

Interior painting does more than refresh a color — it protects your walls, brightens your space, and adds real value to your home. Our interior services include:

  • Walls and ceilings in any room, from bedrooms to open-concept living areas
  • Trim, baseboards, crown molding, doors, and door frames
  • Accent and feature walls to add depth and personality
  • Kitchens, bathrooms, and laundry rooms with mildew-resistant paint
  • Hallways, stairwells, and high-ceiling spaces
  • Repainting after a move, renovation, or to cover years of wear

How We Work

Our Interior Painting Process

1

Free Estimate & Color Help

Eliseo visits your home, measures the space, discusses colors and finishes, and delivers a detailed, itemized estimate within 24-48 hours.

2

Protection & Prep

We move and cover furniture, mask floors, trim, and fixtures, then patch nail holes, fill cracks, sand glossy surfaces, and caulk gaps for a flawless base.

3

Priming

We spot-prime repairs and stains — or full-prime when changing colors dramatically — so your topcoat is even, true, and long-lasting.

4

Two-Coat Application

We apply two coats of premium paint with crisp cut-in lines and smooth, roller-mark-free walls, working room by room to keep your home livable.

5

Cleanup & Walkthrough

We remove all materials, reset your furniture, and walk every room with you to confirm you love the result before we consider the job done.

Frequently Asked Questions

Interior Painting Questions

Interior painting in Riverview typically ranges from $300-$800 per room and $2,500-$5,500 for a full 3-bedroom home, depending on room count, ceiling height, wall condition, and the number of coats required. Pricing includes preparation, primer, two coats of premium paint, and complete cleanup. Contact us for a free, detailed estimate.
A single room is usually completed in a day. A full interior repaint of a 3-bedroom home typically takes 3-5 days depending on the scope, the amount of trim and detail work, and drying time between coats. We confirm your timeline in writing before we start.
We use premium low-VOC interior paints from Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore, including mildew-resistant formulations for bathrooms, kitchens, and laundry rooms where Florida humidity is highest. Low-VOC products mean minimal odor so you can return to your space sooner.
We handle most of the heavy lifting. We move and cover furniture, protect your floors, and tape off trim and fixtures. We simply ask that you remove small personal items, wall decor, and fragile belongings from the rooms being painted.
