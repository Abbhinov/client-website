Exterior Cleaning

Professional Pressure Washing in Riverview, FL

Ayala Pro Painting provides professional pressure washing and soft washing services for homes and businesses throughout Riverview, Florida and south Hillsborough County. Florida’s humidity creates ideal conditions for mold, mildew, and algae to accumulate on driveways, sidewalks, home exteriors, pool decks, fences, and roofs. Our pressure washing services use professional-grade equipment and surface-appropriate techniques to safely remove buildup without damaging your property. Pressure washing is available as a standalone service or as part of our exterior painting preparation process.

Signs You Need This Service

Is Mold, Mildew, or Algae Taking Over Your Property?

In Florida’s humid climate, exterior surfaces develop buildup faster than almost anywhere in the country. Professional pressure washing addresses:

  • Black streaks and green algae on driveways, sidewalks, and walkways
  • Green or black mildew on home siding, stucco, and trim
  • Slippery pool decks and patios from algae growth (a safety hazard)
  • Dingy, discolored fences that detract from your property’s appearance
  • Oxidation and chalking on painted exterior surfaces
  • HOA notices about property appearance and cleanliness standards

How We Work

Our Pressure Washing Process

1

Property Assessment

Evaluate each surface to determine the appropriate cleaning method (high-pressure, soft wash, or surface cleaner) and PSI setting to avoid damage.

2

Pre-Treatment

Apply biodegradable cleaning solution to break down mold, mildew, algae, and organic stains. Allow dwell time for the solution to work before washing.

3

Surface Cleaning

Clean all surfaces using the appropriate technique. Driveways and concrete get high-pressure surface cleaning. Stucco and painted surfaces get soft washing to prevent damage.

4

Detail Work

Hand-clean edges, corners, and areas the surface cleaner can’t reach. Remove stubborn stains with targeted treatment and rinse thoroughly.

5

Final Rinse & Inspection

Complete rinse of all surfaces and adjacent areas. Inspect for any remaining spots and re-treat if necessary. Clean up any displaced debris.

Why Choose Us

Why Choose Professional Pressure Washing?

Right PSI for Each Surface

Too much pressure damages stucco, paint, and soft surfaces. Too little doesn’t clean. We adjust for every surface type.

Soft Wash for Delicate Surfaces

Stucco, painted surfaces, and screen enclosures get our soft wash treatment — cleaning power from detergent, not pressure.

Pre-Treatment Chemicals

We pre-treat mold and algae with biodegradable solutions that kill growth at the root, not just blast it off the surface.

Uniform Results

Our surface cleaner attachment eliminates the zebra stripe pattern that wand washing creates on concrete.

Included With Exterior Painting
