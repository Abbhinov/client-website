Common Questions About Home Painting in Riverview

Residential painting costs in Riverview vary by project type. Interior rooms typically range from $300–$800 each, full interior repaints from $2,500–$8,000, and full exterior repaints from $3,000–$12,000. Cabinet refinishing ranges from $3,500–$10,000. We provide free, detailed estimates for every project.
For interior painting, we handle most preparation including furniture moving and floor protection. We ask that you remove small personal items, wall decorations, and fragile items from the work area. For exterior painting, please ensure we have clear access to all sides of the home and move vehicles away from the work area.
