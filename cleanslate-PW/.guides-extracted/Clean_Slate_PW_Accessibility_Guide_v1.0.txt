Section 3: Required Content Sections


Commitment Statement: Clean Slate Pressure Washing is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We are continually improving the user experience for everyone and applying the relevant accessibility standards.


Conformance Target: This website targets conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA.


Measures Taken: Semantic HTML structure, ARIA landmarks and labels, keyboard navigation support, color contrast ratios meeting or exceeding 4.5:1 for text, alt text on all meaningful images, skip-to-content navigation link, focus indicators on all interactive elements, and form labels associated with inputs.


Known Limitations: Third-party content (Google Maps embeds, review widgets) may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. We provide text alternatives where possible.


Feedback &amp; Contact: If you encounter an accessibility barrier on this website, please contact us: info@cleanslatepw.com or (813) 555-0283. We will make reasonable efforts to provide the information or service you need through an alternative accessible method.


Assessment: This website was assessed using automated tools (axe DevTools, WAVE) and manual keyboard/screen reader testing.


Last Reviewed: Display the date of the most recent accessibility review.
