3.1 Exact Copy

Pressure washing costs in Brandon, Florida typically range from $150 for a basic sidewalk cleaning to $800 or more for a full-home exterior soft wash with driveway, depending on home size, number of surfaces being cleaned, and current condition. The most commonly requested service — a single-story house soft wash — costs $299–$449 in the Brandon area as of 2026. Driveway pressure washing runs $189–$349 depending on driveway size and staining severity. Roof soft washing ranges from $349–$599 based on roof size and algae coverage. These ranges reflect pricing from licensed, insured professional companies using commercial-grade equipment — not unlicensed operators using residential-grade equipment, which may quote lower but carry significantly more risk of property damage and no recourse if damage occurs.

Detailed pricing table: House soft wash ($299–$549 by sq ft tiers), roof soft wash ($349–$599), driveway ($189–$349 by size), pool deck ($199–$399), lanai/screen ($179–$349), fence ($199–$399 by linear feet), gutters ($149–$299), commercial (custom quote). Note that ranges reflect Brandon-area market rates from licensed operators.


What Affects the Price?

Property size (sq ft), number of stories, surface material type, contamination severity (light maintenance vs. heavy algae), accessibility (gated community, steep grade, narrow side yards), number of services bundled, frequency (one-time vs. recurring contract).


Bundle Savings

Most homeowners save 10–20% by combining services. Common bundles: house + driveway ($450–$700), house + driveway + lanai ($600–$950), full exterior (house + roof + driveway + lanai, $900–$1,400). Bundle pricing eliminates duplicate setup time and truck mobilization costs.


Red Flags in Pressure Washing Quotes

Quotes significantly below market rate (often unlicensed, uninsured, using residential equipment). No written estimate. Hourly billing instead of flat-rate. No mention of cleaning method (soft wash vs. pressure). No proof of insurance when asked. Payment required before work begins.


Why Flat-Rate Pricing Matters

Flat-rate vs. hourly: incentive alignment (flat-rate companies are incentivized to work efficiently, hourly companies benefit from working slowly). Written estimate protects the homeowner from scope creep and surprise charges.

Q: Why do prices vary so much between companies?

The primary drivers of price variation are licensing and insurance status (licensed/insured companies carry $2,000–$5,000/year in overhead that unlicensed operators do not), equipment quality (commercial vs. residential grade), cleaning method expertise (soft wash capability vs. pressure-only), and whether the company provides a written guarantee.


Q: Is it worth paying more for a licensed company?

Yes. A licensed, insured company provides three protections an unlicensed operator cannot: liability coverage if your property is damaged, workers&apos; compensation coverage if a worker is injured on your property, and a written service guarantee. The cost difference is typically 15–30% — a small premium for significant risk reduction.


Q: Do you offer financing or payment plans?
