3.1 Exact Copy

Hillsborough County HOA communities routinely issue exterior maintenance violation notices for visible algae on roofs, mildew on siding, stained driveways, and discolored fences. Common HOA-governed neighborhoods in the Brandon, Valrico, Riverview, and FishHawk areas enforce exterior cleaning standards through quarterly or annual visual inspections conducted by management companies or volunteer architectural review committees. If you receive a violation notice, you typically have 14–30 days to correct the issue before fines begin — response timelines vary by community covenant. Professional exterior cleaning resolves the most common violations in a single visit, and reputable cleaning companies provide before-and-after photo documentation formatted for HOA compliance submission.
