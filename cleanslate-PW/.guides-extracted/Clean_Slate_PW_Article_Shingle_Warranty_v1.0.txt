3.1 Exact Copy

Most major asphalt shingle manufacturers — including GAF, CertainTeed, Owens Corning, and Atlas — explicitly require that roof cleaning be performed using low-pressure methods only. High-pressure washing (above 100–300 PSI depending on manufacturer) voids the limited warranty coverage by causing granule loss, shingle distortion, and moisture channel creation that accelerates deterioration. This means homeowners who hire a contractor that pressure washes their roof with a standard 2,500–4,000 PSI wand are not only risking physical damage — they are voluntarily voiding their manufacturer warranty, which can represent $5,000–$15,000 in lost coverage on a typical Florida roof.

Why High Pressure Damages Shingles

Technical explanation: asphalt shingles use embedded ceramic granules for UV protection and waterproofing. High-pressure water strips these granules, exposing the asphalt substrate to UV degradation. Additionally, high pressure can lift shingle edges, break the adhesive tar strips that hold shingles flat, and create channels for wind-driven rain to penetrate.


How to Verify Your Specific Warranty

Where to find warranty documents. Key terms to search for: &apos;cleaning,&apos; &apos;maintenance,&apos; &apos;pressure,&apos; &apos;PSI.&apos; If warranty is lost or unavailable, manufacturer websites maintain digital copies searchable by product line.


What Soft Washing Does Differently

Soft wash methodology: chemical treatment at under 100 PSI kills biological growth without mechanical contact with granules. No shingle lifting, no adhesive strip breakage, no granule loss. Fully within every major manufacturer&apos;s maintenance guidelines.

Q: How do I know if my warranty has been voided?

If a contractor has previously used high-pressure washing on your shingle roof, the warranty may already be compromised. However, manufacturers generally do not proactively monitor cleaning methods — warranty disputes arise during claims. If you have documentation of prior high-pressure cleaning, consult your manufacturer&apos;s warranty department for a determination.


Q: Can I get my warranty reinstated after pressure washing?

Generally, no. Warranty coverage lost due to improper maintenance methods cannot be reinstated. However, switching to proper soft washing going forward prevents additional damage and maintains the remaining useful life of the roof.
