Section 1: How to Use This Guide

This guide covers the Contact page (/contact/). It provides all contact methods (phone, email, form, map), business hours, and a streamlined general inquiry form. The Contact page has a stricter LCP target (&lt;1.5s) because it is a direct conversion page — visitors arriving here have high intent.

Section 4: Page Layout — Form First

Two-column layout on desktop: contact form (left, 55%) and business information block (right, 45%). Stacked on mobile: form first, business info below. No hero image — clean white background with minimal distraction. The form is the hero.

5.2 Form Behavior


Inline validation on blur. Specific error messages per field.


reCAPTCHA v3 (invisible, score-based).


Submission endpoint: Cloud Functions API → Zoho CRM + email notification.


On-page success confirmation (no redirect): &apos;Thank you, [Name]! We received your message and will respond within 4 business hours.&apos;


Failure fallback: &apos;Something went wrong — please call us directly at (813) 555-0283.&apos;


GA4 events: form_start (first field focus), form_submit (successful submission).


Submit button: &apos;Send Message&apos;, orange (#E8761A), full-width on mobile, 52px height.

LCP under 1.5 seconds (stricter target for conversion page).


form_start and form_submit GA4 events fire correctly.
