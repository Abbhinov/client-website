6.2 AEO Body — Exact Copy

Clean Slate Pressure Washing provides professional driveway and sidewalk pressure washing for residential and commercial properties throughout Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, and the Tampa Bay region. Unlike delicate surfaces that require soft washing, concrete driveways and sidewalks are cleaned using high-pressure surface cleaning equipment operating at 2,500–4,000 PSI with a rotating bar that delivers an even, streak-free clean across the entire surface. This method removes oil stains, tire marks, rust discoloration, embedded mildew, and years of ground-in dirt in a single pass. Florida&apos;s porous concrete absorbs moisture that promotes rapid mildew colonization, particularly during the humid summer months. Our pre-treatment process applies a detergent solution before surface cleaning to break down organic growth at the root, producing results that last significantly longer than pressure alone.

Section 8: Our Process (5 Steps)

Numbered 5-step process showing exactly how the driveway &amp; sidewalk cleaning service is performed. Each step has a bold heading and 1–2 sentence description. This section builds trust through transparency — homeowners want to know what will happen on their property before they commit.


8.1 Process Steps

Step 1 — Pre-treatment: A degreasing detergent solution is applied to the entire driveway surface and allowed to dwell for 10–15 minutes to loosen oil, organic stains, and embedded grime.

Step 2 — Surface cleaning: Our commercial-grade surface cleaner — a rotating bar with dual spray tips inside a splash-free housing — passes over the concrete at 2,500–4,000 PSI, delivering perfectly even cleaning without the zebra-stripe patterns that handheld wands produce.

Step 3 — Edge detailing: All edges along landscaping beds, garage aprons, curbs, and expansion joints are cleaned with a handheld wand using a fan tip for precision work.

Step 4 — Targeted stain treatment: Any remaining oil spots, rust stains, or battery acid marks receive a second application of specialized chemistry matched to the stain type.

Step 5 — Post-treatment rinse: The entire surface and adjacent areas receive a final rinse to remove all cleaning residue and restore a clean runoff path.

Section 13: FAQ Section

Five questions specific to driveway &amp; sidewalk cleaning. These must match the FAQPage JSON-LD schema exactly.


Q: Can you remove oil stains from my driveway?

In most cases, yes. Fresh oil stains (under 6 months) are almost always fully removed with our degreasing pre-treatment and surface cleaning process. Older, deeply penetrated oil stains may lighten significantly but may not achieve 100% removal depending on the concrete&apos;s porosity and the stain&apos;s age. We assess stain severity during the estimate and set realistic expectations.


Q: How long does driveway cleaning take?

A standard two-car driveway (approximately 400–500 square feet) takes 1–2 hours including pre-treatment, surface cleaning, edge work, and final rinse. Three-car or extended driveways take 2–3 hours. Driveways with heavy staining may require additional treatment time.


Q: Will pressure washing damage my concrete?

No. Concrete is one of the hardest exterior surfaces and is specifically designed to withstand the pressure levels we use (2,500–4,000 PSI). Our surface cleaners deliver even pressure distribution that prevents the etching or streaking that can occur with inexperienced handheld wand use. We also avoid pressure washing on decorative stamped concrete that has a thin color seal — those surfaces receive lower pressure and more chemical treatment.


Q: How often should I have my driveway cleaned?

For most Brandon-area homes, annual driveway cleaning is sufficient to maintain appearance and prevent organic growth from becoming deeply embedded. Properties with heavy tree coverage, irrigation overspray, or vehicles that leak oil may benefit from cleaning every 8–10 months.


Q: Do you clean sidewalks and walkways too?

Yes. We clean all concrete flatwork including sidewalks, walkways, front entry paths, patio slabs, and garage floors using the same surface cleaning equipment and process. Sidewalk cleaning is frequently bundled with driveway service for a complete curb-to-door transformation.
