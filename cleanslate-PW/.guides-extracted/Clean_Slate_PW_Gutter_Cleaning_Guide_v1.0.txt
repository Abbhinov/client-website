6.2 AEO Body — Exact Copy

Clean Slate Pressure Washing provides professional gutter cleaning and exterior gutter brightening services for homeowners throughout Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, and the Tampa Bay area. Gutter service includes two distinct components: interior cleanout (removing leaves, debris, pine needles, and standing water from inside the gutter channel and downspouts) and exterior brightening (removing the dark vertical streaks — called tiger stripes — that form on the outer face of aluminum gutters). Tiger stripes are caused by electrostatic bonding between airborne pollutants, oxidation residue, and the aluminum gutter surface during water runoff. These streaks cannot be removed with regular pressure washing alone — they require a specialized oxidation-dissolving cleaning agent applied by hand and rinsed at low pressure. Both interior and exterior gutter services are performed in a single visit.

Section 8: Our Process (5 Steps)

Numbered 5-step process showing exactly how the gutter cleaning &amp; brightening service is performed. Each step has a bold heading and 1–2 sentence description. This section builds trust through transparency — homeowners want to know what will happen on their property before they commit.


8.1 Process Steps

Step 1 — Interior cleanout: All leaves, twigs, pine needles, shingle granules, and standing water are removed from the gutter channel by hand. Debris is bagged and removed from the property.

Step 2 — Downspout flush: Each downspout is flushed with a garden hose to confirm unobstructed flow. Clogged downspouts are cleared using a plumber&apos;s snake or pressurized water jet.

Step 3 — Flow test: Water is run through the entire gutter system to verify proper drainage slope and identify any sagging sections, loose brackets, or leaking seams.

Step 4 — Exterior brightening: A specialized gutter-brightening solution is applied to the outer face of the gutters using a soft scrub pad. The solution dissolves the electrostatic bond holding tiger stripe deposits to the aluminum.

Step 5 — Rinse and inspection: Gutters are rinsed clean and inspected for any remaining streaks. Problem areas receive a second application. Fascia boards behind gutters are inspected for signs of water damage or rot.

Section 13: FAQ Section

Five questions specific to gutter cleaning &amp; brightening. These must match the FAQPage JSON-LD schema exactly.


Q: What causes tiger stripes on gutters?

Tiger stripes are dark vertical streaks on the exterior face of aluminum gutters. They are caused by a chemical reaction between aluminum oxidation, airborne pollutants, and the water that runs over the gutter lip during rain. The resulting residue bonds electrostatically to the aluminum surface and cannot be removed by pressure washing alone — it requires a specialized chemical treatment.


Q: How often should I clean my gutters in Florida?

We recommend interior gutter cleanout twice per year in Florida — once in late fall after leaf drop and once in late spring before hurricane season. Homes with heavy pine tree coverage may need quarterly cleanout. Exterior gutter brightening is typically needed every 2–3 years depending on the home&apos;s exposure.


Q: Do you install gutter guards?

We do not install gutter guards, but we can assess whether your gutters would benefit from them and recommend a local installer if appropriate. In our experience, gutter guards reduce but do not eliminate the need for periodic cleanout, particularly in areas with heavy pine needle debris.


Q: Can dirty gutters cause damage to my home?

Yes. Clogged gutters cause water to overflow and pool against the fascia board, leading to wood rot, soffit damage, and foundation erosion. Standing water in gutters also breeds mosquitoes — a significant concern in Florida. Regular gutter maintenance prevents these costly issues.


Q: Do you clean gutters on two-story homes?

Yes. We clean gutters on homes of any height using extension ladders and stabilizer bars that protect your gutters from denting. Our technicians are trained in ladder safety and carry appropriate fall protection equipment for two-story and higher work.
