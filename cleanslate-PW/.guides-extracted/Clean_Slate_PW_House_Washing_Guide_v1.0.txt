Section 8: Our Process (5 Steps)

Numbered 5-step process showing exactly how the house soft washing service is performed. Each step has a bold heading and 1–2 sentence description. This section builds trust through transparency — homeowners want to know what will happen on their property before they commit.


8.1 Process Steps

Step 1 — Property walk-around: We inspect the home&apos;s exterior surfaces, identify siding type, note areas of heavy organic growth, and protect landscaping with pre-rinse and tarping.

Step 2 — Solution application: Biodegradable soft wash solution is applied from the bottom up using low-pressure spray tips. The solution dwells for 10–15 minutes, dissolving algae and mildew at the cellular level.

Step 3 — Gentle rinse: Using a wide-angle fan tip at under 500 PSI, we rinse the solution and all dissolved organic matter from top to bottom. No high-pressure wands ever touch the siding.

Step 4 — Detail work: Window frames, soffits, fascia trim, light fixtures, and entry door surrounds are hand-detailed with soft brushes where needed.

Step 5 — Final inspection: Walk-around with the homeowner to confirm every surface meets our standard. Any areas of concern are re-treated on the spot.

Section 13: FAQ Section

Five questions specific to house soft washing. These must match the FAQPage JSON-LD schema exactly.


Q: Will soft washing damage my paint or siding?

No. Our soft wash process uses pressure under 500 PSI — roughly the force of a garden hose. The cleaning agents do the work, not mechanical pressure. This method is safe for painted wood, vinyl, stucco, Hardie plank, and EIFS. We have cleaned over 500 homes in the Brandon area without a single damage claim.


Q: How long does a house wash take?

A typical single-story home takes 2–3 hours. Two-story homes take 3–4 hours. Homes with heavy algae coverage or extensive detail work (shutters, trim, dormers) may take slightly longer. We provide a time estimate with every quote.


Q: How often should I have my house washed in Florida?

For most Tampa Bay homes, we recommend annual house washing. Homes with heavy tree coverage, lakefront properties, or those with north-facing shaded walls may benefit from washing every 8–10 months. Our cleaning agents include a growth inhibitor that extends the time between cleanings.


Q: Is the cleaning solution safe for my landscaping?

Yes. We use biodegradable, plant-safe cleaning agents. Before and after every application, we thoroughly rinse all landscaping, flower beds, and grass areas adjacent to the home. Our solutions break down into inert compounds within minutes of rinsing.


Q: Do you clean second-story homes?

Yes. We clean homes of any height using extension poles and specialized spray tips that reach 35+ feet from ground level. Our technicians never need to walk on your roof or use ladders against your siding — everything is cleaned from ground level for safety.
