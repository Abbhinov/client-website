6.1 What Makes Lithia Unique for Exterior Cleaning


Differentiator 1

Lithia is characterized by larger lots, acreage properties, and rural-suburban estates that distinguish it from the master-planned density of neighboring FishHawk. Properties in Lithia typically have more exterior square footage to clean — longer fence lines, larger driveways, and multi-structure properties (main home plus barn, workshop, or guest house).


Differentiator 2

Lithia&apos;s proximity to the Alafia River and Lithia Springs preserve creates a consistently humid microclimate that accelerates algae and mildew growth on all exterior surfaces. Properties near Lithia Springs Run and the CR-640 corridor experience some of the heaviest organic soiling in our service area.


Differentiator 3

Many Lithia homes sit on well water with high iron content, resulting in persistent orange rust staining on driveways, sidewalks, and any surface exposed to irrigation overspray. Our rust stain removal service is particularly in-demand in the Lithia area — we carry iron-specific oxalic acid treatments on every Lithia job.


Differentiator 4

Lithia&apos;s mix of newer custom homes and established 1990s-era properties means we encounter the full range of exterior materials — from modern stucco and stone veneer to older painted concrete block and wood siding. Each material receives the appropriate cleaning method and chemistry.
