Section 3: Required Content Sections

The privacy policy must address these topics. Have the final version reviewed by legal counsel before publication.


Information We Collect: Name, email, phone, address (from forms). IP address, browser type, pages visited (from analytics). Call recordings (with FL two-party consent disclosure).


How We Use Your Information: To respond to inquiries, provide estimates, schedule services, improve our website, send service-related communications.


Information Sharing: We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared with: CRM provider (Zoho), email service (SendGrid), call tracking (CallRail), analytics (Google). Each processes data per their own privacy policies.


Cookies &amp; Tracking: GA4 analytics cookies, Google Ads remarketing (if applicable), session cookies. Consent managed via cookie banner with Google Consent Mode v2.


Call Recording Disclosure: Florida is a two-party consent state. All call recordings require notification: &apos;This call may be recorded for quality assurance and training purposes.&apos;


Your Rights: Right to request deletion of personal data. Right to opt out of marketing communications. Right to request a copy of stored personal data.


Data Security: SSL encryption on all forms. Data stored in encrypted databases. Access restricted to authorized personnel.


Children&apos;s Privacy: We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.


Policy Updates: Last updated date displayed at top. Material changes communicated via website notice.


Contact: For privacy questions: info@cleanslatepw.com or (813) 555-0283.
