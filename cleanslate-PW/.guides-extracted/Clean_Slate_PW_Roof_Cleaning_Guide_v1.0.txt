Section 8: Our Process (5 Steps)

Numbered 5-step process showing exactly how the roof soft washing service is performed. Each step has a bold heading and 1–2 sentence description. This section builds trust through transparency — homeowners want to know what will happen on their property before they commit.


8.1 Process Steps

Step 1 — Roof inspection: We assess the roof type (asphalt shingle, concrete tile, barrel tile, metal), algae severity, and any pre-existing damage. We photograph problem areas for before-and-after documentation.

Step 2 — Landscaping protection: All plants, shrubs, and grass below the roofline are pre-rinsed and covered as needed. Gutters are checked to ensure proper drainage of the rinse water.

Step 3 — Soft wash application: Our proprietary cleaning solution is applied from ridge to eave using calibrated low-pressure spray equipment operating at under 100 PSI. Solution dwell time is 15–20 minutes.

Step 4 — Gentle rinse: The roof is rinsed with low-pressure water to remove the solution and loosened organic material. No high-pressure wands, no walking on shingles, no surface agitation.

Step 5 — Post-treatment landscaping rinse: All adjacent landscaping receives a thorough fresh-water rinse. Downspouts are checked for proper flow.

Section 13: FAQ Section

Five questions specific to roof soft washing. These must match the FAQPage JSON-LD schema exactly.


Q: What are the black streaks on my roof?

Those dark streaks are Gloeocapsa magma — a cyanobacteria (blue-green algae) that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. It is not dirt, mold, or tree sap. Gloeocapsa spreads via airborne spores, which is why neighboring roofs often develop streaks around the same time. It does not physically damage shingles immediately, but over years it accelerates granule loss and reduces roof reflectivity, increasing cooling costs.


Q: Will roof cleaning void my shingle warranty?

Our soft wash method will not void your warranty — in fact, high-pressure washing is what voids most shingle warranties. GAF, CertainTeed, Owens Corning, and Atlas all explicitly require that roofs be cleaned using low-pressure methods only. Our process stays under 100 PSI, well within every major manufacturer&apos;s guidelines.


Q: How often should I clean my roof in Florida?

Most Florida roofs benefit from soft washing every 2–3 years. Roofs with heavy tree coverage, north-facing slopes, or proximity to lakes and ponds may need cleaning every 18–24 months. Our treatment includes growth-inhibiting agents that slow algae return.


Q: Can you clean tile roofs?

Yes. We clean concrete tile, barrel tile (S-tile), and flat tile roofs using the same soft wash process. Tile roofs require slightly longer dwell times and more solution but respond excellently to soft washing. We never use high-pressure or mechanical scrubbing on tile roofs.


Q: How long does a roof cleaning take?

Most residential roof cleanings take 2–4 hours depending on roof size, pitch, and algae severity. The visual improvement continues for 24–48 hours after cleaning as the remaining dead algae washes away with natural rainfall.
