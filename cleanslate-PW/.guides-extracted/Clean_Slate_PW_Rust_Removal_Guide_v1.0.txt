6.2 AEO Body — Exact Copy

Clean Slate Pressure Washing provides specialized rust and stain removal services for concrete, pavers, and natural stone surfaces throughout Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, and the Tampa Bay region. Standard pressure washing cannot remove many common Florida stains — rust from irrigation well water, orange marks from fertilizer granules, battery acid drips on garage floors, tannin stains from oak leaves and acorns, and black marks from decomposing landscape mulch all require specific chemical treatments matched to the stain chemistry. Our technicians identify the stain type, select the appropriate removal agent (oxalic acid for rust and tannins, sodium hydroxide for organic stains, hydrofluoric acid compounds for severe mineral deposits), apply the treatment, and pressure clean the area after the chemistry has done its work. Most stains require 1–2 treatment cycles for complete removal.

Section 8: Our Process (5 Steps)

Numbered 5-step process showing exactly how the rust &amp; stain removal service is performed. Each step has a bold heading and 1–2 sentence description. This section builds trust through transparency — homeowners want to know what will happen on their property before they commit.


8.1 Process Steps

Step 1 — Stain identification: We determine the stain type (rust, tannin, battery acid, fertilizer, organic, mineral) and the surface material (concrete, pavers, travertine, flagstone) to select the correct chemistry.

Step 2 — Test spot: A small inconspicuous area is treated first to verify the selected chemistry reacts correctly with both the stain and the surface material without discoloration or etching.

Step 3 — Treatment application: The matched removal agent is applied to the stained area and allowed to dwell for the prescribed time. Rust stains require 15–30 minutes; organic stains require 10–20 minutes.

Step 4 — Agitation and rinse: Stubborn stains receive light mechanical agitation with a stiff brush before rinsing. The treatment area is pressure cleaned to remove all chemistry and dissolved stain material.

Step 5 — Second cycle (if needed): Deeply penetrated stains may require a second application. We assess results after the first cycle dries (15–20 minutes) and retreat as necessary at no additional charge within the same visit.

Section 13: FAQ Section

Five questions specific to rust &amp; stain removal. These must match the FAQPage JSON-LD schema exactly.


Q: Can you remove orange rust stains from my driveway?

In most cases, yes. Orange rust stains on Florida driveways are almost always caused by irrigation well water with high iron content. These stains respond well to oxalic acid-based treatments that dissolve the iron oxide without damaging the concrete. Very old, deeply penetrated rust stains may require multiple treatment cycles.


Q: What about fertilizer stains?

Fertilizer granule stains (dark orange or brown spots) are iron-based and respond to the same oxalic acid treatment used for irrigation rust. We recommend applying fertilizer to dry surfaces and watering it in immediately to prevent future staining, or switching to a low-iron formulation.


Q: Can battery acid stains be removed from a garage floor?

Battery acid etches concrete by dissolving the calcium compounds in the surface. We can remove the discoloration and significantly improve the appearance, but if the acid has physically pitted the concrete surface, those texture changes are permanent. We assess the damage during the estimate and provide honest expectations.


Q: Do you remove stains from pavers and natural stone?

Yes, with specific chemistry matched to the material. Concrete pavers tolerate a wide range of treatments. Travertine and natural stone require gentler, pH-specific chemistry to avoid etching. We always test a small area first on any natural stone surface.


Q: How much does stain removal cost?

Stain removal is priced per stain or per area rather than per square foot, because a single rust stain on an otherwise clean driveway requires the same setup and chemistry as a stain on a dirty one. We provide specific pricing after assessing the stain type, size, and surface material during the free estimate.
