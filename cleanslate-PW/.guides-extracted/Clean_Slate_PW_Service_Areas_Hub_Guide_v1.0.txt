Section 1: How to Use This Guide

This guide covers the Service Areas Hub page (/service-areas/). It is the top-level gateway to all geographic content — the county page and 10 individual city landing pages. The hub uses a map-centered layout with a complete city directory to help visitors confirm coverage and self-select their specific location.

The hub carries broad geographic keywords (&apos;pressure washing Tampa Bay&apos;, &apos;exterior cleaning Hillsborough County&apos;) while city pages carry specific geo-modified terms (&apos;pressure washing Brandon FL&apos;). Content on the hub must be distinct from both the homepage service areas section (compact routing block) and the individual city pages (deep local content).
