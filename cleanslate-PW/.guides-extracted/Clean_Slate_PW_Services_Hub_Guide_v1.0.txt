Section 1: How to Use This Guide

This guide provides everything a developer needs to build the Clean Slate PW Services Hub page (/services/) from scratch. The Services Hub is the top-level gateway to all 10 individual service pages. It serves as the central routing page for visitors who arrive with general pressure washing intent and need to self-select the specific service matching their needs.

This page sits one level below the homepage in the site hierarchy and one level above all individual service child pages. It receives internal link equity from the homepage services grid, the header navigation, and the footer. It distributes that equity downward to every child service page through its card grid and inline content links.


1.1 Page Role in the Services Silo

The Services Hub is the parent page of the Services silo. All 10 child service pages are direct children. The hub carries broader, category-level keywords (&apos;pressure washing services Tampa Bay&apos;) while child pages carry specific service + location keywords (&apos;roof cleaning Brandon FL&apos;). Content on this hub must be distinct from both the homepage services grid (which is a compact routing element) and the individual child pages (which go deep on a single service).

Content differentiation rule: The homepage introduces services in ~15 words per card. This hub expands each service to ~40-60 words in an alternating layout with images. Child pages then provide 1,500-2,500 words of dedicated depth per service. No content is duplicated across these three levels.

Section 13: FAQ Section

Five questions targeting service-selection queries. These differ from the homepage FAQ (which targets cost, safety, and geographic questions). The services hub FAQ focuses on helping visitors choose the right service and understand the process.


13.1 FAQ Inventory — Exact Q&amp;A Copy


Q1: How do I know which cleaning service I need?

The simplest way is to tell us what surface you want cleaned and we will recommend the right service and method. Generally: roofs, siding, stucco, and screens require soft washing (low pressure + cleaning agents). Driveways, sidewalks, pool decks, and pavers require pressure washing (high pressure surface cleaning). If you are unsure, request a free estimate and we will assess your property and recommend the correct service.


Q2: Can you clean multiple surfaces in one visit?

Yes. Most homeowners book two or three services in a single appointment — the most common combination is house soft wash plus driveway cleaning. Bundling services in one visit reduces setup time and we offer a discount when you book two or more services together. Our team brings all equipment needed for both soft washing and pressure washing on every truck.


Q3: How long does a typical residential cleaning take?

A standard house soft wash takes 2–3 hours for a single-story home and 3–4 hours for a two-story. Driveway cleaning typically takes 1–2 hours depending on size and staining. Roof soft washing takes 2–4 hours depending on roof size and algae severity. Most bundled appointments (house + driveway) are completed in a single morning or afternoon.


Q4: Do you offer recurring maintenance contracts?

Yes. We offer annual, semi-annual, and quarterly maintenance schedules for both residential and commercial properties. Recurring customers receive priority scheduling, a locked-in rate for 12 months, and automatic reminders before each scheduled service. Contact us to set up a maintenance plan that matches your property&apos;s needs and Florida&apos;s cleaning cycle.


Q5: What areas do you serve?

Clean Slate PW serves Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, Lithia, FishHawk, Apollo Beach, Plant City, Seffner, Bloomingdale, Tampa, and surrounding Hillsborough County communities. For larger commercial projects, we travel throughout the Tampa Bay region. Call (813) 555-0283 to confirm service availability for your specific location.
