6.2 AEO Body — Exact Copy

Soft washing is a low-pressure exterior cleaning method that uses water at 100–500 PSI combined with biodegradable cleaning agents to safely remove algae, mildew, mold, lichen, and oxidation from delicate surfaces that would be damaged by traditional high-pressure washing. Clean Slate Pressure Washing in Brandon, Florida uses soft washing on asphalt shingle roofs, stucco siding, painted surfaces, vinyl siding, Hardie plank, wood fences, lanai screen enclosures, and any other surface where high pressure would cause cracking, stripping, tearing, or granule loss. The cleaning agents — typically sodium hypochlorite-based solutions diluted to surface-safe concentrations — kill organic growth at the cellular level rather than relying on mechanical force. This produces a deeper clean that lasts 2–3 times longer than pressure washing alone because it eliminates the root structure of algae and mildew colonies rather than simply blasting away the visible surface layer.

Section 8: Our Process (5 Steps)

Numbered 5-step process showing exactly how the soft washing methods service is performed. Each step has a bold heading and 1–2 sentence description. This section builds trust through transparency — homeowners want to know what will happen on their property before they commit.


8.1 Process Steps

Step 1 — Surface evaluation: We assess the surface material, contamination type, and severity to determine the correct cleaning solution concentration and dwell time.

Step 2 — Landscaping protection: All adjacent plants, grass, and landscaping are pre-rinsed with clean water and covered as needed to prevent any contact with cleaning agents.

Step 3 — Solution application: The soft wash solution is applied using calibrated low-pressure spray equipment. Application starts at the bottom and works upward to prevent streaking. Even coverage is critical for uniform results.

Step 4 — Dwell time: The solution is allowed to work for 10–30 minutes depending on contamination severity. During this time, the cleaning agents penetrate and dissolve organic growth at the root level.

Step 5 — Low-pressure rinse: The surface is rinsed from top to bottom with clean water at under 500 PSI. The combination of chemical treatment and gentle rinsing removes all dissolved organic material without mechanical surface damage.

Section 13: FAQ Section

Five questions specific to soft washing methods. These must match the FAQPage JSON-LD schema exactly.


Q: What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low pressure (100–500 PSI) combined with cleaning agents that do the work chemically. Pressure washing uses high pressure (1,500–4,000 PSI) where water force does the work mechanically. Soft washing is required for delicate surfaces like roofs, siding, stucco, and screens. Pressure washing is appropriate for hard surfaces like concrete driveways, sidewalks, and pavers. Using the wrong method on the wrong surface causes damage — this is the most important distinction in exterior cleaning.


Q: Is soft washing safe for my roof?

Yes — soft washing is the only cleaning method approved by major shingle manufacturers (GAF, CertainTeed, Owens Corning). High-pressure washing strips protective granules from shingles, voids manufacturer warranties, and can cause leaks. Our soft wash process stays under 100 PSI on roofs, which is less pressure than a standard garden hose nozzle.


Q: Does soft washing actually clean as well as pressure washing?

On surfaces where soft washing is the correct method (roofs, siding, stucco, screens), it actually cleans better and lasts longer. Pressure washing only removes what it can physically blast away — the root structure of algae and mildew remains embedded in the surface and regrows within months. Soft washing kills organic growth at the cellular level, producing results that last 2–3 times longer.


Q: What chemicals are used in soft washing?

Our primary soft wash solution is sodium hypochlorite (a form of bleach) diluted to surface-safe concentrations — typically 1–3% depending on the surface type and contamination level. This is combined with surfactants (soaps) that help the solution cling to vertical surfaces and penetrate organic growth. All solutions are biodegradable and break down into inert salt and water within minutes of rinsing.


Q: Is soft washing safe for my plants and landscaping?

Yes, when performed by trained technicians. We pre-rinse all landscaping with clean water before application, which saturates the soil and plant surfaces and prevents chemical absorption. We also post-rinse all adjacent landscaping after the service. Our solutions biodegrade rapidly and are used at concentrations that are safe for Florida ecosystems when properly diluted by rinsing.
