Section 1: How to Use This Guide

This guide provides everything a developer needs to build the Clean Slate PW homepage from scratch. It is the primary entry point to the entire site and the page on which the majority of conversion traffic will land — whether from Google organic search, the Google Business Profile, direct traffic, or paid ads.

Every section follows the standardized format: Section Overview, Layout Specifications (desktop/tablet/mobile), Content Specifications (exact copy), Design Specifications (CSS properties), Image Specifications, and Developer Notes. The developer should be able to implement the entire page by following this guide top to bottom without outside clarification.


1.1 Guide Relationship to Strategy Document

This homepage guide operationalizes the Clean Slate PW Website Strategy document. The strategy establishes brand positioning, site architecture, and keyword assignments. This guide translates those decisions into production-ready implementation for a single page. The strategy document is the authoritative source for any referenced color values, URL structures, or keyword targets.


1.2 Content Authoring Rules


All copy is final: Implement exactly as written. Word counts, phrasing, and section order are optimized for keyword targeting and conversion.


No placeholder content: Every headline, paragraph, and CTA label is production-ready.


Image alt text is authoritative: Use the exact alt text specified. It is optimized for accessibility and keyword relevance.


Schema JSON-LD is complete: Do not modify schema payloads except to swap in live phone, email, and rating data.

BreadcrumbList Schema

Single-item breadcrumb pointing to the homepage itself. Deploy as third JSON-LD block for sitewide consistency.

Q1: How much does pressure washing cost in Brandon, FL?

Pressure washing costs in Brandon typically range from $189 for a basic driveway to $549 for a full house soft wash, depending on home size, surfaces, and condition. Clean Slate PW provides free, flat-rate written estimates within 24 hours. We never use hourly billing or surprise charges.


Q2: What is the difference between soft washing and pressure washing?

Soft washing uses low-pressure water combined with biodegradable cleaning agents to safely clean delicate surfaces like roofs, stucco, and lanai screens. Pressure washing uses high-pressure water (2,500–4,000 PSI) for durable surfaces like concrete driveways and pavers. Choosing the wrong method causes most DIY damage.


Q3: Will pressure washing damage my roof or siding?

Not when the right method is used. Clean Slate PW uses soft washing — never high pressure — on roofs, stucco, and screens. Soft washing removes algae and stains without lifting shingles or stripping paint. We carry $2M general liability in liability insurance and provide a written satisfaction guarantee.


Q4: How often should I have my house cleaned in Florida?

Florida&apos;s humidity accelerates algae and mildew growth. We recommend annual house and roof soft washing for most Tampa Bay homes, with driveway and pool deck cleaning every 12–18 months. Homes with heavy tree coverage or shaded walls may benefit from cleaning every 8–10 months.


Q5: Are your cleaning solutions safe for plants and pets?

Yes. Clean Slate PW uses biodegradable, plant-safe cleaning agents on every project. We rinse and protect landscaping before and after each service. After approximately 30 minutes of drying time, treated areas are safe for children, pets, and wildlife.


Q6: Do you serve communities outside of Brandon?

Yes. Clean Slate PW serves Brandon and the surrounding Tampa Bay region including Riverview, Valrico, Lithia, FishHawk, Bloomingdale, Apollo Beach, Plant City, Seffner, and Tampa. We frequently travel for larger projects throughout Hillsborough County. Call (813) 555-0283 to confirm availability for your neighborhood.
