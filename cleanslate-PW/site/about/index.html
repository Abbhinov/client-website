About Clean Slate Pressure Washing

Founded in 2021 by Jason Caldwell. Licensed, insured, and committed to doing exterior cleaning the right way — every surface, every time.

Our Story

How Clean Slate PW Got Started

Jason Caldwell started Clean Slate Pressure Washing in 2021 after watching a well-meaning pressure washing contractor blast the paint off his neighbor's stucco home in their Brandon neighborhood. The contractor had used a 4,000 PSI surface wand on painted stucco — a surface that should never see anything above 500 PSI. The resulting damage cost the homeowner over $8,000 in repainting and stucco repair.

That experience became the founding principle of Clean Slate PW: the right method matters more than the equipment. Jason Caldwell spent six months studying soft wash chemistry, surface compatibility, manufacturer warranty requirements, and Florida-specific algae biology before cleaning a single customer's home. He earned UAMCC certification, secured $2M general liability in general liability insurance, and built a service protocol that matches the cleaning approach to the surface — not the other way around.

Today, Clean Slate PW serves over 40 Tampa Bay homeowners per month across Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, Lithia, FishHawk, and surrounding communities. The company has grown entirely through referrals and repeat customers — a track record that reflects Jason Caldwell's belief that the best marketing is work that speaks for itself.

The story continues to be the same: know the surface, choose the right method, use the right chemistry, and stand behind every result with a written guarantee.

What We Believe

Four Values — Each With Proof, Not Just Words

Method Over Muscle

We choose the cleaning method that protects your surface, not the fastest or cheapest approach.

We turn down jobs where the customer insists on high-pressure cleaning on a surface that requires soft washing. Protecting your home matters more than closing the sale.

Radical Transparency

Flat-rate quotes, itemized by service, delivered in writing within 24 hours. No surprises, no hidden fees.

Every estimate includes a line-by-line breakdown of what we will clean, how we will clean it, and exactly what it costs. If the scope changes on-site, we get approval before proceeding.

Stand Behind Every Job

100% satisfaction guarantee — written, not verbal. If you are not happy, we return at no charge.

In 2021 through today, we have had a 0% dispute rate on completed projects. When a rare issue arises, we address it within 48 hours.

Florida-First Expertise

We specialize in Florida's climate, Florida's algae, Florida's building materials, and Florida's HOA landscape.

Our technicians can identify Gloeocapsa magma, lichen, and oxidation on sight — and they know which chemistry treats each one without damaging the underlying surface.
