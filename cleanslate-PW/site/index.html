Professional Exterior Cleaning for Brandon, FL & the Tampa Bay Region

Clean Slate Pressure Washing is a licensed and insured exterior cleaning company serving Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, Lithia, Tampa, and surrounding Hillsborough County communities. Founded in 2021 by Jason Caldwell, the company specializes in soft washing for delicate surfaces including asphalt shingle roofs, stucco siding, and lanai screens, alongside high-pressure surface cleaning for driveways, sidewalks, and pool decks. All services use commercial-grade equipment and biodegradable cleaning agents that are safe for landscaping, pets, and Florida's freshwater ecosystems. Clean Slate Pressure Washing carries a Florida business license, $2M general liability in general liability insurance, and is rated 4.9 stars on Google with over 150 verified customer reviews. The company offers free estimates within 24 hours, transparent flat-rate pricing, and a 100% satisfaction guarantee on every residential and commercial project completed throughout the Tampa Bay region.

Why Homeowners Choose Us

Six Reasons Tampa Bay Trusts Us With Their Home

Right Method, Right Pressure

We don't blast everything with high pressure. Roofs and screens get gentle soft washing. Driveways get surface cleaning. Choosing the correct method protects your surfaces and prevents the costly damage that DIY and uncertified contractors often cause.

Florida-Specific Expertise

Florida's humidity grows algae, mildew, and Gloeocapsa magma faster than almost anywhere. We know which agents work on Florida stains, which surfaces tolerate which chemistries, and how to clean without harming the landscaping you've maintained.

HOA-Compliant Cleaning

Most Tampa Bay neighborhoods sit inside HOA boundaries with specific maintenance rules. We know the common notice formats, document our work for compliance letters, and schedule within HOA-approved hours.

Transparent Flat-Rate Pricing

No hourly meter running. No surprise charges. Our estimates are flat-rate and itemized by service, so you know exactly what you're paying before we turn on the equipment.

24-Hour Estimate Response

Submit a request and get a written estimate within 24 hours, often same-day. Our team responds 7 days a week including weekends.

100% Satisfaction Guarantee

If you're not satisfied with any service, we return and address it at no cost. Written guarantee — no fine print, no exceptions.
