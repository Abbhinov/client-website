Professional Pressure Washing & Soft Washing in Tampa, FL

Clean Slate Pressure Washing provides professional pressure washing and soft washing services for homes and businesses in Tampa, Florida and the surrounding Hillsborough County communities. Our Tampa services include house soft washing for siding and stucco, roof soft washing for shingle and tile roofs, high-pressure driveway and sidewalk cleaning, pool deck and paver restoration, lanai and screen enclosure cleaning, fence washing, gutter cleaning and brightening, and commercial exterior maintenance. Every project in Tampa is performed by licensed, insured technicians using commercial-grade equipment and biodegradable cleaning agents safe for Florida landscapes and ecosystems. Clean Slate PW offers free estimates within 24 hours, transparent flat-rate pricing with no hidden fees, and a 100% satisfaction guarantee on every Tampa project.

What Makes Tampa Unique for Exterior Cleaning

1

Clean Slate PW serves select Tampa neighborhoods where our Brandon-based team can provide the same responsive scheduling and service quality our Hillsborough County clients expect. Our primary Tampa coverage includes East Tampa (including the USF area and Temple Terrace corridor), South Tampa (including Palma Ceia, Bayshore, and Hyde Park), New Tampa, and Westchase.

2

Tampa's architectural diversity — from 1920s bungalows in Seminole Heights to 2020s modern construction in Westshore Marina District — requires a wider range of cleaning approaches than any single suburban community. We assess each Tampa property individually to determine the correct combination of soft washing and pressure washing for its specific materials and era.

3

South Tampa's proximity to Hillsborough Bay means waterfront and bayshore properties face the same salt-air accelerated oxidation challenges as Apollo Beach. Our Tampa waterfront protocol includes salt-neutralizing pre-rinse steps not needed for inland properties.

4

Tampa's commercial density creates strong demand for our storefront, parking lot, and restaurant patio cleaning services. We service commercial properties throughout the Tampa metro area regardless of residential service boundaries — our commercial coverage extends anywhere within a 45-minute drive of Brandon.
