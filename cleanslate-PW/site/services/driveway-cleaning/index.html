Driveway & Sidewalk Pressure Washing

Clean Slate Pressure Washing provides professional driveway and sidewalk pressure washing for residential and commercial properties throughout Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, and the Tampa Bay region. Unlike delicate surfaces that require soft washing, concrete driveways and sidewalks are cleaned using high-pressure surface cleaning equipment operating at 2,500–4,000 PSI with a rotating bar that delivers an even, streak-free clean across the entire surface. This method removes oil stains, tire marks, rust discoloration, embedded mildew, and years of ground-in dirt in a single pass. Florida's porous concrete absorbs moisture that promotes rapid mildew colonization, particularly during the humid summer months. Our pre-treatment process applies a detergent solution before surface cleaning to break down organic growth at the root, producing results that last significantly longer than pressure alone.

That First Curb-Side Look After a Rain

The driveway looks dirty in dry weather and worse after a rain — gray-green algae bloom on the cool shaded edges, a milky film along the garage apron, fertilizer rust along the irrigation line, and a half-circle of brown right where the car parks. Florida concrete is porous: every summer humidity, every irrigation cycle, and every dropped acorn feeds the mildew and stains that pressure alone cannot rinse away. Handheld wand washing — even with high PSI — leaves "zebra stripes" because the operator's pass speed varies every few inches, and oil stains the wand cannot lift remain darker than the surrounding clean concrete.

A commercial surface cleaner solves both problems at once. The rotating bar inside the splash housing distributes the pressure evenly across a 16- to 20-inch swath, eliminating stripes and finishing in a fraction of the time. Combined with a degreasing pre-treatment that loosens oil and lifts mildew at the root, your driveway returns to a uniform, near-new finish — and stays that way longer because the source organisms have been killed, not just blasted away.

How We Do It

Our 5-Step Driveway & Sidewalk Cleaning Process

  1. Pre-treatment

    A degreasing detergent solution is applied to the entire driveway surface and allowed to dwell for 10–15 minutes to loosen oil, organic stains, and embedded grime.

  2. Surface cleaning

    Our commercial-grade surface cleaner — a rotating bar with dual spray tips inside a splash-free housing — passes over the concrete at 2,500–4,000 PSI, delivering perfectly even cleaning without zebra-stripe patterns.

  3. Edge detailing

    All edges along landscaping beds, garage aprons, curbs, and expansion joints are cleaned with a handheld wand using a fan tip for precision work.

  4. Targeted stain treatment

    Any remaining oil spots, rust stains, or battery acid marks receive a second application of specialized chemistry matched to the stain type.

  5. Post-treatment rinse

    The entire surface and adjacent areas receive a final rinse to remove all cleaning residue and restore a clean runoff path.
