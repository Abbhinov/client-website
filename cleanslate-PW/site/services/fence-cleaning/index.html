Fence Cleaning & Restoration

Clean Slate Pressure Washing provides professional fence cleaning and restoration services for homeowners throughout Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, and the Tampa Bay region. Florida fences face aggressive mildew colonization, algae growth, and UV degradation that transforms white vinyl to dingy gray, natural wood to weathered silver, and painted aluminum to chalky dull. We match the cleaning method to the fence material: soft washing with mildew-specific agents for vinyl and painted aluminum fences, low-pressure wood brightening with oxalic acid-based solutions for cedar and pine, and targeted stain treatment for rust marks from irrigation overspray. A clean fence dramatically improves a property's curb appeal and is one of the most common surfaces cited in HOA exterior maintenance violation notices in Hillsborough County communities.

The Fence That Stopped Looking White

White vinyl fencing in Florida has a problem: it does not stay white. Within two summers, a film of green-and-black mildew bonds to the textured surface — heaviest on the shaded north side, where humidity sits longest. Wood fences gray out, splinter, and grow lichen along the rails. Painted aluminum chalks and the finish dulls. Then the HOA letter arrives, with a 30-day notice and a specific reference to "visible mildew on perimeter fencing" or "discoloration not consistent with community standards."

You can rent a pressure washer, point it at the fence, and chase the green stripes down the rails. It works for a week or two — and on wood, it shreds the grain. The correct fix is matched chemistry plus matched pressure: soft wash solution on vinyl and aluminum at under 500 PSI, oxalic acid-based brightening on cedar and pine at under 1,000 PSI, and targeted stain treatment where irrigation rust has set in. A properly cleaned fence stays cleaner longer because the mildew is killed at the cellular level, not just blown off the surface.

How We Do It

Our 5-Step Fence Cleaning Process

  1. Material identification

    We determine the fence type (vinyl, wood species, painted aluminum, ornamental iron, composite) and select the appropriate chemistry and pressure level.

  2. Solution application

    Cleaning solution matched to the material is applied to all fence surfaces. Vinyl and aluminum receive mildew-dissolving soft wash solution. Wood receives a wood-brightening agent that lifts gray weathering and restores natural tannins.

  3. Dwell time

    Solution is allowed to work for 10–20 minutes depending on contamination severity. Heavy mildew areas may receive a second application.

  4. Cleaning

    Vinyl and aluminum fences are rinsed at soft wash pressure (under 500 PSI). Wood fences receive low-pressure rinsing (under 1,000 PSI) to prevent fiber damage and grain raising.

  5. Post-treatment

    Wood fences can receive an optional wood preservative or stain application after cleaning and drying (quoted separately). Vinyl fences dry to their restored original color within hours.
