Gutter Cleaning & Exterior Brightening

Clean Slate Pressure Washing provides professional gutter cleaning and exterior gutter brightening services for homeowners throughout Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, and the Tampa Bay area. Gutter service includes two distinct components: interior cleanout (removing leaves, debris, pine needles, and standing water from inside the gutter channel and downspouts) and exterior brightening (removing the dark vertical streaks — called tiger stripes — that form on the outer face of aluminum gutters). Tiger stripes are caused by electrostatic bonding between airborne pollutants, oxidation residue, and the aluminum gutter surface during water runoff. These streaks cannot be removed with regular pressure washing alone — they require a specialized oxidation-dissolving cleaning agent applied by hand and rinsed at low pressure. Both interior and exterior gutter services are performed in a single visit.

The Streaks That Won't Rinse Off

The gutters were white when they were installed. Then a few summers passed. From the curb, the dark vertical streaks running down the outer face are the first thing you notice — particularly under each downspout joint, where the wind has blown rain water in concentrated columns over the lip and across the aluminum. Pressure washing the outside makes a small visible difference, then the streaks reappear after the next rain. Meanwhile, inside the gutter channel, leaves and pine needles have built up into a wet mat that traps standing water, breeds mosquitoes, and starts to rot the fascia behind the gutter.

Two problems, two different fixes. Interior cleanout is mechanical — leaves and debris come out by hand, downspouts get flushed, the system gets a flow test. Exterior tiger stripes are chemical — they're an electrostatic bond between aluminum oxidation and airborne pollutants, and they require a specialized brightening agent applied by hand and gently rinsed. Done together in one visit, your gutters drain like new and look like new — and the fascia behind them stops rotting before the damage gets expensive.

How We Do It

Our 5-Step Gutter Cleaning & Brightening Process

  1. Interior cleanout

    All leaves, twigs, pine needles, shingle granules, and standing water are removed from the gutter channel by hand. Debris is bagged and removed from the property.

  2. Downspout flush

    Each downspout is flushed with a garden hose to confirm unobstructed flow. Clogged downspouts are cleared using a plumber's snake or pressurized water jet.

  3. Flow test

    Water is run through the entire gutter system to verify proper drainage slope and identify any sagging sections, loose brackets, or leaking seams.

  4. Exterior brightening

    A specialized gutter-brightening solution is applied to the outer face of the gutters using a soft scrub pad. The solution dissolves the electrostatic bond holding tiger stripe deposits to the aluminum.

  5. Rinse and inspection

    Gutters are rinsed clean and inspected for any remaining streaks. Problem areas receive a second application. Fascia boards behind gutters are inspected for signs of water damage or rot.
