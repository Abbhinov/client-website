Complete Exterior Cleaning Services for Homes & Businesses

Clean Slate Pressure Washing provides a full range of residential and commercial exterior cleaning services throughout Brandon, Riverview, Valrico, Lithia, and the greater Tampa Bay region. Our service catalog includes house soft washing for painted siding and stucco, roof soft washing for asphalt shingle and tile roofs, high-pressure surface cleaning for concrete driveways and sidewalks, paver cleaning and sealing for pool decks and patios, lanai and screen enclosure cleaning, fence restoration, gutter clean-out and exterior brightening, commercial storefront and parking area cleaning, and specialized rust and stain removal. Every service uses the appropriate cleaning method for the surface type — soft washing at 100–500 PSI for delicate materials, pressure washing at 1,500–4,000 PSI for durable concrete and stone. All work is performed by licensed, insured technicians using commercial-grade equipment and biodegradable cleaning agents.

Residential Services

Exterior Cleaning for Every Surface of Your Home

Each service below uses the cleaning method specifically matched to the surface type — because in Florida's climate, the wrong approach does more harm than good.

House Soft Washing

Florida's humidity coats vinyl siding, stucco, and painted surfaces with a persistent layer of mildew, algae, and grime. Our low-pressure soft wash system uses biodegradable cleaning agents at under 500 PSI to safely dissolve organic growth and restore your home's original finish — without stripping paint, cracking stucco, or voiding manufacturer warranties.

Learn more about House Soft Washing →

Roof Soft Washing

Black streaks on your roof are Gloeocapsa magma — a cyanobacteria that feeds on the limestone filler in asphalt shingles. Our roof soft wash kills the algae at its root using industry-approved chemistry at under 100 PSI. No high-pressure wands ever touch your shingles, protecting both the roof and your manufacturer's warranty.

Learn more about Roof Soft Washing →

Driveway & Sidewalk Cleaning

Concrete driveways and sidewalks can handle what delicate surfaces cannot: high-pressure surface cleaning at 2,500–4,000 PSI. Our commercial surface cleaners eliminate oil stains, tire marks, rust, mildew, and years of embedded dirt in a single pass, leaving a uniform, streak-free finish across the entire surface.

Learn more about Driveway & Sidewalk Cleaning →

Pool Deck & Paver Cleaning

Tampa Bay pool decks take a beating from chlorine splash, Florida rain, and constant foot traffic. We clean travertine, brick pavers, stamped concrete, and cool-deck coatings using the appropriate pressure for each material. Paver re-sanding and sealing services available to lock in the clean and prevent weed growth.

Learn more about Pool Deck & Paver Cleaning →

Lanai & Screen Cleaning

Pool cages and lanai screens accumulate a film of pollen, mildew, and oxidation that blocks light and traps humidity inside the enclosure. Our soft wash process cleans screens, frames, and the ceiling without tearing a single screen panel — guaranteed. We also clean the concrete or paver floor inside the cage.

Learn more about Lanai & Screen Cleaning →

Fence Cleaning & Restoration

Vinyl fences yellow and stain. Wood fences gray and mildew. Aluminum fences oxidize and pit. We match the cleaning method to the material: soft wash for vinyl and painted aluminum, low-pressure wood brightening for cedar and pine, and targeted stain removal for rust and irrigation marks on any fence type.

Learn more about Fence Cleaning & Restoration →

Gutter Cleaning & Brightening

Interior gutter clean-out removes leaves, debris, and standing water that breed mosquitoes and cause fascia rot. Exterior gutter brightening removes the dark 'tiger stripes' — oxidation streaks caused by water runoff interacting with the aluminum finish. Both services performed in a single visit.

Learn more about Gutter Cleaning & Brightening →

Two Methods. One Clean Guarantee.

Soft Washing

100–500 PSI
How It Works
Low-pressure water + biodegradable cleaning agents dissolve algae, mildew, and stains at the root.
Best For
Roofs, stucco, vinyl siding, painted surfaces, lanai screens, wood fences.
Why It Matters
Protects shingles, paint, and delicate finishes. Required by most shingle manufacturers to maintain warranty.
Risk If Wrong Method Used
Using high pressure on roofs strips granules, voids warranties, and causes leaks.

Pressure Washing

1,500–4,000 PSI
How It Works
High-pressure water physically blasts embedded dirt, oil, and grime from hard surfaces.
Best For
Concrete driveways, sidewalks, pavers, pool decks, parking lots, brick.
Why It Matters
Delivers deep cleaning on surfaces that can withstand force. Removes stains soft washing alone cannot.
Risk If Wrong Method Used
Using soft wash on concrete leaves embedded stains; insufficient cleaning power.
