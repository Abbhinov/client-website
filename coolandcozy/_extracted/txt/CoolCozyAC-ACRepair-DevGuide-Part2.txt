Factors That Affect Your AC Repair Cost
Every repair is different. Here are the main factors that move your cost within these ranges:
Type of failure — A failed capacitor ($15 part, 30-minute install) costs far less than a failed compressor ($400–$800 part, 2–3 hour job).
System age and type — Older systems (10+ years) may require discontinued parts that cost more to source. R-22 (Freon) systems require expensive alternative refrigerants since R-22 was phased out.
Accessibility — Attic-mounted air handlers in Jacksonville homes are harder to access than closet or garage installations, adding labor time.
Time of service — Emergency or after-hours service may carry a premium, though Cool & Cozy works to keep emergency pricing fair.
Repair vs. replace decision — If the repair cost exceeds 50% of a new system’s price and the system is 12+ years old, replacement often makes more financial sense. We’ll be honest about this — we don’t profit from repairing a system that should be replaced.

FAQ 1
Q: How long does an AC repair typically take?
A: Most common AC repairs — capacitor replacement, contactor replacement, refrigerant recharge, or condensate drain clearing — are completed in 1 to 2 hours. More complex repairs like compressor replacement, evaporator coil replacement, or extensive electrical work may take 3 to 5 hours. Cool & Cozy aims to complete every repair in a single visit. Our service trucks carry the most commonly needed parts so we do not have to schedule a return trip for standard components.
Word Count: 74 words

FAQ 2
Q: Should I repair or replace my AC system?
A: As a general guideline, if the repair cost exceeds 50% of what a new system would cost and your current system is 12 or more years old, replacement often makes better financial sense in the long run. Other factors include refrigerant type (R-22 systems are increasingly expensive to maintain), energy efficiency of the existing unit versus modern systems, and how frequently you have needed repairs in the past 2 years. Cool & Cozy evaluates all of these factors and provides an honest recommendation. We will never push a replacement when a repair will solve the problem. For a detailed comparison, see our guide: When to Repair vs. Replace Your AC.
Word Count: 110 words
Internal Link: “When to Repair vs. Replace Your AC” → /resources/when-to-repair-vs-replace-ac

FAQ 3
Q: Do you charge a diagnostic or service call fee?
A: Cool & Cozy provides free diagnostic estimates. Our technician inspects your system, identifies the problem, and provides a written quote at no charge. If you approve the repair, the diagnostic is included in the repair price. If you choose not to proceed, you owe nothing. There are no trip charges, no service call fees, and no pressure.
Word Count: 55 words

FAQ 4
Q: Can you repair my AC the same day I call?
A: In most cases, yes. Cool & Cozy offers same-day AC repair service throughout Jacksonville and Duval County, especially during emergency situations in summer. Our service vehicles are stocked with the most commonly needed parts — capacitors, contactors, fan motors, and refrigerant — so most repairs can be completed in a single visit without ordering parts. For less common components, we order the part and return to complete the repair as quickly as possible, typically within 1 to 2 business days.
Word Count: 83 words

FAQ 5
Q: What brands of AC do you repair?
A: Cool & Cozy repairs all major residential and commercial AC brands including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Daikin, York, Bryant, Amana, Mitsubishi, Fujitsu, LG, and more. We are not locked into any single manufacturer. Whether your system was installed by us, by another contractor, or came with your home, we can diagnose and repair it.
Word Count: 55 words

FAQ 6
Q: What should I do if my AC stops working in the middle of the night?
A: First, check your thermostat settings and batteries, and check your circuit breaker panel for a tripped breaker — these are the two most common causes of sudden AC failure that you can resolve without a technician. If neither fixes the problem, turn the system off to prevent further damage (especially if you hear unusual noises or see ice forming), open windows if outdoor temperature allows, and call Cool & Cozy at (904) 555-0199 first thing in the morning. We prioritize emergency calls and will get to you as early as possible. If indoor temperatures become unsafe for elderly residents, infants, or pets, consider relocating temporarily and let us know the urgency when you call.
Word Count: 120 words
