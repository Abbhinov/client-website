How Cool & Cozy AC Repair Got Started
Story Copy
Cool & Cozy AC Repair was incorporated in Jacksonville, Florida in October 2018 — not as a franchise, not backed by investors, but as a family’s bet on a simple idea: that Jacksonville deserved an AC contractor who shows up on time, charges what they quote, and treats every home like their own.
The company started with one truck, one technician, and a commitment to doing things right the first time. That commitment hasn’t changed. What has changed is the track record behind it. Over the years, Cool & Cozy has completed 21 documented building permits with Duval County, earned a BuildZoom score of 100 (placing it in the top 13% of all 191,428 licensed contractors in Florida), and built a reputation on Thumbtack for work quality, professionalism, and pricing that customers describe as “exceptional.”
Today, Cool & Cozy serves homeowners across Jacksonville’s neighborhoods — from Mandarin to the Beaches, from Riverside to Arlington — alongside property managers who trust the company with their entire rental portfolios. The team is bilingual in English and Spanish, reflecting the diverse community Jacksonville is becoming. The pricing is still honest, the service is still personal, and the trucks still show up on time.

H2
What We Stand For

Value
What It Means in Practice
Honest Pricing
You get a written quote before work begins. The quote is the price. No change orders, no “while we’re here” surprises, no upselling parts or services you don’t need. If a repair is cheaper than we estimated, you pay the lower amount.
Show Up On Time
We give you a specific arrival window and call 30 minutes before we arrive. We don’t ask you to block off a half-day. If we’re running late (it happens), we call to let you know — you’re never left wondering.
Fix It Right
We diagnose the root cause, not just the symptom. We use the correct parts at the correct specifications. We test the system thoroughly before we leave. And if something we repaired fails within our warranty period, we come back and make it right at no additional cost.
Respect Your Home
We wear shoe covers indoors, lay drop cloths around work areas, and clean up completely when we’re done. Your home should look the same after we leave — except the AC works.
Speak Your Language
Our bilingual team serves English and Spanish-speaking customers with equal care and clarity. When you’re stressed about a broken AC, communicating in the language you’re most comfortable with makes the experience better.
Earn the Relationship
We don’t expect loyalty — we earn it. Through consistent quality, fair pricing, and service that respects your time and your budget. Every job is an audition for the next one.

Each value is stated as a BEHAVIOR, not a platitude. “Honesty” is a platitude. “You get a written quote before work begins and the quote is the price” is a behavior. The specificity makes the values believable.
