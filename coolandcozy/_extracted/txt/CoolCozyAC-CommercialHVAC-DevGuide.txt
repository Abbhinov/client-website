1. How to Use This Guide
This guide defines the Commercial HVAC service child page at /services/commercial-hvac. This is the property manager flagship page — the destination for every PM-specific CTA across the site (“Discuss Your Properties,” “Get a Multi-Unit Quote,” “Build Your Portfolio Plan”). While it serves all commercial HVAC needs, its primary audience is property managers and landlords managing multi-unit residential portfolios in Duval County.
This page has a different tone than residential service pages. Property managers are professional buyers who evaluate contractors based on reliability, communication quality, pricing transparency, and operational efficiency — not on emergency urgency or emotional comfort messaging. The content is structured, metrics-forward, and speaks to business outcomes: reduced tenant complaints, predictable maintenance costs, extended equipment life, and professional communication.
Cool & Cozy’s specific expertise with property managers is the single biggest differentiator documented in this guide. No Tier 1 Jacksonville HVAC competitor has a dedicated commercial/PM page with this depth. This page establishes Cool & Cozy as the obvious choice for property managers seeking a reliable HVAC partner.
1.1 Content Rules
All content fresh and original. No duplication from PM callout sections on homepage, repair, installation, or maintenance pages.
Tone is professional, direct, and outcome-focused. No casual language, no emergency urgency. This audience makes deliberate decisions.
Proof points are specific and verifiable: license number, BuildZoom ranking, permit count, founding year.
Property manager pain points addressed directly: tenant communication, access coordination, response time, invoicing, portfolio reporting.
Commercial system expertise demonstrated through specific equipment types, not just “we do commercial too.”
Bilingual capability framed as a business advantage for tenant communication, not just a cultural nicety.

FAQ 1
Q: Do you serve both residential and commercial HVAC?
A: Yes. Cool & Cozy’s Florida contractor license CAC1819948 covers both residential and commercial HVAC work. We serve single-family homeowners, multi-unit property managers, landlords, Airbnb hosts, restaurants, retail spaces, offices, and medical practices throughout Jacksonville and Duval County. Many property managers appreciate having one contractor who handles both their personal home and their investment properties.

FAQ 2
Q: How do your property management maintenance agreements work?
A: We build a custom maintenance plan around your portfolio. Each unit receives two preventive maintenance visits per year (spring pre-cooling and fall pre-heating), scheduled in coordinated blocks to minimize per-visit costs. Agreement members receive priority scheduling for repair calls, discounted diagnostics, bilingual tenant communication, and post-cycle system condition reports that score each unit green, yellow, or red. Per-unit pricing with volume discounts for portfolios of 10 or more units. Call (904) 555-0199 to discuss your specific portfolio.

FAQ 3
Q: How do you handle tenant access and communication?
A: We document your preferred access procedure for each property: lockbox codes, tenant-scheduled appointments, management-escorted visits, or gate/building entry codes. Our technicians arrive prepared and communicate directly with tenants in English or Spanish. We schedule around tenant preferences when possible and always clean up after completing work. Your office does not need to be involved in the day-of coordination.

FAQ 4
Q: What happens if a tenant has an AC emergency after hours?
A: Portfolio maintenance agreement members receive priority emergency dispatching. Call (904) 555-0199 and identify yourself as a portfolio partner. We prioritize your tenant’s emergency above standard residential calls and coordinate access using your documented procedures. If after-hours dispatch is not available, we schedule your tenant as the first priority call the following morning and communicate the timeline directly with the tenant so they know what to expect.

FAQ 5
Q: Can you help me plan AC replacements across my portfolio?
A: Absolutely. Our portfolio health reporting includes system age, condition scoring, and estimated remaining lifespan for every unit. We help you plan phased replacements — prioritizing the oldest or worst-performing systems first — so you can budget capital expenditures annually instead of reacting to surprise failures. We also provide volume pricing on equipment and labor for multi-unit replacement projects and can standardize equipment across your portfolio for simpler ongoing maintenance.

FAQ 6
Q: Do you provide invoicing that works for property management accounting?
A: Yes. Every invoice is itemized per property address and unit number with service details, parts used, labor time, and technician notes. This format is designed for direct expense allocation to individual properties or units without manual reprocessing. We can also provide summary reports across your entire portfolio for periodic financial review.
