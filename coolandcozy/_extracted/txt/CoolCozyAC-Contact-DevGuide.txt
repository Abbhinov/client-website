Emergency Service Detection
When “Emergency AC Repair” is selected in the Service Needed dropdown (either manually or via ?service=emergency-ac-repair parameter):
Display an inline alert above the Message field: “⚡ For immediate emergency service, call (904) 555-0199 directly. We prioritize phone calls for the fastest emergency response.”
The alert is styled as a Coral-bordered card (#E8603C left border, #FFF5F0 background, Poppins 14px/500)
The form still submits normally — the alert is informational, not a block
Submission with Emergency selected triggers SMS alert to owner’s mobile (see Section 4)

Form UX Requirements
Labels positioned ABOVE fields (never placeholder-only)
Required fields marked with red asterisk (*) + legend at top: “* Required field”
Inline validation on blur: specific error messages per field (not generic “Please fill in this field”)
Error messages appear immediately BELOW the errored field in #D43B3B, Poppins 13px
reCAPTCHA v3 (invisible, score-based) — no checkbox. Score threshold > 0.5.
Submit button: full-width, Coral bg (#E8603C), white text, Poppins 16px/600, 52px height, border-radius 12px
Submit button text: “Send My Request” (not generic “Submit”)
Submit button hover: #D04E2A, box-shadow 0 4px 12px rgba(232,96,60,0.3)
Prevent double-submission: disable button + show spinner on click, re-enable on error

4.3 Auto-Response Email
Sent immediately on successful submission via SendGrid or Google Workspace
Subject: “Cool & Cozy AC Repair — We’ve received your request”
Body includes: service requested, expected response time (within 2 hours during business hours), phone number for faster service, brief company intro
Language: if Preferred Language = Spanish, send Spanish-language auto-response
Emergency variant: auto-response emphasizes calling for fastest response
DEV ACTION: PLACEHOLDER: Auto-response email templates must be created for English and Spanish, standard and emergency variants (4 templates total). Branded with Cool & Cozy logo and colors.
4.4 CRM Integration
Create or update Zoho CRM lead record on every submission
Map: Full Name → Lead Name, Phone → Phone, Email → Email, Service → Custom field, Property Type → Custom field, Number of Units → Custom field, Language → Custom field
Lead Source: “Website Contact Form”
Lead Status: “New”
Emergency flag: Custom boolean field in Zoho
4.5 Spam Prevention
Honeypot field: if filled, reject silently (return 200 OK with no action)
reCAPTCHA v3: score < 0.5 = reject with generic error
Server-side validation: all fields re-validated regardless of client-side validation
Rate limiting: max 3 submissions per IP per hour
Email format: RFC 5322 compliance check
Phone format: US phone number pattern (10 digits)
