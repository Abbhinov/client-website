3.3 Why Finance Your AC System?
H2
Why Financing Often Makes More Financial Sense Than Paying Cash

Energy savings offset your payment — A new SEER2 16–18 system replacing a 10-year-old SEER 10 system saves approximately $40–$80 per month on JEA electricity. If your monthly financing payment is $80–$120, the net cost of upgrading is $0–$40 per month — and you have a new system with a full warranty instead of an aging system costing you money in both energy waste and repair bills.

Avoid the emergency markup — Homeowners who wait until their system fails completely often pay premium pricing for emergency replacement: rush ordering, overtime installation, and limited equipment selection. Financing allows you to plan the replacement proactively, choose the best equipment for your home, and schedule installation at your convenience — all at standard pricing.

Invest in higher efficiency — Without financing, many homeowners choose the cheapest system that meets the minimum standard. Financing lets you invest in a SEER2 18–20+ system that saves significantly more energy over its 15-year life — savings that often exceed the interest cost of the financing itself.

Preserve your emergency fund — A $6,000–$10,000 cash outlay depletes savings you may need for other expenses. Financing preserves your cash reserves while still getting the system your home needs today.

3.4 How Financing Works
H2
The Application Process: Simple, Fast, No Pressure

Step 1: Get Your Free Estimate
Cool & Cozy provides a free in-home estimate with 2–3 equipment options at different price points and efficiency levels. During the estimate, we discuss financing as one of the payment options — there is no pressure to finance or to decide on the spot.
Step 2: Apply in Minutes
If you are interested in financing, the application takes approximately 5 minutes and can be completed on a tablet during your estimate appointment or online at your convenience. The initial application uses a soft credit inquiry that does not affect your credit score. You receive a decision within minutes.
Step 3: Review Terms and Choose
If approved, you receive clear terms: monthly payment amount, interest rate, promotional period (if applicable), loan duration, and total cost of financing. Compare this against the cash price and your expected energy savings. Take time to decide — there is no expiration pressure.
Step 4: Installation Proceeds
Once you choose to proceed, Cool & Cozy schedules your installation. The financing paperwork is handled concurrently. You make your first payment approximately 30 days after installation is complete. You enjoy your new system immediately while payments are spread over time.

3.6 FAQ (4 Questions)
FAQ 1
Q: Does applying for AC financing affect my credit score?
A: The initial application uses a soft credit inquiry that does not affect your credit score. A hard credit inquiry (which may temporarily affect your score by a few points) occurs only if you choose to accept the financing offer and proceed with the loan. You can apply, see your terms, and decide without any impact to your credit.

FAQ 2
Q: What credit score do I need to qualify for AC financing?
A: Credit requirements vary by lending partner and plan type. Promotional 0% APR offers typically require higher credit scores, while standard rate plans may be available to a broader range of qualifications. Cool & Cozy’s lending partner provides decisions within minutes — applying is the fastest way to find out what you qualify for. There is no cost or obligation to apply. FAQ 3
Q: Can I pay off my AC financing early?
A: Yes. All financing plans available through Cool & Cozy have no prepayment penalties. You can make extra payments, pay off the balance in full, or increase your monthly payment at any time without fees. Early payoff on a promotional 0% APR plan means you pay no interest at all.

FAQ 4
Q: Is financing available for AC repairs, or only for new installations?
A: Financing is primarily available for new AC installations and system replacements, where the investment amount justifies monthly payment structuring. For smaller repairs, Cool & Cozy provides a clear written quote before work begins so you know the exact cost upfront. If a repair quote is high enough to warrant financing, we discuss that option during your service visit.
