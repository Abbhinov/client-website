1. How to Use This Guide
1.1 Relationship to Strategy Document
This developer implementation guide is one of approximately 40 page-level guides that collectively define the complete coolcozyac.com website. It implements the brand system, design tokens, content standards, and architectural decisions established in the Cool & Cozy AC Repair Website Strategy & Architecture document (v1.0, May 2026). That strategy document is the canonical reference for site-wide patterns.
This guide covers the Homepage (/) — the single most important page on the site. The homepage serves four simultaneous roles: primary landing page for brand searches and Map Pack clicks, referral validation endpoint for word-of-mouth leads, trust gateway for property managers evaluating a new contractor, and the conversion funnel that drives phone calls and form submissions. Every design decision, content block, and technical specification in this guide traces directly to one of these roles.
1.2 Design Philosophy: Jacksonville Coastal
The Cool & Cozy visual identity draws from Jacksonville’s coastal subtropical character — evergreen canopies, warm sand, sea-misted horizons, and sunset coral. This is intentionally distinct from the corporate navy-and-orange palette common in the HVAC industry. The brand says “comfortable, approachable, trustworthy” before the visitor reads a single word. Rounded corners, warm backgrounds, and breathing whitespace reinforce the “cozy” in the brand name. Professional credibility comes through content density and verifiable claims, not through dark corporate aesthetics.
The dual audience — homeowners seeking comfort and property managers seeking reliability — is served by a design that feels warm to residential visitors and organized enough to satisfy commercial evaluators. The property management section of the homepage uses a slightly more structured layout with metrics and proof points that speak directly to that audience.
1.3 Content Authoring Rules
All content is written fresh and original. No content from competitors, directory listings, or the audit documents.
Content targets 8th–10th grade reading level for residential sections; professional-but-clear for property management sections.
Every claim is factually verifiable: CAC1819948 license, BuildZoom top 13%, 21 permitted projects, incorporated 2018.
Jacksonville-specific references: St. Johns River humidity, coastal salt air corrosion, Florida Building Code, JEA utility context, neighborhood names.
AEO opening paragraph within first 100–150 words of visible body content answers: “Who provides AC repair in Jacksonville, FL?”
Bilingual indicator (“Se habla español”) appears in trust bar and footer; full Spanish content lives on /es.

5.1 Emergency / Seasonal Banner
Section Overview
A slim, dismissible banner pinned to the very top of the viewport, above the header. During summer cooling season (May–October), it displays an emergency/urgency message. During winter (November–April), it can switch to a heating or tune-up promotion. The banner creates immediate relevance and positions Cool & Cozy as responsive to Jacksonville’s current weather conditions.
Conversion Role: Drive emergency calls during peak season; promote seasonal offers during shoulder months
Visibility: Appears on first page load; dismissible via X button; dismissed state persists for session via sessionStorage
