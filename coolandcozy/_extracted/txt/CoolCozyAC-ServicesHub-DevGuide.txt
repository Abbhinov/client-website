1. How to Use This Guide
This guide defines the Services Hub page at /services. It is the anchor of the Services silo — the organizational parent of all eight service child pages. Its primary function is authority distribution: it receives link equity from the homepage, internal cross-links, and external sources, then distributes that authority downstream to each service child through contextual links.
The Services Hub is NOT a content-light index page. It carries substantive copy, an AEO opening paragraph, a dedicated property management callout, a process section, and its own FAQ with schema. It must stand alone as a valuable page for visitors who arrive directly from search, not just as a navigation waypoint.
Header, footer, sticky mobile CTA bar, and emergency banner are defined in the Homepage Developer Guide and apply identically to this page. They are referenced but not re-documented here.
1.1 Content Authoring Rules
All content fresh and original. No duplication from the homepage or any service child page.
The hub provides overview-depth content. Detailed service information lives on each child page.
Hub content must be genuinely useful to someone who does not click through to a child — it answers “What HVAC services does Cool & Cozy offer?” comprehensively.
Property management positioning is reinforced here with a dedicated section, distinct from the homepage PM section in angle and copy.
AEO opening paragraph answers: “What HVAC services are available in Jacksonville, FL?”

FAQ 1
Q: What brands of AC equipment do you service?
A: Cool & Cozy services all major residential and commercial AC brands including Carrier, Trane, Lennox, Rheem, Goodman, Daikin, York, Bryant, Amana, and Mitsubishi. Whether your system was installed by us or by another contractor, we can diagnose, repair, and maintain it. We are not locked into any single manufacturer — our recommendations are based on what is best for your specific situation and budget.

FAQ 2
Q: Do you offer maintenance plans or service agreements?
A: Yes. Cool & Cozy offers maintenance agreements for both residential homeowners and property managers. Plans include scheduled seasonal tune-ups (typically twice per year — spring and fall), priority scheduling for service calls, and discounted diagnostic fees. For property managers with multiple units, we build custom agreements with per-unit pricing and coordinated scheduling across your entire portfolio.

FAQ 3
Q: Can you handle both residential and commercial HVAC work?
A: Yes. Our Florida contractor license CAC1819948 covers both residential and commercial HVAC work. We serve single-family homeowners, multi-unit property managers, Airbnb hosts, restaurants, retail spaces, medical offices, and small commercial buildings throughout Jacksonville and Duval County. Our commercial services page has more detail on how we work with business clients.

FAQ 4
Q: How quickly can you get to me for an AC repair?
A: For standard repair requests, we typically schedule service within 24–48 hours. For emergency situations — especially during Jacksonville’s summer months when indoor temperatures can become unsafe — we offer same-day priority response. Call (904) 555-0199 and let us know it’s urgent.

FAQ 5
Q: Do I need to be home for a service call?
A: For most AC repairs and maintenance, yes — we need access to both the indoor and outdoor components of your system. We’ll give you a specific arrival window so you are not waiting all day. For property managers, we coordinate access directly with tenants or use your lock-box/key procedures so you do not need to be on-site for every unit.
