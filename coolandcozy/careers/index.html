Build Your HVAC Career with Cool & Cozy in Jacksonville

We're a family-owned Jacksonville HVAC company looking for technicians who take pride in their work, treat customers' homes with respect, and want to grow with a team that values honesty and craftsmanship.

What Makes Cool & Cozy Different as an Employer

Family-owned, not corporate

You are not a number. You work directly with the owner, you know every member of the team, and your voice matters in how we operate. There are no layers of management between you and the decision-makers.

Honest work environment

We do not upsell customers on work they do not need, and we do not pressure technicians to push unnecessary repairs. If the capacitor is the problem, we replace the capacitor. Your integrity is never compromised for revenue.

Bilingual team, diverse community

Our team serves Jacksonville's diverse community in English and Spanish. If you are bilingual, your language skills are valued and utilized daily. If you are not, you will work alongside bilingual colleagues and serve a customer base that reflects Jacksonville's growing diversity.

Growing company, growing opportunities

Cool & Cozy is expanding. Early team members have the opportunity to grow into senior roles, lead crews, and help shape the company's direction as we add territory and services.

HVAC Service Technician

Full-time Jacksonville, FL · Field

Responsibilities

  • Diagnose, repair, and maintain residential and commercial AC systems throughout Jacksonville and surrounding areas
  • Install new AC systems including split systems, package units, ductless mini-splits, and heat pumps
  • Execute preventive maintenance tune-ups per Cool & Cozy's 26-point checklist
  • Communicate clearly with customers about diagnoses, repair options, and pricing — in English and/or Spanish
  • Maintain a clean, organized service vehicle and work area
  • Document all work with accurate notes and photos for customer records

Requirements

  • Valid Florida HVAC contractor license or EPA 608 Universal certification (CAC or registered technician under a licensed contractor)
  • 2+ years residential HVAC experience (commercial experience a plus)
  • Valid Florida driver's license with clean driving record
  • Own basic hand tools (company provides specialty and diagnostic equipment)
  • Bilingual English/Spanish strongly preferred but not required
  • Reliable, punctual, and professional in customer-facing interactions
  • Comfortable working in attics, crawlspaces, and Florida summer heat

Benefits

  • Competitive pay (range confirmed during interview)
  • Company service vehicle and fuel card
  • Specialty tools and diagnostic equipment provided
  • Paid training and ongoing professional development
  • Flexible scheduling with no mandatory overtime
  • Family-owned culture with direct owner access
