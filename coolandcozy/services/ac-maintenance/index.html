What Does an AC Tune-Up Include?

A comprehensive 26-point inspection, cleaning, and performance test — exactly what's covered, with no vague claims.

Inspection

Thorough inspection of all system components:

  • Thermostat calibration and operation check
  • Electrical connections tightened and tested
  • Capacitor and contactor condition check
  • Compressor amperage reading and comparison to specification
  • Blower motor amperage and operation
  • Condensate drain line flow verification
  • Drain pan condition and alignment
  • Ductwork connection inspection at air handler
  • Safety controls and limit switches tested
  • Overall system age and condition assessment

Cleaning

Cleaning critical surfaces for maximum efficiency:

  • Outdoor condenser coil rinse and debris removal
  • Indoor evaporator coil inspection (cleaning if accessible)
  • Condensate drain line flush (algae and buildup removal)
  • Air filter check and replacement recommendation
  • Outdoor unit perimeter cleared (vegetation, debris)
  • Indoor supply and return vent inspection

Performance Testing

Verifying your system is operating to specification:

  • Refrigerant pressure check (high and low side)
  • Temperature split measurement (supply vs. return air)
  • Airflow volume verification at supply vents
  • System cycling test (proper on/off behavior)
  • Energy consumption comparison to manufacturer baseline
  • Written report with findings and recommendations

Why Jacksonville's Climate Makes AC Maintenance Essential

This isn't generic "maintenance is good" advice — Jacksonville's environment creates specific demands that make maintenance more critical here than in cooler, drier climates.

  • 2,500+ annual operating hours

    Your AC runs roughly 3x the national average. Components wear proportionally faster. A capacitor rated for 10 years in Michigan may last 6 in Jacksonville.

  • Sustained high humidity (60–90% year-round)

    Your system removes moisture as well as heat. Without clean coils and proper refrigerant charge, it loses dehumidification capacity — leaving your home cool but clammy.

  • Salt air corrosion (coastal and Beaches neighborhoods)

    Properties near the Intracoastal Waterway and the Beaches face accelerated corrosion on outdoor condenser coils, copper refrigerant lines, and electrical connections. Regular inspection catches corrosion before it causes failure.

  • Algae growth in condensate drains

    Jacksonville's warmth and humidity create ideal conditions for algae to clog condensate drain lines. A clogged drain causes water damage to floors, walls, and ceilings. A 60-second drain flush during maintenance prevents this.

  • Pollen and debris loading

    Live oaks, pine pollen, and lawn clippings accumulate on outdoor condenser coils, reducing airflow and forcing the compressor to work harder. Seasonal coil cleaning restores efficiency.

  • JEA energy costs

    A poorly maintained system uses 15–25% more electricity. On a $200/month summer JEA bill, that's $30–$50/month in avoidable cost — paying for the maintenance visit within 2–3 months.
