Our Approach to Property Management HVAC

A True HVAC Partnership for Your Portfolio

Most HVAC companies treat commercial and property management work as an afterthought — the same residential technician, the same scheduling process, the same invoicing. Cool & Cozy builds a structured partnership around your portfolio's specific needs: dedicated contact, documented procedures, and operational commitments that make HVAC one less thing you have to manage.

Single Point of Contact

You get one dedicated contact at Cool & Cozy who knows your properties, your procedures, and your expectations. No explaining your portfolio from scratch every time you call. Your contact knows which units have older systems, which tenants prefer Spanish communication, and which properties use lockbox access.

Documented Access Procedures

We document your preferred access method for every property: lockbox codes, tenant scheduling requirements, management-escorted access, gate codes, and after-hours entry procedures. Our technicians arrive prepared, reducing tenant disruption and eliminating the "who has the key?" scramble.

Bilingual Tenant Communication

In Jacksonville's diverse communities, a significant percentage of tenants prefer Spanish-language communication. Cool & Cozy's bilingual technicians communicate directly with tenants in English or Spanish — scheduling access, explaining the repair, and following up — so your tenants feel heard and your office doesn't need to translate.

Priority Emergency Response

A tenant AC emergency is your emergency. Portfolio partners get priority dispatching above standard residential calls. We understand that a broken AC in a rental means a phone call from an unhappy tenant, a potential lease issue, and a problem that needs resolution today — not Thursday.

Transparent Per-Unit Invoicing

Every invoice is itemized per unit with service details, parts used, and technician notes. No bundled invoices that force you to allocate costs yourself. Each line item matches to a specific property address and unit number for clean expense tracking and owner reporting.

Portfolio Health Reporting

After each maintenance cycle, you receive a portfolio-wide system condition report: each unit scored green (good), yellow (monitor — aging component or efficiency decline), or red (plan for replacement). This lets you budget capital expenditures proactively instead of reacting to emergency failures.

Commercial HVAC Equipment We Repair, Install & Maintain

Specific equipment expertise that residential-only contractors can't match.

  • Rooftop Units (RTUs) — Self-contained heating and cooling systems installed on commercial rooftops. Common in strip malls, restaurants, and small office buildings. We service, repair, and replace RTUs from all major commercial manufacturers.
  • PTAC / PTHP Units — Packaged terminal air conditioners and heat pumps — the individual wall units found in hotels, assisted living facilities, and some apartment buildings. We service, clean, and replace PTACs across multi-unit properties.
  • Commercial Split Systems — Larger-capacity versions of residential split systems, used in offices, medical practices, and mid-size commercial spaces. We handle systems up to 25 tons.
  • VRF/VRV Systems — Variable refrigerant flow systems that provide zone-level control across large commercial buildings. We service Daikin, Mitsubishi, and LG VRF systems.
  • Ductless Multi-Zone Commercial — Multi-head mini-split installations for commercial spaces without ductwork, including converted historic buildings and mixed-use properties.
  • Make-Up Air & Exhaust Systems — Kitchen ventilation make-up air units and commercial exhaust systems for restaurants and food service. Required for code compliance and indoor air quality.
  • Walk-In Coolers & Freezers — Refrigeration systems for restaurants, convenience stores, and food storage operations. We diagnose compressor, evaporator, and defrost system issues.
