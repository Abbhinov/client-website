While You Wait: 6 Steps to Stay Safe and Protect Your System

If you've already called us, follow these steps until our technician arrives. Steps 1–3 occasionally resolve the issue entirely.

Turn the system OFF at the thermostat

If you hear unusual noises, smell burning, or see ice forming, continuing to run the system can cause additional damage. Switching to "Off" or "Fan Only" prevents compressor damage.

Check your circuit breaker panel

A tripped breaker is one of the most common causes of sudden AC failure. If the HVAC breaker is tripped, reset it once. If it trips again immediately, leave it off — this indicates an electrical fault that needs professional diagnosis.

Check your thermostat

Verify it is set to "Cool" (not "Heat" or "Off"), the temperature is set below the current room temperature, and the batteries are not dead (if it uses batteries). A blank thermostat screen often just needs fresh batteries.

Maximize natural airflow

Open interior doors and any windows that catch a breeze. Use portable fans to circulate air. Close blinds and curtains on sun-facing windows to reduce heat gain. Move to the lowest level of your home (heat rises).

Stay hydrated

In Jacksonville's humidity, dehydration happens faster than you expect. Drink water, avoid alcohol and caffeine, and keep pets' water bowls full. If elderly family members or infants are present and indoor temperatures exceed 85°F, consider relocating them temporarily.

Clear the area around your outdoor unit

Remove any debris, vegetation, or objects within 2 feet of the condenser. This gives the technician immediate access on arrival and may even resolve airflow-related issues.
