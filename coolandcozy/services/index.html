Residential & General HVAC Services

Keeping Jacksonville homes comfortable year-round.

AC Repair & Diagnostics

When your air conditioner stops cooling, makes unusual noises, cycles on and off rapidly, or simply won't turn on, Cool & Cozy's licensed technicians diagnose the root cause and fix it — often the same day you call. We work on all major brands and system types, from central air to ductless mini-splits.

Common issues: warm air blowing, frozen evaporator coil, refrigerant leak, capacitor failure, compressor issues, thermostat malfunction

AC Installation & System Replacement

Whether you're building new, replacing a failed system, or upgrading to a high-efficiency unit, Cool & Cozy sizes, recommends, and installs the right equipment for your Jacksonville home. We calculate your cooling load based on square footage, insulation, window exposure, and Florida's specific humidity demands — not a one-size-fits-all approach.

Common applications: new construction, end-of-life replacement, efficiency upgrade, system upsizing, ductless mini-split conversion

Preventive Maintenance & Tune-Ups

Jacksonville's year-round cooling demand puts constant stress on your AC system. Regular maintenance catches small issues before they become expensive repairs, extends equipment life, maintains warranty coverage, and keeps your energy bills predictable. Cool & Cozy offers one-time tune-ups and recurring maintenance agreements with priority scheduling.

Includes: coil cleaning, filter replacement, refrigerant check, electrical inspection, condensate drain flush, thermostat calibration

Emergency AC Repair

When it's 95 degrees in July, high humidity is pushing the heat index past 105, and your AC just stopped working — that's not a next-week problem. Cool & Cozy offers priority emergency response for Jacksonville homeowners and property managers. Call and tell us it's an emergency — we prioritize these calls above routine service.

Emergency scenarios: complete system failure in summer heat, AC tripping breaker repeatedly, water flooding from unit, dangerous indoor temperatures

Learn More

Ductwork Repair, Sealing & Replacement

Your AC system is only as effective as the ducts delivering cooled air throughout your home. Leaking, disconnected, or poorly designed ductwork wastes 20–30% of your cooling energy and creates hot spots in certain rooms. Cool & Cozy inspects, seals, repairs, and replaces ductwork to restore proper airflow and efficiency.

Signs of duct problems: uneven cooling room to room, high energy bills, excessive dust, whistling or rattling from vents, rooms that never cool

Learn More

Indoor Air Quality Solutions

Jacksonville's subtropical humidity creates ideal conditions for mold, mildew, dust mites, and airborne allergens inside your home. Cool & Cozy installs whole-home air purification, UV germicidal lights, HEPA filtration upgrades, and dehumidification systems that work with your existing HVAC to deliver cleaner, healthier indoor air.

Solutions offered: whole-home dehumidifiers, air purifiers, UV lights for mold prevention, HEPA filter upgrades, ERV/HRV ventilation
