Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a haircut cost at Cutz by Raul?

Men's haircuts start at $30 for a 30-minute appointment. Kids' haircuts (under 12) are $25. Combo services like haircut plus beard grooming range from $40 to $50 depending on the package. Beard shaping as a standalone service is $20. All prices are transparent with no hidden fees.

Do I need an appointment, or do you accept walk-ins?

Appointments are recommended and can be booked online through Booksy for guaranteed availability.

Is Cutz by Raul good with kids?

Yes. Raul is known for his patience with young clients, from toddlers experiencing their first haircut to pre-teens who want the same sharp styles as adults. The studio is family-friendly with a comfortable environment designed to put kids at ease.

What are the hours and where is the shop located?

Cutz by Raul is located inside Blessed by Blends Barber Studio at 7822 US-301 S in Riverview, Florida 33578. Hours are Monday through Friday 9 AM to 7 PM, Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM, and Sunday 9 AM to 2 PM. Free parking is available in the plaza lot.

What forms of payment do you accept?

Cutz by Raul accepts all major credit cards, debit cards, and cash. Payment is collected at the time of service.
