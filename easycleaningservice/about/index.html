About Easy Clean Service – Locally Owned House Cleaning in Tampa, FL

Easy Clean Service is not a franchise. It is not a faceless booking platform that sends a different stranger to your home every visit. It is a locally owned Tampa cleaning company founded and personally managed by Kessia Marquez – someone who has been cleaning Tampa homes with her own hands since 2014 and who treats every client’s home with the same care and respect she gives her own. When you call Easy Clean, you talk to the owner. When something needs attention, the owner handles it.

Meet the Owner: Kessia Marquez

Kessia Marquez started Easy Clean Service in 2014 with a simple belief: that every Tampa family deserves a clean, healthy home and the time to actually enjoy it. What began as one woman with a work ethic, a reliable car, and a commitment to doing the job right has grown into a trusted cleaning team that serves homes across 12 Tampa-area neighborhoods, from the historic bungalows of Seminole Heights to the waterfront estates of Hyde Park.

More than a decade later, Kessia still personally manages every client relationship. She answers the phone when you call. She builds your cleaning plan based on your specific home, your schedule, and your priorities. She selects and trains every member of the cleaning team, and she holds them to the same standard she held herself when she was the one walking through your front door with a bucket of supplies and a checklist. That hands-on involvement is not a marketing slogan – it is how Kessia runs her business every single day.

Kessia speaks fluent Spanish and English, making Easy Clean one of the few Tampa cleaning services where every client can communicate comfortably in their preferred language. In a city as culturally diverse as Tampa – especially in communities like Town ’N’ Country where over 60 percent of residents speak Spanish at home – this bilingual capability is not just a convenience; it is a meaningful differentiator that builds trust from the very first phone call.

What Sets Easy Clean Apart

Personal Accountability, Not Corporate Scripts

When you hire Easy Clean, you work directly with the owner. Kessia builds your cleaning plan, assigns your team, and personally ensures the quality of every visit. If something is not right, you do not file a ticket with a call center – you tell Kessia, and she makes it right.

Same Trusted Team on Every Visit

We assign the same cleaner or team to your home on every recurring appointment. They learn your home’s layout, your preferences, and the surfaces in your home, producing faster, better, more consistent results visit after visit.

Eco-Friendly & Pet-Safe Products

Every product we use is non-toxic, environmentally responsible, and completely safe for children, pets, and people with sensitivities. Tampa families should not have to choose between a clean home and a safe one.

No Contracts, No Pressure

No long-term agreements. No cancellation fees. We earn your continued business through the quality of our work, not the fine print of a contract. Pause, skip, or change your schedule any time.

100% Satisfaction Guarantee

If any area of your home does not meet your expectations after a cleaning visit, let us know within 24 hours and we will return to address it at no additional charge. We have maintained this guarantee since day one.

Bilingual Service – Se Habla Español

Kessia speaks fluent Spanish, and many of our team members do as well. Request your estimate, communicate preferences, and ask questions in whichever language is most comfortable for you.

The People Who Clean Your Home

Hiring, training, and trust standards we hold every team member to.

  1. 1

    Background Verification

    Every team member is background-checked before they are hired. We verify identity, employment history, and references. We do not cut corners on this step because your safety and peace of mind are non-negotiable.

  2. 2

    Hands-On Training

    New team members train alongside experienced cleaners before working independently. They learn our cleaning checklist, product usage protocols, surface-specific techniques, and our client communication standards.

  3. 3

    Product Knowledge

    Our team is trained on every product we use – what it does, what surfaces it is safe for, and what to avoid. The wrong product on the wrong surface (terrazzo, marble, quartz, natural stone) causes permanent damage. We do not let that happen.

  4. 4

    Quality Standards

    Every cleaning visit follows a structured checklist. Kessia reviews client feedback regularly and addresses any quality concerns immediately. The same-team-every-visit model means your cleaner develops expertise in your specific home over time.

  5. 5

    Full Insurance Coverage

    Easy Clean Service carries general liability insurance that protects both our team and your property. In the unlikely event of accidental damage during a cleaning, you are fully covered. We provide proof of insurance upon request.

A Tampa Business Serving Tampa Families

Easy Clean Service is not headquartered in another state. We are not a franchise paying licensing fees to a national brand. Every dollar you spend with Easy Clean stays in the Tampa Bay economy – supporting local jobs, local families, and a local business owner who lives in the same community she serves.

Over the past decade, we have built relationships across the Tampa area that go beyond cleaning. We know the neighborhoods because we work in them every day. We understand the specific challenges of Tampa homes – the humidity that accelerates mold growth in bathrooms, the hard water that stains fixtures and shower glass, the pollen that coats every surface during spring, and the sandy floors that come with Florida living. We clean Tampa homes the way Tampa homes need to be cleaned, because this is our home too.

We currently serve 12 neighborhoods across Hillsborough and Pasco Counties, covering over 150 sub-communities from South Tampa to Wesley Chapel and everywhere in between.
