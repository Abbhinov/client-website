How Much Does House Cleaning Cost in Tampa, FL? (2026 Guide)

By Kessia Marquez, Owner of Easy Clean Service · Updated April 2026 · 8 min read

House cleaning in Tampa, FL typically costs between $100 and $350 per visit for residential services, with deep cleaning, move-out cleaning, and commercial services running higher depending on the scope of work. The exact price you will pay depends on four main factors: the type of cleaning service you need, the size and condition of your home, how frequently you want the service, and whether you have any special requirements like pet hair removal or specialty surface care.

Tampa’s unique climate also plays a role in cleaning costs that many national price guides overlook. The constant humidity accelerates mold and mildew growth in bathrooms and kitchens, hard water from the local supply leaves mineral deposits on glass and fixtures, and pollen coats surfaces for months during spring. These Florida-specific challenges often mean Tampa cleaning visits require more time and more specialized products than the same-sized home in a drier climate would need.

This guide breaks down what Tampa homeowners actually pay for each type of cleaning service in 2026, based on local market data and Easy Clean Service’s experience cleaning homes across 12 Tampa-area neighborhoods for over a decade.

What Factors Affect House Cleaning Prices in Tampa?

  • Home condition. A maintained home is faster and easier to clean than one that has not been touched in six months. First-time cleanings almost always cost more than subsequent visits, which is why we recommend starting with a deep cleaning and then transitioning to regular maintenance.
  • Number of bathrooms. Bathrooms are the most labor-intensive room. A 3-bedroom home with 3 bathrooms will cost more to clean than a 3-bedroom home with 1 bathroom.
  • Pets. Homes with dogs and cats require extra time for pet hair removal. If you have multiple pets, expect your cleaning cost to be 10 to 20 percent higher.
  • Specialty surfaces. Terrazzo floors (common in older Carrollwood homes), marble countertops (common in Hyde Park and South Tampa), and natural stone showers all require specific products that take more care to apply.
  • Tampa’s climate. Humidity is the invisible factor that makes Tampa cleaning costs slightly higher than national averages. Mold growth, mildew on grout and caulk, and dust mites thrive in our climate – and need to be addressed every visit.
  • Frequency. Cleaning more often actually reduces your per-visit cost. Weekly customers pay the least per cleaning because each visit is faster.

How to Get the Best Value on House Cleaning in Tampa

  1. Start with a deep cleaning, then maintain with recurring visits. Reset your home to a fresh baseline, then switch to weekly or biweekly regular cleaning to keep it there. Each recurring visit is faster and cheaper.
  2. Choose biweekly if you are on a budget. Roughly half the monthly cost of weekly service. The sweet spot for most Tampa households.
  3. Bundle services when you can. Combined move-out and move-in cleaning, recurring turnover agreements for rentals – bundles often come with better rates.
  4. Declutter before the cleaning team arrives. You are paying for cleaning, not organizing.
  5. Skip the big-name franchise and hire local. National franchises charge premium prices to cover franchise fees. A locally owned company like Easy Clean provides the same (or better) quality because the owner is directly accountable.
