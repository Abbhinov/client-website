1. Page Strategy & Purpose
1.1 Page Purpose
The Contact page serves three critical functions that affect the entire site’s performance. First, it is the primary conversion endpoint – the destination where every “Get Your Free Estimate” CTA button across the site ultimately sends visitors who are ready to act. Second, it is a foundational local SEO asset – the page where Google validates the business’s NAP (Name, Address, Phone) data, which must match the Google Business Profile and every other directory listing exactly. Third, it is the page that AI answer engines and voice assistants reference when a user asks “how do I contact Easy Clean Service in Tampa” or “what are their hours.”
Unlike most other pages on the site, the Contact page prioritizes immediate action over content consumption. The design should lead with the phone number, the form, and the WhatsApp link – putting every contact channel within one scroll on mobile and visible without scrolling on desktop.

3.5 Contact-Specific FAQ
  SECTION ID: #contact-faq  
H2 Heading
Common Questions About Getting Started
Q1: How quickly will you respond to my estimate request?
During business hours (Monday–Friday 8AM–6PM, Saturday 9AM–2PM), we respond to form submissions and WhatsApp messages within a few hours. Phone calls are answered live whenever possible. If you reach voicemail, we return calls the same business day.
Q2: Is the estimate really free?
Yes, completely free with no obligation. We will ask about your home’s size, its current condition, and what service you need, then provide a clear quote with no hidden fees or surprise charges. You are under no pressure to book.
Q3: Can I get an estimate in Spanish?
Absolutely. Our owner Kessia speaks fluent Spanish. Select “Español” as your preferred language on the form, or simply call (813) 534-9216 and ask for your estimate in Spanish. We will communicate in whichever language is most comfortable for you throughout the entire process.
Q4: What information do I need to provide for an accurate estimate?
The more detail, the better, but at minimum we need your home’s size (number of bedrooms and bathrooms), your zip code or neighborhood, and what type of cleaning you need. Helpful extras include how long it has been since your last professional cleaning, whether you have pets, and any specialty surfaces (marble, terrazzo, hardwood) that require specific products.
Q5: Do I need to be home for the estimate?
For most standard estimates, we can provide an accurate quote based on the information you share by phone or form. We do not require an in-home walkthrough before booking. Once we arrive for the first cleaning, we will verify the scope and adjust if needed – always communicating any changes to you before proceeding.

5. On-Page SEO, Internal Linking & Conversion
5.1 NAP Consistency Requirements
* Business name: "Easy Clean Service" – use this exact format everywhere. Not "Easy Clean," not "Easy Clean Services" (plural), not "Easy Cleaning Service."
* Phone: (813) 534-9216 – use this exact format with parentheses. Match in schema, GBP, Yelp, Facebook, and all directory listings.
* Address: Match whatever is used in the Google Business Profile. If GBP uses a service-area model without a street address, do NOT publish a street address on the website.
* Hours: Must match GBP hours exactly. If Kessia changes hours seasonally, update both simultaneously.
WARNING: NAP inconsistencies across the web are one of the top reasons local businesses fail to rank in Google’s Local 3-Pack. Every directory listing, social profile, and on-site mention of the business name, phone, and address must be identical.
5.2 Internal Linking Strategy
* Every CTA button on the site: "Get Your Free Estimate" buttons across all 20+ pages link to either this page or to on-page #estimate-form anchors. The Contact page is the universal conversion endpoint.
