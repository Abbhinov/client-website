3.8 Frequently Asked Questions
  SECTION ID: #faq  
H2 Heading
Questions Tampa Airbnb Hosts Ask About Our Cleaning Service
Q1: How much does Airbnb turnover cleaning cost in Tampa?
Standard turnover cleaning in Tampa ranges from $100 to $400+ per visit depending on the property size. A typical 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom rental usually falls between $140 and $220 for a standard turnover. Deep turnovers, which include inside-appliance cleaning and baseboard detailing, cost more and are recommended every 5 to 10 bookings. Linen changes, restocking, and damage reporting are included in our standard service at no extra charge. Call (813) 534-9216 for a quote tailored to your property.
Q2: Can you handle same-day turnovers between guests?
Yes. We understand that back-to-back bookings are common in Tampa’s rental market, especially during peak season. We coordinate with your booking calendar to schedule turnovers during the window between check-out and check-in. For tight turnarounds, give us as much advance notice as possible so we can prioritize your property in our schedule.
Q3: Do you handle laundry and linen changes?
Yes. We strip all beds and swap used towels during every turnover. If you provide your own linen sets, we launder them on-site (if washer and dryer are available) or swap in a fresh set and launder the used linens for your next turnover. We remake all beds to hotel-quality staging standards with crisp sheets, properly tucked corners, and neatly positioned decorative pillows.
Q4: Will you restock guest supplies?
Yes. As part of every turnover, we check and replenish guest essentials based on your custom supply list. This typically includes toilet paper, paper towels, dish soap, trash bags, hand soap, shampoo, conditioner, and body wash. When inventory runs low, we notify you so you can resupply. If you prefer, we can purchase supplies on your behalf.
Q5: Do you report property damage after turnovers?
Yes. During every turnover, our team does a walkthrough check for any guest damage, missing items, stains, broken fixtures, or maintenance issues. We report findings to you immediately so you can document the damage for your platform’s resolution center or your property insurance before the next guest arrives. Early detection protects you financially and ensures the next guest has a flawless experience.
Q6: Do you work with property management companies?
Absolutely. We partner with several property management companies and co-hosting services in the Tampa area. We coordinate scheduling through your preferred communication channel, follow platform-specific checklists, and can integrate into your existing turnover workflow. Contact us to discuss volume pricing for multi-property accounts.
