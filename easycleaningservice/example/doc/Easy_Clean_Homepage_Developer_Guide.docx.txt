3.10 Frequently Asked Questions

H2 Heading
Frequently Asked Questions About Our Cleaning Services
Implement as an accordion component (expandable/collapsible). The questions and answers below match the FAQPage schema in Section 2.2. Always keep the schema and the visible FAQ content in sync.
Q1: How much does house cleaning cost in Tampa, FL?
House cleaning costs in Tampa typically range from $120 to $250 for a standard cleaning, depending on the size of your home, its current condition, and which services you select. Deep cleaning and move-out cleaning generally run higher due to the extra time and detail involved. We provide personalized free estimates so you know exactly what to expect before we start. Call us at (813) 534-9216 or fill out the form on this page to get your custom quote.
Q2: What is included in a deep cleaning service?
Our deep cleaning covers your entire home from top to bottom. That includes dusting ceiling fans and light fixtures, wiping down baseboards and door frames, cleaning inside the oven and microwave, scrubbing grout in kitchens and bathrooms, detailing window sills and blinds, and sanitizing all high-touch surfaces like light switches and door handles. We also move smaller furniture to clean underneath. It is the most thorough cleaning we offer and is popular as a first-time service or a seasonal reset.
Q3: Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment?
Yes. Our team arrives fully equipped with all cleaning products, tools, and equipment. We use eco-friendly, pet-safe, and child-safe products that are effective without leaving behind harsh chemical residues or strong fumes. If you have a preferred product you would like us to use in your home, just let us know and we are happy to accommodate.
Q4: Are your cleaners insured and background-checked?
Absolutely. Easy Clean Service is fully insured for your protection and peace of mind. Every member of our cleaning team is carefully vetted, trained in our cleaning procedures, and held to strict quality standards. We treat your home with the same care and respect we give our own.
Q5: What areas of Tampa do you serve?
We serve the greater Tampa Bay area including South Tampa, Westchase, Carrollwood, Brandon, Riverview, Lutz, Wesley Chapel, Town N Country, Temple Terrace, New Tampa, Seminole Heights, Hyde Park, and surrounding communities in Hillsborough County. If you are not sure whether we cover your area, give us a call at (813) 534-9216 and we will let you know.
Q6: Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
That is entirely up to you. Many of our regular clients provide a key or door code so our team can clean while they are at work or running errands. Others prefer to be home, and that is perfectly fine too. We will work around whatever is most comfortable for you.
Q7: How do I prepare my home before a cleaning visit?
Just pick up personal items, clothing, and toys from the floors so our team can focus on the actual cleaning rather than organizing. If you have pets, please make sure they are comfortable and secured in a safe area. Everything else, we handle.

6.3 Exit-Intent Popup (Desktop Only)
Trigger a popup when the user moves their cursor toward the browser’s close/back button area. The popup should offer a first-time customer incentive:
Headline: "Wait – Get 15% Off Your First Cleaning!"
Body: "Enter your email and we will send you a discount code for 15% off your first cleaning service."
Fields: Email address + Submit button
Show only once per session. Store a cookie/localStorage flag to prevent repeated popups.
6.4 Social Proof Notification (Optional)
Consider implementing a small toast notification that periodically appears in the bottom-left corner showing recent activity, such as: "Sarah from Westchase just booked a deep cleaning – 2 hours ago." These can be generated from real booking data or rotated from a curated list of recent customers (with their permission). This tactic increases urgency and social proof.
* Color contrast: All text must meet a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against its background (3:1 for large text).
* Alt text: Every image must have descriptive alt text. Decorative images use alt="" (empty).
* Keyboard navigation: All interactive elements (links, buttons, form fields, accordion toggles) must be reachable and operable via keyboard.
* Focus indicators: Visible focus outlines on all interactive elements. Do not remove the default outline without providing an alternative.
* Form labels: Every form input must have an associated <label> element. Do not rely on placeholder text alone.
* ARIA landmarks: Use role attributes or semantic HTML5 elements for navigation, main content, and footer.
* Skip link: Add a "Skip to main content" link as the first focusable element on the page.
* Language attribute: Set lang="en" on the <html> element.
