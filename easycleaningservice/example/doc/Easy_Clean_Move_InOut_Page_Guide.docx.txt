3.8 Frequently Asked Questions
  SECTION ID: #faq  
H2 Heading
Move-In & Move-Out Cleaning Questions from Tampa Movers
Q1: How much does move-out cleaning cost in Tampa?
Move-out cleaning in Tampa typically ranges from $180 to $650 depending on the size and condition of the property. A standard 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom apartment in average condition usually falls between $250 and $380. The final price depends on the square footage, number of bathrooms, current cleanliness, and any special requests. Call us at (813) 534-9216 or use the form on this page for a free, personalized estimate.
Q2: Will move-out cleaning help me get my security deposit back?
In most cases, yes. Cleaning charges are one of the most common reasons landlords deduct from security deposits in Florida. A professional move-out cleaning costs less than what most landlords charge if they hire their own crew and bill you for it. We clean to inspection standards and many of our customers report receiving their full deposit back after using our service.
Q3: How far in advance should I book a move-out cleaning?
We recommend booking as soon as you know your move-out date, ideally one to two weeks ahead. However, we understand that moves rarely go exactly as planned, so we do our best to accommodate short-notice requests – sometimes even within 24 to 48 hours. The sooner you contact us, the more flexibility we have with scheduling.
Q4: Does the property need to be completely empty for move cleaning?
Empty properties allow for the most thorough cleaning since we can reach every surface, corner, and closet without working around furniture and boxes. However, we can absolutely clean a partially furnished or occupied property. If large items remain, we will clean around and behind them to the extent that is safely possible. For the best results, we recommend scheduling the cleaning after the movers have removed the bulk of your belongings.
Q5: Do you offer both move-in and move-out cleaning for the same customer?
Yes, and this is actually one of our most popular requests. Many Tampa residents hire us to clean their old apartment or house for the landlord inspection and then clean their new home before they start unpacking. We can schedule both cleanings on the same day or on consecutive days depending on the size of the properties. Ask about bundled pricing when both services are booked together.
Q6: Can you work with my landlord’s specific cleaning checklist?
Absolutely. If your landlord or property management company has provided a cleaning checklist for move-out, send it to us and we will make sure every item on their list is addressed. Our standard move-out cleaning already covers everything most landlords expect, but we are always happy to verify and add any specific requirements they have.
