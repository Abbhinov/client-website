3.2 Neighborhood-Specific Content – Seminole Heights [UNIQUE]
  SECTION ID: #about-seminole-heights  
H2 Heading
Expert Cleaning for Seminole Heights’ Historic Bungalows and Creative Community
Seminole Heights was born in 1911 when developer T. Roy Young platted 40 acres into what would become Tampa’s first true suburb. The neighborhood was founded during the peak of the American bungalow movement, and as a result, Seminole Heights boasts more Craftsman bungalows than any other neighborhood in Tampa. These wood-frame homes feature high ceilings, wide eaves, large front porches, and the cross-ventilation design that kept Florida homes cool before air conditioning. Over a century later, many of these original bungalows have been lovingly restored by homeowners who value architectural character over cookie-cutter convenience.
Cleaning a Seminole Heights bungalow is not the same as cleaning a modern suburban home, and our team knows the difference. Original hardwood floors – often heart pine or oak that has been refinished to a beautiful patina – require pH-neutral cleaners and careful mopping to protect the wood and its finish. Plaster walls, common in homes built before the 1950s, can be damaged by the moisture from aggressive cleaning. High ceilings (often 10 feet or more) collect cobwebs and dust in crown molding details that standard cleaning ignores. Original bathroom tile with nearly a century of grout requires professional-grade treatment to remove embedded staining without damaging the delicate glazed surfaces. We treat these homes with the respect their history deserves.
The three sub-neighborhoods that make up Seminole Heights each have their own character. Old Seminole Heights, nearest the Hillsborough River, has the highest concentration of restored historic homes and is home to the area’s most active neighborhood association. South Seminole Heights sits closest to downtown Tampa, making it popular with young professionals who walk or bike to work. Southeast Seminole Heights – named Best Neighborhood in America by NUSA in 2003 – features many restored bungalows alongside a growing number of new-construction infill homes that bring contemporary design to historic streets.
Seminole Heights’ renaissance over the past decade has brought new energy, new residents, and new construction alongside the historic fabric. Some homeowners live in fully restored 1920s originals; others occupy brand-new builds constructed on lots where older homes were demolished. A few blocks may have both. This means our cleaning team needs to be equally comfortable caring for century-old heart pine floors and brand-new engineered hardwood, for original clawfoot tubs and frameless glass showers, for plaster walls and modern drywall. That versatility is exactly what we bring to every Seminole Heights home we clean.

3.7 Frequently Asked Questions
  SECTION ID: #faq  
H2 Heading
House Cleaning Questions from Seminole Heights Residents
Q1: Do you serve all of Seminole Heights?
Yes. We clean homes in Old Seminole Heights, South Seminole Heights, Southeast Seminole Heights, Hampton Terrace, and all surrounding streets within the 33603 and 33604 zip codes. We also serve bordering neighborhoods including Riverside Heights and Tampa Heights. Call (813) 534-9216 to confirm.
Q2: How much does house cleaning cost in Seminole Heights?
Regular cleaning typically ranges from $110 to $240 per visit depending on home size and frequency. Many Seminole Heights bungalows are 2 to 3 bedrooms with 1 to 2 bathrooms, which generally falls in the $120 to $180 range for biweekly service. Larger restored homes or new-construction infill may run higher.
Q3: Do you have experience cleaning original hardwood floors?
Yes, and this is one of the most important things we do in Seminole Heights. Original heart pine and oak hardwood floors require pH-neutral cleaners and careful technique to protect the wood and its finish. We never use vinegar, ammonia, or steam on hardwood – all of which can damage the wood over time. Our approach preserves the patina and character that makes these floors the centerpiece of so many Heights bungalows.
Q4: Can you clean both restored bungalows and new-construction homes?
Absolutely. Seminole Heights is a mix of fully original 1920s homes, extensively renovated mid-century properties, and brand-new infill construction. Our team is equally comfortable with each. We adjust our products and techniques based on the surfaces in your specific home – whether that means century-old plaster and heart pine or modern drywall and engineered hardwood.
Q5: Do you clean the restaurants and shops along Florida Avenue?
Yes. We offer commercial cleaning for the independent businesses that give Seminole Heights its character. Restaurants, cafes, breweries, boutiques, and studios along Florida Avenue and Nebraska Avenue are all within our scope. We schedule around your business hours.
