1. Page Strategy & Keyword Targets
1.1 Page Purpose
This page targets South Tampa residents who search for cleaning services using their neighborhood name. Local search queries like "house cleaning South Tampa" or "cleaning service near Hyde Park Tampa" carry extremely high commercial intent – these people are looking for a provider in their specific area and are ready to hire. By creating a dedicated page for South Tampa, Easy Clean Service can rank for these geo-modified keywords that the homepage and main service pages cannot individually target.

6. Template Replication Guide for Future Neighborhoods
1. Copy the WordPress page template from the South Tampa page.
2. Update the URL slug to the new neighborhood (e.g., /service-areas/westchase/).
3. Replace all [TEMPLATE] section content: title tag, meta description, H1, H2 headings, hero subheading, CTA button text, schema AreaServed, and form submit button text.
4. Write 300–500 words of unique neighborhood-specific content (Section 3.2 equivalent) covering that area’s home types, notable sub-neighborhoods, local landmarks, and specific cleaning challenges.
5. List the sub-neighborhoods served (Section 3.3 equivalent). Research each area to compile the correct list.
6. Write 4–6 FAQ answers with at least 2 that are specific to the neighborhood (home styles, pricing notes, local cleaning challenges).
7. Source 1–2 locally recognizable images for the hero and neighborhood content sections.
8. Update the Nearby Areas links to reference geographically adjacent neighborhoods.
9. Validate all schema at validator.schema.org before publishing.
10. Update cross-links: After publishing, go back to the adjacent neighborhood pages and add a link to the new page in their Nearby Areas sections.
Content Uniqueness Requirement
Each neighborhood page MUST have at least 60% unique body copy to avoid thin/duplicate content penalties. The sections that change between neighborhoods are: the hero subheading (Section 3.1), the neighborhood-specific content (Section 3.2), the sub-neighborhood list (Section 3.3), and at least 2 FAQ answers (Section 3.7). The sections that use template language but swap neighborhood names are: the services section (3.4), the trust section (3.5), the how-it-works section (3.6), and the estimate form (3.8).
WARNING: Do NOT simply find-and-replace "South Tampa" with the new neighborhood name and publish. Google’s algorithms specifically detect and penalize doorway pages that use this approach. The unique neighborhood content sections must contain genuinely original information about each area’s housing stock, demographics, local landmarks, and specific cleaning challenges.
