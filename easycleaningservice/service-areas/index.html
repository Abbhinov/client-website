House Cleaning Service Areas Across Tampa, FL

Easy Clean Service proudly cleans homes and businesses across the greater Tampa Bay area. From the historic bungalows of Seminole Heights to the luxury waterfront homes of Hyde Park, from the master-planned communities of Wesley Chapel to the established family neighborhoods of Brandon – we currently serve 12 neighborhoods across Hillsborough and Pasco Counties, covering over 150 distinct sub-communities and every major zip code in the Tampa metropolitan area.

Cleaning Services Across 12 Tampa-Area Neighborhoods

Easy Clean Service covers a service area that spans from Wesley Chapel in the north to Riverview in the south, and from the Gulf-adjacent communities of Westchase and Town ’N’ Country in the west to Brandon and Temple Terrace in the east. Our footprint includes both Hillsborough County (where 11 of our 12 neighborhood pages are located) and Pasco County (Wesley Chapel), giving us reach across the two largest counties in the Tampa Bay metropolitan area.

Across these 12 neighborhoods, we serve over 150 distinct sub-communities, gated developments, and residential areas. Our team cleans homes ranging from 700-square-foot apartments near the University of South Florida to 6,000-square-foot estates in Lutz’s gated communities, and everything in between. We adjust our approach, our timing, and our products based on each neighborhood’s specific housing stock, climate challenges, and homeowner expectations.

If your neighborhood is not listed below, call us at (813) 534-9216 – we may still be able to serve your area, and we are actively expanding our coverage.
