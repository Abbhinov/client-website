Why Cleanliness Is the Most Important Factor in Your Tampa Rental's Success

Cleanliness is consistently the number one factor guests mention in reviews, and it is the single most common reason for negative ratings on Airbnb and VRBO. A property can have a stunning location, beautiful decor, and top-tier amenities, but one dirty bathroom or a stained pillowcase can erase all of that in a guest's mind. On platforms where listings live and die by their star rating, a drop from 4.8 to 4.5 can push your property off the first page of search results and significantly reduce your booking rate.

In Tampa's competitive short-term rental market - with properties across South Tampa, Ybor City, Channelside, and the waterfront competing for the same guests - you cannot afford inconsistent cleaning. Every turnover must meet the same standard. Every guest must walk into a space that feels fresh, sanitized, and hotel-level clean. That's exactly what Easy Clean Service delivers.

What Every Turnover Cleaning Includes

Our turnover cleaning goes beyond basic tidying. We follow a detailed checklist on every visit to make sure nothing is missed and every guest has the same flawless first impression. Here is exactly what we do between check-out and check-in:

  • Wash, dry, and put away all dishes, utensils, and cookware
  • Clean and sanitize countertops, backsplash, and sink
  • Wipe exterior and interior of microwave
  • Wipe exterior of refrigerator, oven, and dishwasher
  • Clean stovetop surface and drip pans
  • Check refrigerator and dispose of any food or beverages left by guests
  • Wipe cabinet fronts and handles
  • Empty trash, replace liner, and take bag to collection area
  • Sweep and mop floor
  • Restock paper towels, dish soap, and trash bags per host instructions
  • Scrub and disinfect toilet inside and out
  • Clean bathtub, shower walls, and glass door
  • Scrub sink, polish faucet, and wipe countertop
  • Clean mirror streak-free
  • Remove used towels and replace with fresh linens
  • Restock toilet paper, hand soap, shampoo, conditioner, and body wash
  • Empty trash and replace liner
  • Sweep and mop floor
  • Check for mold or mildew and spot-treat if present
  • Strip all bed linens including sheets, pillowcases, and duvet covers
  • Remake beds with fresh, clean linens and stage with decorative pillows
  • Dust all surfaces including nightstands, dressers, and headboard
  • Check under beds and behind furniture for forgotten guest items
  • Vacuum carpet or sweep and mop hard floors
  • Empty closets of any guest belongings and check hangers
  • Empty trash cans
  • Dust all surfaces including shelves, tables, and entertainment centers
  • Vacuum sofas and under cushions (check for lost items)
  • Vacuum carpet or sweep and mop hard floors
  • Clean glass surfaces, mirrors, and TV screen
  • Straighten all furniture, cushions, and decorative items to staging standards
  • Dust blinds and wipe window sills
  • Remove cobwebs from ceiling corners
  • Sweep patio, balcony, or lanai
  • Wipe down outdoor furniture and table
  • Empty and clean outdoor ashtrays if present
  • Check pool area for towels or guest items (if private pool)
  • Ensure outdoor lights are functional
  • Test all lights and replace any burned-out bulbs
  • Check thermostat and set to host-specified temperature
  • Verify all doors and windows are locked
  • Run a final walkthrough to confirm staging matches listing photos
  • Report any guest damage, missing items, or maintenance issues to host

Turnover Services That Go Beyond the Mop and Bucket

Your guest expects hotel-level cleanliness and staging - and your business needs consistent, repeatable results. We handle the details that protect your ratings and reduce surprises between stays.

  • Linen Service: We strip all beds, remove used towels, launder everything or swap in fresh sets (based on your preference), and remake beds to hotel-level staging standards with crisp sheets and neatly folded towels.
  • Supply Restocking: We monitor and replenish guest essentials based on your custom list - toilet paper, paper towels, dish soap, trash bags, coffee pods, shampoo, conditioner, and body wash. We notify you when stock is running low so you can resupply before it runs out.
  • Damage & Inventory Reporting: After every turnover, we do a walkthrough check of your property and notify you immediately if we spot any guest damage, missing items, broken fixtures, or maintenance issues that need attention before the next guest arrives.
  • Staging to Listing Photos: We position pillows, throws, decor items, and amenities to match the layout shown in your listing photos. Consistency between what guests see online and what they find on arrival is essential for positive reviews.
  • Flexible Same-Day Turnarounds: We understand that check-out and check-in sometimes happen on the same day with only a few hours in between. We work with your booking calendar to schedule turnovers with the speed your operation demands.
