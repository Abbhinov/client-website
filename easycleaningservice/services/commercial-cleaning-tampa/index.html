What Every Commercial Cleaning Visit Covers

Every commercial cleaning visit follows a structured checklist customized to your facility. The following tasks represent our standard scope for a typical Tampa office or commercial space. We can add or remove tasks based on your specific needs and priorities.

Reception & common areas
  • Dust and wipe all surfaces including reception desks, waiting area tables, and shelving
  • Vacuum carpet or sweep and mop hard floors throughout all common areas
  • Clean glass entry doors, sidelights, and interior glass partitions
  • Empty all trash and recycling bins and replace liners
  • Wipe and sanitize high-touch surfaces: door handles, elevator buttons, light switches, handrails
  • Dust window sills and blinds
  • Spot-clean walls and doors for scuffs, fingerprints, and marks
  • Straighten furniture and organize waiting area materials
Open office & workstations
  • Dust desks, chairs, file cabinets, bookshelves, and credenzas
  • Wipe phone handsets, keyboard surfaces, and mouse surfaces (exterior only)
  • Clean and organize conference tables and chairs after meetings
  • Vacuum carpet including edges and under desks
  • Empty individual trash cans and shred bins
  • Clean whiteboard trays and wipe presentation equipment surfaces
  • Dust picture frames, awards, and decorative items
Break room & kitchen
  • Wipe and sanitize countertops, table surfaces, and sink
  • Clean exterior and interior of microwave
  • Wipe exterior of refrigerator, coffee maker, and other shared appliances
  • Clean inside refrigerator on a scheduled basis (recommend monthly)
  • Empty trash and recycling, replace liners
  • Sweep and mop floor
  • Restock paper towels and dish soap (supplies provided by client or available for purchase)
Restrooms
  • Scrub and disinfect all toilets and urinals inside and out
  • Clean and sanitize sinks, faucets, and countertops
  • Clean and polish mirrors streak-free
  • Restock toilet paper, paper towels, hand soap, and sanitizer
  • Empty sanitary disposal bins
  • Wipe and sanitize partitions, door handles, and light switches
  • Sweep and mop floor with commercial disinfectant
  • Check and report any plumbing issues or supply shortages
