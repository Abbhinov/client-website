Move-Out Cleaning: Leave Your Tampa Rental Spotless and Protect Your Deposit

Your lease likely requires you to return the property in clean, move-in-ready condition. Failing to meet that standard can cost you hundreds of dollars from your security deposit – or even lead to additional charges. Our move-out cleaning service is specifically designed to meet and exceed landlord expectations in the Tampa rental market. We clean every area that property managers and landlords inspect, from the inside of kitchen appliances to the tracks of sliding glass doors.

We have cleaned hundreds of Tampa apartments, condos, townhouses, and single-family rentals for tenants, property management companies, and realtors. We know what inspectors look for because we have seen the checklists they use. When we leave, the property is ready to hand over with confidence.

Kitchen & Appliance Checklist
  • Clean and degrease stovetop, burner grates, and range hood
  • Clean inside oven including racks, walls, and door glass
  • Clean inside microwave including turntable
  • Clean inside refrigerator including shelves, drawers, and door seals
  • Clean inside dishwasher and wipe door and edges
  • Sanitize all countertops and backsplash
  • Scrub sink basin, polish faucet and disposal splash guard
  • Wipe inside and outside of all cabinets and drawers
  • Clean light switch plates, outlet covers, and door handles
  • Sweep and mop floors including behind where appliances were
Bathrooms & Fixture Checklist
  • Scrub and disinfect toilet completely including base, hinges, and tank exterior
  • Deep-scrub bathtub, shower walls, door or curtain rod, and tile grout
  • Remove hard water stains and soap scum from all surfaces
  • Clean and polish sink, faucet, and all chrome fixtures
  • Clean mirror and medicine cabinet inside and out
  • Wipe inside all vanity cabinets and drawers
  • Clean exhaust fan vent cover
  • Scrub floor on hands and knees including behind toilet and along edges
Floors, Closets & Final Details
  • Dust all surfaces, shelving, and built-in fixtures
  • Clean inside all closets including shelves and rods
  • Wipe window sills, blinds, and window tracks
  • Clean light fixtures and ceiling fan blades
  • Wipe baseboards, door frames, and light switch plates
  • Vacuum carpet or sweep and mop hard floors including edges and inside closets
  • Dust all surfaces and built-in shelving
  • Clean all window sills, blinds, and accessible window tracks
  • Wipe all baseboards throughout the entire home
  • Clean all door frames, door handles, and light switch plates
  • Remove cobwebs from ceiling corners
  • Vacuum all carpet or sweep and mop all hard floors edge to edge
  • Clean sliding glass door tracks if applicable
  • Spot-clean walls for scuff marks and handprints
  • Clean interior side of entry door

Move-In Cleaning: Start Fresh in Your New Tampa Home

Even if the previous occupants had the property cleaned before they left, you have no way of knowing how thorough that cleaning actually was. Dust settles in empty rooms. Cabinets and drawers that sat open during showings collect debris. Appliances that look clean on the outside may harbor grime on the inside. A professional move-in cleaning gives you the peace of mind of knowing that every surface your family will touch has been properly cleaned and sanitized before you unpack your first box.

Our move-in cleaning covers the same comprehensive checklist as our move-out service. We clean inside cabinets and closets so your belongings go onto fresh shelves. We sanitize bathrooms and kitchens so you are cooking and bathing in a space that meets your standards, not the last tenant’s. We detail baseboards, window tracks, and light fixtures so the home truly feels like yours from day one.

This service is also ideal for new construction homes and recently renovated properties where construction dust, drywall residue, and paint splatter may be present on surfaces that look finished but are not actually clean.

How Professional Move-Out Cleaning Helps You Get Your Full Deposit Back

In Florida, landlords have 15 to 60 days after you vacate to return your security deposit or provide an itemized list of deductions. Cleaning charges are among the most common reasons tenants lose part or all of their deposit. The cost of a professional move-out cleaning is almost always less than the amount a landlord would deduct for cleaning – and significantly less than what they would charge if they hire their own cleaning crew and pass the bill to you.

By hiring Easy Clean Service to handle your move-out cleaning, you ensure the property is returned in a condition that leaves no room for cleaning-related deductions. We clean to landlord inspection standards because we know exactly what they check. Many of our customers send photos of our work to their property managers and receive their full deposit back without any pushback.
