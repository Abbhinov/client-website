What Every Regular Cleaning Visit Covers

Our regular cleaning service is designed to keep your entire home maintained at a consistently high standard. Every visit follows the same thorough routine, so you always know exactly what to expect when our team walks through the door. We bring all supplies and equipment – you do not need to provide anything.

Kitchen
  • Wipe and sanitize all countertops and backsplash areas
  • Clean and polish sink basin and faucet
  • Wipe exterior of all appliances (refrigerator, oven, microwave, dishwasher)
  • Clean stovetop surface and drip pans
  • Wipe down cabinet fronts and handles
  • Clean interior and exterior of microwave
  • Empty trash and replace liner
  • Sweep and mop floor
Bathrooms
  • Scrub and disinfect toilet inside and out including base and handle
  • Clean and sanitize bathtub, shower walls, and shower door
  • Scrub sink basin and polish faucet and hardware
  • Clean and streak-free mirrors
  • Wipe countertops and vanity surfaces
  • Empty trash and replace liner
  • Sweep and mop floor
Bedrooms
  • Dust all accessible surfaces (nightstands, dressers, shelves, headboard)
  • Make beds and straighten linens
  • Vacuum carpet or sweep and mop hard floors
  • Empty small trash cans
Living areas
  • Dust all accessible surfaces including shelves, entertainment centers, and tables
  • Dust lampshades and picture frames
  • Vacuum carpet, rugs, and upholstered furniture surfaces
  • Sweep and mop hard floors
  • Straighten cushions and light tidying of visible areas
  • Empty trash cans
Whole home
  • Dust all accessible surfaces top to bottom
  • Vacuum all carpeted areas and rugs
  • Sweep and mop all hard floors
  • Wipe light switches and door handles in high-traffic areas
  • Spot-clean visible marks on walls and doors
  • Cobweb removal from accessible ceiling corners

How Often Should You Have Your Tampa Home Cleaned?

There is no one-size-fits-all answer, and the right schedule depends on your household’s lifestyle. The good news is that you can switch frequencies at any time with no penalty or contract. Here is a general guide to help you decide:

Frequency Best For What You Get Cost Savings
Weekly Busy families with kids and pets, allergy sufferers, households where someone works from home, entertaining regularly Highest level of consistency. Home always feels guest-ready. Dust, pet hair, and kitchen buildup never have time to accumulate. Lowest per-visit rate. Best overall value for busy households.
Biweekly Dual-income couples, smaller households, homeowners who handle light tidying between visits Our most popular schedule. Balances a consistently clean home with a manageable budget. Enough frequency to stay ahead of Tampa humidity and allergens. Strong value. Most customers choose this option.
Monthly Individuals or couples with minimal daily mess, seasonal residents, vacation homes between guests A reliable monthly refresh that covers all the rooms and resets your home. More thorough than a quick weekly visit since more buildup occurs between appointments. Lowest total monthly cost. Good for lighter households.

Not sure which schedule works best? Start with biweekly – it is our most popular option for a reason. After a few visits, you will have a good sense of whether your home needs more or less frequent attention. You can increase to weekly or dial back to monthly at any point. We never lock you into a contract, so adjusting your schedule is always one phone call away.

One important note: if your home has not been professionally cleaned recently, we recommend starting with a one-time deep cleaning before transitioning to a regular schedule. Why Recurring Cleaning Is Worth It for Tampa Homes

  • Consistency You Can Count On: Your home stays at a reliable standard week after week. No more scrambling before guests arrive or spending your Saturday scrubbing bathrooms. You always know your next cleaning is on the calendar.
  • Same Trusted Team Every Visit: We assign the same cleaner or team to your home on every visit. They learn your preferences, know where things go, and understand which areas need extra attention. That familiarity translates to better, faster results each time.
  • Better Value Per Visit: Recurring customers receive better per-visit pricing than one-time bookings. The more frequently we clean, the less time each visit takes because there is less buildup between appointments – and that savings gets passed on to you.
  • Healthier Home Environment: Tampa’s humidity, pollen, and sandy outdoor conditions mean dust, allergens, and mildew build up quickly. Regular professional cleaning keeps these under control so your household breathes easier – literally.
  • Total Flexibility, Zero Commitment: No contracts, no cancellation fees. Pause when you go on vacation, skip a visit when family is in town, or change your frequency anytime. The service works around your life, not the other way around.
  • Protect Your Home’s Value: Regular cleaning prevents the kind of grime buildup that causes long-term damage – stained grout, deteriorating caulk, scratched floors from embedded dirt, and mildew that eats into surfaces. It is an investment in the longevity of your home.
