## Users

Primary visitors are people near 6043 W Addison St in Chicago's 60634 area who need a reliable laundromat, wash and fold, pickup and delivery, or large machines for oversized household items. ## Product Purpose

Laundry Station's website should make the store feel dependable again: serviced machines, posted prices, and done-for-you laundry options. The homepage is the front door, routing visitors to service pages, pricing, FAQ, contact, and booking.

## Positioning

Fixed. Posted. Done for you. The site answers the old reputation problem with concrete operating facts instead of apologies, ratings, discount claims, or "under new management" language.

## Operating Context

The site is a local SEO and AEO build for Laundry Station at 6043 W Addison St, Chicago, IL 60634. It is planned as an English and Spanish static site with service pages, a pricing page called The Board, area support through copy/schema rather than a launch Areas silo, FAQ, contact, legal pages, and resource content.

## Product Principles

Reliability is the throughline: repaired, serviced, and working beats vague claims.

Posted prices matter more than discount language.

Answer-first copy supports local search and AI answer engines.

Spanish is a native site, not a machine translation.

Every claim must be verifiable or left unresolved.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The design system requires AA contrast, visible focus, 44px minimum tap targets, semantic HTML, real tables for tabular data, native FAQ disclosure controls, and layouts that survive 320px mobile widths without horizontal overflow.
