1. How to Use This Guide
This developer guide provides the complete implementation specification for the About page (/about/). This is a trust-building page — its conversion role is indirect. Visitors land here to learn who’s behind the business before deciding to use it. The page tells the family-business story, showcases the facility investment, and connects Spin It Up to the Avondale community.
The About page doesn’t have a dedicated form or direct conversion CTA. Its job is to build enough trust that the visitor proceeds to a service page, the contact page, or calls directly. The final CTA block routes them there.
Content Note
NOTE:  The story content in this guide is written as a plausible family-business narrative based on the audit data (family-owned, Avondale community, facility investment in new machines). The client MUST review and revise the story sections to reflect the actual owner’s history, motivations, and family background. Placeholder names and specific biographical details should be replaced with real information before publication.
Content Authoring Rules
All content written fresh.
Story content must be client-reviewed and revised to reflect real history.
Photos must be real — not stock. Owner, family, staff, facility.
Tone: warm, genuine, community-oriented. Not corporate. Not salesy.
