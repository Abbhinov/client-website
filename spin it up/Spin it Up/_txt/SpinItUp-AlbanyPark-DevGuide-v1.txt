This guide covers the Albany Park area landing page (/areas/albany-park/). Albany Park is the extended delivery zone — the farthest neighborhood from our facility (~2.5 miles). The page must clearly communicate delivery availability while being transparent that walk-in visits require a slightly longer trip. The page also highlights Albany Park’s extraordinary cultural diversity, which is directly relevant to our bilingual service commitment.

13. Section 9: FAQ Section
H2: "Albany Park Laundry FAQ" — Centered. Font: 32px/700.

#
Question
Answer
1
Do you deliver to Albany Park?
Yes. Albany Park is in our extended delivery zone. Most Albany Park addresses are covered. Enter your address in the Cents app to confirm delivery availability for your specific location. If your address falls outside our current zone, call us at (773) 654-1275 — we may be able to arrange delivery.
2
How far is Spin It Up from Albany Park?
Our facility at 3710 N Kimball Ave in Avondale is approximately 2.5 miles south of central Albany Park. That’s about an 8-minute drive via Kimball Ave or Kedzie Ave. The CTA #82 Kimball bus runs directly between Albany Park and our location.
3
Do you have staff who speak Spanish?
Yes. Se habla español. Our attendants can assist Spanish-speaking customers at our facility. Our website will also have a Spanish-language version in a future update.
4
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept cash, credit cards, debit cards, EBT (for self-service washing and drying), and our reloadable laundry card. Pickup and delivery orders are paid via credit or debit card through the Cents app.
