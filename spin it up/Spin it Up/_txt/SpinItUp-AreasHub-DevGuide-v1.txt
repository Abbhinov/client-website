1. How to Use This Guide
This guide covers the Service Areas Hub page (/areas/). It is the parent page of the Areas silo, linking down to individual neighborhood child pages. Its primary job is geographic SEO coverage — establishing that Spin It Up serves specific neighborhoods on Chicago’s Northwest Side — and routing visitors to their neighborhood’s dedicated page.
The page is deliberately concise (400–600 words). Detailed neighborhood content lives on the child pages. This hub provides the map, the links, and the AEO anchor for “laundromat near me” queries with geographic context.
