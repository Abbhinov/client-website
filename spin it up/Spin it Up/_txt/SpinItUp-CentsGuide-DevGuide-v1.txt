10. Section 6: How to Schedule Your First Pickup
H2: "Step 2: Schedule Your First Pickup"
This is the HowTo schema section. Each step maps to a HowTo step in the JSON-LD.

Step
Title
Instruction
1
Enter Your Address
Open the Cents app and enter your pickup address. The app will confirm whether you’re in Spin It Up’s delivery zone (Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, Albany Park).
2
Choose a Pickup Window
Select a date and time window for pickup. We offer windows throughout the day, seven days a week. Pick the time that works best for your schedule.
3
Add Special Instructions
Note any preferences: stain locations, fabric care requests, detergent preferences (fragrance-free, hypoallergenic), or sorting instructions. This step is optional but helps us give you the best results.
4
Review Your Order
The app shows your estimated price based on the per-pound rate ($1.75/lb standard, $2.25/lb express) plus the $5.00 pickup fee. Confirm everything looks right.
5
Place Your Order
Tap "Confirm Order." Your payment method is charged after your laundry is weighed at our facility — not at the time of ordering. The app estimate is based on typical load weight.
6
Leave Your Bag Out
Place your laundry bag at your door, in your building lobby, or at the designated pickup spot before your window starts. You don’t need to be home — the driver will collect it. You’ll receive a notification when pickup is complete.
"That’s it. Your laundry is now in our hands. We’ll wash, dry, and fold everything and deliver it back within 24 hours (or same day for express orders placed before 10 AM)."

12. Section 8: Tips & Troubleshooting
H2: "Tips for the Best Experience"

Tip
Detail
Use a sturdy bag
A drawstring laundry bag or large garbage bag works well. Make sure it’s sealed so nothing falls out during pickup.
Note stain locations
If you have items with stains, mention them in the special instructions. We treat all visible stains, but a heads-up helps us catch everything.
Set up recurring pickups
If you order weekly, use the app’s recurring pickup feature to schedule automatically. One less thing to remember.
Check your delivery area
Albany Park is our extended zone. If you’re at the edge of our area, the app will let you know at checkout.
Contact us if anything’s off
If your order has any issues, call (773) 654-1275 or message us through the app. We’ll make it right.
Troubleshooting
Issue
Solution
App says my address isn’t in the delivery zone
Try the exact address format (include apartment number). If it still doesn’t work, call (773) 654-1275 — we may be able to arrange delivery.
Payment method declined
Verify your card details. The app accepts Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and Discover. Prepaid cards may not work.
I missed the pickup window
Reschedule through the app for the next available window. No cancellation fee.
I need to cancel my order
Cancel through the app before the driver departs. No charge if cancelled before pickup.

14. Section 10: FAQ Section
H2: "Cents App FAQ"

#
Question
Answer
1
Is the Cents app free?
Yes. The Cents app is free to download and free to create an account. You only pay when you place a laundry order.
2
Can I order without downloading the app?
Yes. You can place orders through the Cents website on any browser. Go to [CENTS_WEB_URL] and follow the same steps. The website works on desktop and mobile.
3
How is the price calculated?
Your order is priced by weight. Standard pickup & delivery is $1.75 per pound with a $5.00 pickup fee. Your laundry is weighed at our facility after pickup, and the final charge is applied to your payment method at that time.
4
What if I want to switch to drop-off instead?
You can cancel your pickup order through the app (before the driver departs) and visit our facility at 3710 N Kimball Ave for drop-off wash-and-fold at $1.50 per pound instead.
