1. How to Use This Guide
This developer guide provides the complete implementation specification for the Commercial Laundry Services page (/services/commercial/). This page targets business customers — Airbnb hosts, salons, gyms, restaurants, property managers, and small hotels — who need reliable, high-volume laundry on a recurring schedule. The audit identified the existing Commercial Services page as better than 80% of competitors but undermined by Gmail as the business email and a confusing .com domain. This rebuilt page resolves those issues and expands the content to convert commercial leads.
Commercial visitors have different decision criteria than residential customers: they care about reliability, turnaround SLAs, volume pricing, and professional credibility. The page tone is accordingly more B2B-oriented while maintaining Spin It Up’s friendly brand voice.
Content Authoring Rules
All content written fresh. No content from the current site or competitors.
AEO opening paragraph within the first 100–150 words answers “commercial laundry service in Chicago.”
Phone number and email render as clickable links (tel: and mailto:).
This page includes a dedicated commercial inquiry form — not just the generic contact form.

15. Section 11: FAQ Section
Section Overview
Commercial-specific FAQ. Addresses B2B concerns: contracts, billing, volume thresholds, turnaround guarantees, and insurance. FAQPage schema required. Background: White (#FFFFFF).
Content Specifications
H2: "Commercial Laundry FAQ" — 3 words. Centered. Font: 32px/700. Margin-bottom 40px.

#
Question
Answer
1
Is there a minimum volume for commercial accounts?
There is no strict minimum, but volume-based pricing discounts begin at approximately 50 lb per week. Orders below that threshold are processed at our standard wash-and-fold rates ($1.50–$1.75/lb), which are still competitive for small businesses.
2
Do I need to sign a contract?
No. All commercial accounts operate on a month-to-month basis. There are no long-term contracts, cancellation fees, or setup charges. Service continues for as long as it works for your business.
3
How fast is turnaround for commercial orders?
Standard commercial turnaround is 24 hours from drop-off or pickup. For accounts on a recurring schedule, we build your laundry into our daily production workflow so it is ready at the same time each cycle. Rush turnaround is available by arrangement.
4
Do you pick up and deliver for commercial accounts?
Yes. Pickup and delivery is available for all commercial accounts within our service area (Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, Albany Park). We can arrange scheduled recurring pickups or on-demand pickups based on your needs.
5
How do you handle stains and heavily soiled items?
We pre-treat all visible stains before washing using commercial-grade stain removers. For restaurant accounts, we use high-temperature wash cycles and grease-cutting detergents. If an item cannot be fully cleaned, we will notify you before returning it.
6
How does billing work?
Commercial accounts receive monthly invoices based on actual volume processed. Invoices include itemized details by date and weight. Payment is accepted via credit card, ACH transfer, or check. We can accommodate your preferred billing format.
7
Can you match my Airbnb check-in schedule?
Yes. Many of our Airbnb host clients have back-to-back bookings with tight turnover windows. We prioritize turnover loads and can offer expedited turnaround for linen sets that need to be guest-ready by a specific time. Contact us to discuss your booking schedule.
