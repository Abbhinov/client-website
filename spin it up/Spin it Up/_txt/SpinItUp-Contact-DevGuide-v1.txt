1. How to Use This Guide
This developer guide provides the complete implementation specification for the Contact page (/contact/). This is a conversion page — the strictest LCP target (<1.5s), minimal content, form-first design. The page exists to convert visitors into leads via form submission, phone call, or directions click. Every design decision prioritizes reducing friction between intent and action.
The contact page is intentionally short (300–400 words). Visitors who arrive here have already decided to reach out — the page’s job is to make that as easy as possible, not to sell them on the service. Trust reinforcement is present but secondary to the form.
Content Authoring Rules
All content written fresh.
Form-first layout: the form is the most prominent element on the page.
LCP <1.5s: no hero images, minimal CSS, no heavy assets above the fold.
All contact methods clickable: tel: for phone, mailto: for email, Google Maps link for address.

Form UX Specifications

Field styling: height 48px (text/email/tel/select), border 1px #E2E8F0, border-radius 8px, padding 12px 16px. Focus: border-color #1E40AF + box-shadow 0 0 0 3px rgba(30,64,175,0.15).

Success state: form fields replaced with confirmation message (not a redirect). “Thank you, [Name]. We’ve received your message and will be in touch shortly.” + phone number as tel: link for immediate needs.
Spam prevention: honeypot hidden field + reCAPTCHA v3 (invisible).
Submission throttle: prevent double-submit on double-click (disable button on first click, re-enable on error).

16px minimum font on all inputs to prevent iOS auto-zoom.
