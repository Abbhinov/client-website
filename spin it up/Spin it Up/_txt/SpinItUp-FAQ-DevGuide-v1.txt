1. How to Use This Guide
This developer guide provides the complete implementation specification for the FAQ page (/faq/). This is the site’s comprehensive question-and-answer hub — 18 questions organized into 6 categories. The page serves three purposes: capturing long-tail search queries and voice search traffic, providing answers that reduce friction before conversion, and cross-linking visitors to the relevant service pages.
Each service child page has its own 5–7 question FAQ section. This page consolidates the most important questions from across the site plus additional questions that don’t fit on individual service pages. There is intentional overlap between this page and the per-page FAQs — the same question can appear in both places because users land on different pages. The FAQ schema on this page covers all 18 questions.
Content Authoring Rules
All content written fresh.
Every answer includes a direct, extractable first sentence before elaboration.
Answers that reference a specific service link to that service’s page.
Phone number in any answer renders as a tel: link.

What is the largest washer you have?
Our largest machines hold up to 130 pounds — the biggest washers in the Avondale area. Can I wash a king-size comforter?
Yes. Our extra-large (60–80 lb) and commercial (130 lb) washers handle king-size comforters easily. We recommend the extra-large machine for most comforters and the commercial machine for thick, heavy king-size comforters or if you want to wash the comforter with pillows and sheets in the same load. How long does wash and fold take?
Standard turnaround is next day. If you drop off before 10:00 AM, same-day service is available at the $2.00 per pound rate. We call or text you when your order is ready for pickup. If you cannot pick up on the expected day, we hold your order for up to 7 days at no additional charge.

Do you separate colors and whites?
Yes. Our attendants sort every order by color, fabric type, and washing temperature before loading. Whites, darks, and colors are washed separately. Delicate items are identified and handled with appropriate care. Your laundry is never mixed with another customer’s clothes.

Yes. Pre-wash stain treatment is included on every wash-and-fold and pickup-and-delivery order at no extra charge. We use commercial-grade stain removers appropriate for each fabric type. If a stain cannot be fully removed, we will let you know when you pick up.

How does laundry pickup and delivery work?
Download the free Cents app on your phone, enter your address, select a pickup window, and confirm your order. Our driver picks up your laundry from your door during the selected time. We wash, dry, and fold everything at our facility. Your clean laundry is delivered back to your door, typically within 24 hours. You do not need to be home for pickup or delivery.

We currently deliver to Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, and Albany Park on Chicago’s Northwest Side. The Cents app confirms whether your address is in our delivery zone when you enter it. If you are nearby but outside the listed areas, call us at (773) 654-1275 — we may be able to arrange delivery.

Yes. The Cents app allows you to set up recurring pickups on a weekly or biweekly schedule. Your address, preferences, and payment method are saved so reordering takes a single tap. You can pause or cancel anytime without fees.

How much does wash and fold cost?
Standard wash-and-fold pricing starts at $1.50 per pound with a 10 lb minimum. Same-day service is $2.00 per pound for orders dropped off before 10:00 AM.
