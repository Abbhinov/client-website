1. How to Use This Guide
This developer guide provides the complete implementation specification for the Pickup & Delivery page (/services/pickup-delivery/). This page was identified in the April 2026 audit as a critical missing asset — Spin It Up’s highest-value service (Cents app-powered pickup and delivery) had no dedicated information page. Visitors were expected to commit to an order without first understanding how the service works, what it costs, or where it delivers.
This page resolves that gap entirely. It is the longest service child page in the site (1,000–1,200 words) because it must educate visitors on a service they may never have used before, build trust in a door-to-door process, explain the Cents app, and convert them to their first order.
This guide references the Website Strategy & Architecture Document (v1.0) for brand system and design tokens, and prior developer guides for shared component specifications.
Content Authoring Rules
All content written fresh. No content from the current GoDaddy site or competitor sites.
AEO opening paragraph within the first 100–150 words directly answers “laundry pickup and delivery in Chicago.”
All phone numbers render as clickable tel: links.
All “Schedule Pickup” CTAs link to the Cents app ordering URL.

15. Section 11: FAQ Section
Section Overview
Pickup-and-delivery-specific FAQ. These questions are the ones the audit identified as unanswered on the current site: turnaround, minimums, what happens if you’re not home, stain handling, etc. FAQPage schema required. Background: Ice Blue (#F0F9FF).
Content Specifications
H2: "Pickup & Delivery FAQ" — 4 words. Centered. Font: 32px/700, Dark Slate. Margin-bottom 40px.

#
Question
Answer
1
How does laundry pickup and delivery work?
Download the Cents app (free on iOS and Android), enter your address, choose a pickup time, and confirm your order. Our driver picks up your laundry from your door during the selected window. We wash, dry, and fold everything at our facility at 3710 N Kimball Ave. Your clean laundry is delivered back to your door, typically within 24 hours of pickup.
2
Do I need to be home for pickup?
No. You can leave your laundry bag at your door, in your building lobby, or at any designated spot you specify in the app. Our driver will collect it during your chosen pickup window. You’ll receive a notification when it’s picked up.
3
What neighborhoods do you deliver to?
We currently deliver to Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, and Albany Park. When you enter your address in the Cents app, it will confirm whether you’re in our delivery zone. If you’re nearby but outside the listed areas, call us at (773) 654-1275 — we may be able to arrange delivery.
4
How much does pickup and delivery cost?
Standard pricing is $1.75 per pound with a 10 lb minimum, plus a $5.00 pickup fee per pickup. Express same-day service is $2.25 per pound (order before 10 AM). Comforters are priced at a flat rate. Stain treatment is included at no extra charge. You see the exact price in the Cents app before confirming.
5
How long does pickup and delivery take?
Standard turnaround is 24 hours from pickup to delivery. Express same-day service is available for orders placed before 10:00 AM. You’ll receive notifications at each stage: pickup confirmed, cleaning in progress, and delivery on the way.
6
What if something gets damaged or lost?
Each customer’s laundry is processed individually and never mixed with another customer’s clothes. We take great care with every order. If any issue arises, contact us at (773) 654-1275 or through the Cents app and we will resolve it promptly.
