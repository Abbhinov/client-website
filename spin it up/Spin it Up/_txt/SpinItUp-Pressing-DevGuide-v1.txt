1. How to Use This Guide
This developer guide provides the complete implementation specification for the Ironing & Pressing page (/services/ironing-pressing/). This is the fifth and final service child page in the Services silo. It targets customers who need professional garment finishing — dress shirts, suits, uniforms, and formal wear. The page is shorter than the other service pages (600–800 words) because pressing is a narrower, more straightforward service.
The strategic role of this page is twofold: capture the niche “ironing service” / “pressing service” search queries that competitors ignore, and cross-sell as an add-on to wash-and-fold and commercial orders.
Content Authoring Rules
All content written fresh.
AEO opening paragraph within the first 100–150 words answers “ironing and pressing service in Avondale Chicago.”
All phone numbers as clickable tel: links.
Pricing must be confirmed with client before publication.

13. Section 9: FAQ Section
Section Overview
Pressing-specific FAQ. Shorter than other service FAQs (4 questions) given the narrower service scope. FAQPage schema required. Background: White (#FFFFFF).
Content Specifications
H2: "Ironing & Pressing FAQ" — 4 words. Centered. Font: 32px/700. Margin-bottom 40px.

#
Question
Answer
1
Do I need to wash my clothes before bringing them in for pressing?
Yes. We press clean garments only. If your clothes need washing first, drop them off for our wash-and-fold service and add pressing — we handle both and everything is ready for pickup at the same time. If you bring in soiled garments for pressing, we will let you know they need to be washed first.
2
How long does pressing take?
Standard turnaround is next day. If you drop off before 10:00 AM, ask about same-day availability. We will call or text you when your order is ready. We hold completed orders for up to 7 days.
3
Do you press suits?
Yes. We press suit jackets, blazers, sport coats, and dress pants. A full suit (jacket + pants) is $7.50. Each piece is pressed at the appropriate temperature and shaped on a professional form. Returned on hangers.
4
Do you offer pressing for businesses?
Yes. We provide uniform pressing for restaurants, salons, medical practices, and other businesses. Commercial accounts receive volume pricing. Visit our Commercial Services page or call (773) 654-1275 for a custom quote.
