1. How to Use This Guide
This developer guide provides the complete implementation specification for the Pricing page (/pricing/). This is a standalone page — not part of any silo — that serves as the single source of truth for all Spin It Up Laundry pricing. Every service page links here for full pricing details. The audit identified placeholder text on the current pricing page as a Critical finding; this page replaces that content entirely with complete, structured pricing tables.
This is a high-intent page. Visitors who land here are actively comparing options or preparing to visit. The page must be fast (LCP <1.5s target for conversion pages), scannable, and mobile-optimized with tables that reflow to card layouts.
Content Authoring Rules
All content written fresh. Resolves the Critical audit finding of placeholder text.
AEO opening paragraph within the first 100–150 words answers “how much does laundry cost at Spin It Up.”
All pricing must be confirmed with client before publication. Values in this guide are based on audit data and industry norms.
Phone numbers and Cents app links render as clickable elements.

15. Section 11: FAQ Section
Section Overview
Pricing-specific FAQ. Targets the most common pricing queries that drive traffic to this page. FAQPage schema required. Background: White (#FFFFFF).
Content Specifications
H2: "Pricing FAQ" — 2 words. Centered. Font: 32px/700. Margin-bottom 40px.

#
Question
Answer
1
How much does wash and fold cost per pound?
Standard wash-and-fold pricing at Spin It Up Laundry is $1.50 per pound with a 10 lb minimum. Same-day service is $2.00 per pound for orders dropped off before 10:00 AM. Stain treatment is included on every order at no extra charge.
2
How much does it cost to wash a comforter?
Comforter pricing depends on size: full/twin is $12.00, queen is $15.00, and king is $18.00 for drop-off service. Pickup and delivery pricing is slightly higher to cover the driver cost: full/twin $15.00, queen $18.00, king $22.00.
3
Is drying really free on Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday?
Yes. Every Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday, all dryers are free when you wash with us at Spin It Up Laundry. There is no minimum wash requirement. Wash any load, any size, and dry for free in any dryer from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM.
4
Why is pickup and delivery more expensive than drop-off?
Pickup and delivery includes a $5.00 pickup fee and a per-pound rate of $1.75 (vs. $1.50 for drop-off). The difference covers our driver’s time and fuel to pick up and deliver to your door. The wash-and-fold process itself is identical.
5
Can I use EBT to pay for wash and fold?
EBT is accepted for self-service washing and drying at our facility. Wash-and-fold, pickup and delivery, and commercial services are paid via credit card, debit card, cash, or laundry card.
6
Is there a minimum order for wash and fold?
Yes. The minimum for standard wash-and-fold is 10 pounds (both drop-off and pickup/delivery). Most customers’ laundry bags weigh 12–18 pounds, so the minimum is typically met naturally.
