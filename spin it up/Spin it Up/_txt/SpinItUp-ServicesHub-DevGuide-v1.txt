1. How to Use This Guide
This developer guide provides the complete implementation specification for the Spin It Up Laundry Services Hub page (/services/). This page functions as the top-level gateway to all five service child pages, organized as clickable cards with descriptions and CTAs. Its primary job is click-through to child pages, not direct conversion.
This guide references the Spin It Up Laundry Website Strategy & Architecture Document (v1.0) for brand system, site architecture, and keyword strategy. The strategy document is the authoritative source for design tokens, color palette, typography scale, and component specifications. This guide applies those specifications to the Services Hub page.

11. Section 7: FAQ Section
Section Overview
Accordion FAQ section with service-level questions. These are broader than the homepage FAQ (which covers facility/logistics) — these address service selection and comparison queries. FAQPage schema required. Background: Ice Blue (#F0F9FF).

#
Question
Answer
1
What is the difference between wash-and-fold and pickup and delivery?
Wash-and-fold is a drop-off service: you bring your laundry to our location at 3710 N Kimball Ave, and we wash, dry, and fold it for you to pick up later — typically within 24 hours. Pickup and delivery adds door-to-door convenience: you schedule through the Cents app, and our driver picks up and delivers your laundry to your home or office. Both services use the same professional wash process and include stain treatment.
2
What is the largest load you can wash?
Our largest machines hold up to 130 pounds, making them the biggest washers in the Avondale area. These commercial-capacity machines handle king-size comforters, sleeping bags, large blankets, restaurant linens, and commercial bulk loads that standard laundromat machines cannot fit.
3
Do you offer same-day laundry service?
For wash-and-fold drop-off, most orders dropped off before 10:00 AM are ready for same-day pickup by closing time. Standard turnaround is next-day. Pickup and delivery orders are typically completed within 24 hours of pickup. Contact us at (773) 654-1275 for rush service availability.
4
How does commercial laundry pricing work?
Commercial accounts receive volume-based pricing tailored to your business needs. Pricing depends on load frequency, volume, and service type. Airbnb hosts, salons, gyms, and restaurants typically save 15–25% compared to standard per-pound rates. Contact us for a custom quote.
5
Can I use EBT for wash-and-fold or pickup service?
EBT is accepted for self-service washing and drying at our facility. Wash-and-fold, pickup and delivery, and commercial services are paid via credit card, debit card, or cash. We are one of the few laundromats in Avondale that accepts EBT for any service.
