1. Executive Summary
Spin It Up Laundry at 3710 N Kimball Ave operates one of the best-equipped independent laundromats in Chicago’s Avondale neighborhood — 41 washers, 52 dryers, 130 lb commercial-capacity machines, Cents app integration for online ordering, EBT acceptance, free WiFi, free coffee, gaming machines, and active weekly promotions including free-drying specials. The facility is genuinely competitive with chain operations on amenities and capacity. The digital presence is not.
The April 2026 audit scored the current website at 53/100 (Needs Improvement). Critical issues include a broken SSL certificate, placeholder text on the pricing page, no structured data, no Spanish-language content despite serving one of Chicago’s most heavily Latino neighborhoods, no dedicated Pickup & Delivery information page, and a competing .com domain pointing to a Miami business. The site is built on GoDaddy’s website builder, which imposes meaningful limitations on SEO control, structured data implementation, and bilingual content architecture.
This strategy document defines the complete architecture for a rebuilt website that resolves every issue identified in the audit and positions Spin It Up to dominate local search in Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, and Albany Park. The build encompasses 22 English pages and 8 Spanish-language priority pages, a full brand and design system, keyword strategy across 5 content silos, conversion-optimized page templates, and a phased rollout targeting a score of 80+ within 90 days of launch.
The companion Homepage Developer Guide (delivered alongside this document) provides the section-by-section, copy-complete implementation specification for the first and highest-priority page in the build.

5.3 Silo Structure
Each silo operates as a self-contained topic cluster. Hub pages link down to all children; children link up to hub and across to siblings. Cross-silo links connect service pages to relevant area pages and resource articles. This structure supports both traditional SEO and entity-based GEO signals.
Services Silo: /services/ (hub) → 5 child pages. Each child links to related area pages, FAQ section, and pricing. Hub links to all children with service cards.
Areas Silo: /areas/ (hub) → 5 neighborhood pages. Each area page links to all services available in that area, the contact page, and the Cents app ordering page. Hub displays interactive map with all service areas.
Resources Silo: /resources/ (hub) → blog articles. Each article links to 2+ service pages and 1+ related articles. Hub shows card grid of all articles sorted by recency.
Company Pages: /about/, /contact/, /faq/ — linked from global navigation. FAQ page cross-links to service pages for detailed answers. Contact page is primary conversion target.
Legal Pages: footer-only links. /privacy-policy/, /terms-of-service/, /accessibility/.

9. Bilingual Strategy
Avondale is one of Chicago’s most heavily Mexican-American neighborhoods. Spanish-language search for laundromat services is significant — “lavanderia cerca de mi” alone carries 22,000+ monthly searches nationally. Wash Smart Lavanderia, Spin It Up’s largest regional competitor, wins the bilingual market by default because they publish full Spanish content. Spin It Up currently has zero Spanish-language content on its website.
9.1 Architecture
All Spanish pages live under the /es/ URL prefix: /es/, /es/servicios/, /es/precios/, etc.
Each English page includes <link rel="alternate" hreflang="es" href="..."> pointing to its Spanish counterpart, and vice versa.
A language toggle (EN | ES) appears in both the desktop header and mobile menu, using a flag icon or text label.
The toggle links to the equivalent page in the other language (not to the homepage). If no translation exists, it links to the Spanish/English homepage.
Google Search Console submits separate sitemaps for /sitemap-en.xml and /sitemap-es.xml.
9.2 Content Standards
Spanish content is not machine-translated. All Spanish pages are written by a native or fluent speaker using conversational Mexican Spanish appropriate to the Avondale community. The tone matches the English site: friendly, direct, informative. Spanish pages are not word-for-word translations — they are culturally adapted. For example, the EBT acceptance messaging is more prominent in Spanish pages because it’s a higher-priority concern for the Spanish-speaking customer segment.
9.3 Priority Order
Phase 2 translations (highest impact first): Homepage, Services Hub, Self-Service, Wash & Fold, Pickup & Delivery, Pricing, Contact. Phase 3: FAQ, area pages. The homepage Spanish version should be ready to launch alongside the English site if timeline permits.

11.3 Image Standards

11.5 Security & Compliance
SSL/TLS: HTTPS enforced on all pages, HSTS header enabled. This is the #1 priority technical fix.
Security headers: Content-Security-Policy, X-Frame-Options, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy.
Privacy Policy page addressing data collection, cookie usage, form data handling, and call recording (Illinois is a two-party consent state for call recording — disclosure required).
Cookie consent banner if using non-essential cookies (analytics, remarketing).
WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance: contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, alt text, ARIA landmarks, skip-to-content link.
ADA compliance statement page linked from footer.
