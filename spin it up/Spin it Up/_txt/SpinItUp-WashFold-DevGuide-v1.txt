14. Section 10: FAQ Section
Section Overview
Wash-and-fold-specific FAQ targeting long-tail queries about service details, pricing, and process. FAQPage schema required. Background: Ice Blue (#F0F9FF).
Content Specifications
H2: "Wash & Fold FAQ" — 4 words. Centered. Font: 32px/700, Dark Slate. Margin-bottom 40px.

#
Question
Answer
1
How much does wash and fold cost?
Standard wash-and-fold pricing at Spin It Up Laundry starts at $1.50 per pound with a 10 lb minimum. Same-day service is available at $2.00 per pound for orders dropped off before 10:00 AM. Comforters are priced at a flat rate: $12 for full/twin, $15 for queen, and $18 for king. Stain treatment is included in every order at no additional charge.
2
How long does wash and fold take?
Standard turnaround is next day. If you drop off before 10:00 AM, same-day service is available at the $2.00/lb rate. We call or text you when your order is ready. If you cannot pick up on the expected day, we hold your order for up to 7 days at no additional charge.
3
Do you separate colors and whites?
Yes. Our attendants sort every order by color, fabric type, and washing temperature before loading. Whites, darks, and colors are washed separately. Delicate items are identified and handled appropriately.
4
Can I request a specific detergent?
We use a professional-grade detergent as our standard. If you prefer hypoallergenic, fragrance-free, or a specific brand, let us know at drop-off and we will accommodate your request at no extra charge.
5
What if something gets lost or damaged?
We take care with every order. Each customer’s laundry is washed individually — never mixed with another customer’s clothes. If any issue arises, contact us at (773) 654-1275 and we will make it right.
6
Is there a minimum order?
Yes. The minimum order for standard wash-and-fold is 10 pounds. Most customers find that a standard laundry bag weighs 12–18 pounds, so most orders exceed the minimum naturally.
