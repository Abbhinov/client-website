About Spin It Up Laundry

Spin It Up Laundry is a modern, full-service laundromat located in the heart of Avondale at 3710 N Kimball Ave, Chicago, IL 60618. We proudly serve families, renters, workers, students, and local businesses throughout Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, Albany Park, and Chicago's Northwest Side.

Our goal is simple: make laundry day easier, faster, cleaner, and more affordable.

Whether you prefer to do your own laundry, drop it off for wash-and-fold, schedule pickup and delivery, or need reliable laundry service for your business, Spin It Up Laundry is built to handle it all in one convenient place.
