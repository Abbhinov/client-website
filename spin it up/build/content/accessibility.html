Accessibility Statement

Effective: May 24, 2026

Our Commitment

Spin It Up Laundry is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all visitors, including people with disabilities. We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level, which defines standards for making web content more accessible to people with visual, auditory, physical, speech, cognitive, and neurological disabilities.

What We're Doing

We have taken the following steps to make our website accessible:

  • Using semantic HTML to ensure proper document structure for screen readers
  • Providing alternative text for all meaningful images
  • Ensuring sufficient color contrast between text and backgrounds (minimum 4.5:1 ratio for normal text)
  • Making all interactive elements accessible via keyboard navigation
  • Providing visible focus indicators on all focusable elements
  • Using ARIA landmarks and labels to improve screen reader navigation
  • Ensuring forms have properly associated labels and clear error messaging
  • Making phone numbers clickable for assistive technology and mobile users
  • Not disabling pinch-to-zoom on mobile devices
  • Ensuring all content is readable at 200% zoom

Known Limitations

While we strive for full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, some limitations may exist: embedded Google Maps content is controlled by Google and may have accessibility limitations beyond our control, the Cents app for pickup and delivery ordering is a third-party platform with its own accessibility practices, and some older images may not yet have fully descriptive alternative text. We are actively working to address these gaps.

Third-Party Content

Our website includes content from third-party services including Google Maps, the Cents ordering platform, and social media embeds. While we select partners who share our commitment to accessibility, we cannot guarantee the accessibility of third-party content. If you experience difficulty with any third-party features, please contact us and we will help you access the information or service through an alternative method.

Compliance Standard

This website targets conformance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. This is an ongoing effort, and we regularly review and update our site to improve accessibility.
