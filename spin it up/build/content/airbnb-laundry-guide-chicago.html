The Airbnb Host's Guide to Laundry in Chicago

By Spin It Up Laundry • Published May 24, 2026 • 7 min read

For a short-term rental host, laundry is the hidden bottleneck of every turnover. Guests check out at 11 AM and new ones arrive at 3 PM, and in those four hours the sheets, towels, and duvet covers have to be stripped, washed, dried, folded, and put back on the bed. This guide breaks down how Chicago hosts keep up — whether you self-manage one unit or run several.

The turnover math nobody warns you about

A single one-bedroom turnover generates roughly 15–25 lb of laundry: a set of sheets, a duvet cover, pillowcases, two to four bath towels, hand towels, a bath mat, and kitchen linens. On a home washer that's two or three cycles plus drying — easily three to four hours of machine time. With back-to-back bookings, you physically cannot finish before the next guest arrives. That's why most hosts end up doing one of three things: buying multiple linen sets, washing offsite, or outsourcing entirely.

Build par stock so laundry never blocks a check-in

"Par stock" means keeping enough linen sets that a clean set is always ready while another is in the wash. The rule of thumb for hosts is three sets per bed: one on the bed, one clean in the closet, one in the laundry cycle. The same goes for towels. With three sets, a delayed wash never delays a check-in — you just make the bed with the spare set and launder on your own schedule.

Where to actually do the laundry

Your options, roughly in order of how much time they cost you:

  • In-unit washer/dryer: convenient but slow, and it ties up the unit during the turnover window. Fine for a single low-volume listing.
  • Self-service at a laundromat: our large and 130 lb machines wash several rooms' worth of linens in a single cycle — far faster than running four home loads. Good if you have time to be there.
  • Wash & fold drop-off or pickup: the lowest-effort option. You hand off the dirty linens and get them back clean, folded, and ready to go on the bed.

Keep linens guest-ready, not just clean

Guests notice grey, pilled towels and stained sheets — they show up in reviews. A few habits that protect your linens and your rating:

  • Treat stains fast (makeup, wine, sunscreen) — our wash & fold includes stain treatment on every order.
  • Wash whites separately on hot to keep them bright; retire any linen that's gone permanently grey.
  • Rotate sets evenly so they wear at the same rate.
  • Keep a tight, hotel-style fold for a professional first impression.
