Using EBT & SNAP for Laundry: What to Know

By Spin It Up Laundry • Published May 24, 2026 • 5 min read

Spin It Up Laundry is one of the few laundromats in the Avondale area that accepts EBT — and we get a lot of questions about how that works. There's an important distinction to understand, because not every part of an EBT card can be used for laundry. Here's a clear, honest breakdown.

The short version: SNAP (food) benefits can only be used for groceries — not laundry. But many EBT cards also carry cash benefits, and those can be used for laundry. When we say we "accept EBT," we mean the cash-benefit side of your Illinois Link card.

SNAP vs. cash benefits on your EBT card

In Illinois, benefits are delivered on a single Link card (the state's EBT card), but that card can hold two separate types of benefits:

  • SNAP benefits (formerly food stamps) — restricted by federal law to eligible food items at authorized grocery retailers. SNAP cannot be used for laundry, services, or non-food items.
  • Cash benefits — from programs like TANF (Temporary Assistance for Needy Families). These work like cash and can be used for laundry, including at a laundromat that accepts EBT cash.

So whether you can pay for laundry with your Link card depends on whether you have cash benefits on it — not on the SNAP food portion.

Where to apply for benefits

If you think you may qualify for SNAP or cash assistance in Illinois, you can apply through the Illinois Department of Human Services at abe.illinois.gov or by calling the IDHS hotline. Eligibility and benefit types are determined by the state — we're a laundromat, not a benefits office, but we're glad to make laundry one less thing to worry about.
