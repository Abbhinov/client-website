General

Spin It Up Laundry is open from 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM, seven days a week, including all major holidays. Our last wash cycle starts at 9:30 PM to ensure all loads complete before closing. We recommend arriving by 9:00 PM for evening visits to allow time for a full wash-and-dry cycle.

We are located at 3710 N Kimball Ave in the Avondale neighborhood of Chicago, Illinois 60618. We are on Kimball Ave between Irving Park Rd and Addison St, near the intersection with School St. Free parking is available directly in front of the laundromat, and there is additional free street parking on Kimball Ave and surrounding residential streets.

We offer five laundry services: self-service laundry with 41 washers and 52 dryers including 130 lb commercial machines, professional wash-and-fold drop-off starting at $1.50 per pound, pickup and delivery ordered through the Cents app, commercial laundry services for businesses, and garment ironing and pressing. Visit our Services page for details on each.

Yes. Spin It Up Laundry accepts EBT (Electronic Benefit Transfer) payments for self-service washing and drying. We are one of the few laundromats in the Avondale area that accepts EBT. Swipe your EBT card at the front counter to load a laundry card and use it at any machine. Wash-and-fold, pickup and delivery, and commercial services are paid via credit card, debit card, cash, or laundry card.

Yes. Free parking is available directly in front of the laundromat at 3710 N Kimball Ave. There is also free street parking on Kimball Ave and surrounding residential streets. We do not have a dedicated lot, but street parking is typically available within one block.

Wash & Fold

Standard turnaround is next day. If you drop off before 10:00 AM, same-day service is available at the $1.75 per pound rate. We call or text you when your order is ready for pickup. If you cannot pick up on the expected day, we hold your order for up to 7 days at no additional charge. See our Wash & Fold page.

Pickup & Delivery

Download the free Cents app on your phone, enter your address, select a pickup window, and confirm your order. Our driver picks up your laundry from your door during the selected time. We wash, dry, and fold everything at our facility. Your clean laundry is delivered back to your door, typically within 24 hours. You do not need to be home for pickup or delivery. See our Pickup & Delivery page.

We currently deliver to Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, and Albany Park on Chicago's Northwest Side. The Cents app confirms whether your address is in our delivery zone when you enter it. If you are nearby but outside the listed areas, call us at (773) 654-1275 — we may be able to arrange delivery. See our Service Areas.

Yes. The Cents app allows you to set up recurring pickups on a weekly or biweekly schedule. Your address, preferences, and payment method are saved so reordering takes a single tap. You can pause or cancel anytime without fees. See our Pickup & Delivery page.

Commercial Services

Yes. We provide commercial laundry services for Airbnb and vacation rental hosts, hair salons, gyms, restaurants, small hotels, and property management companies. We offer volume-based pricing, consistent turnaround, and scheduled pickup and delivery for commercial accounts. No long-term contracts required. Visit our Commercial Services page or call (773) 654-1275 for a custom quote.

There is no strict minimum. Volume-based pricing discounts begin at approximately 50 lb per week. Orders below that threshold are processed at our standard wash-and-fold rates, which are still competitive for small businesses like Airbnb hosts with one or two properties. See our Commercial Services page.
