How to Use the Cents App for Laundry Pickup & Delivery

By Spin It Up Laundry • Published May 24, 2026 • 5 min read

The Cents app is the fastest way to schedule laundry pickup and delivery with Spin It Up Laundry in Chicago. Available free on iOS and Android, the app lets you enter your address, choose a pickup time, add special instructions, and pay securely — all in under two minutes. Once you've placed an order, our driver picks up your laundry from your door, we wash, dry, and fold everything at our facility at 3710 N Kimball Ave, and deliver it back clean within 24 hours. This guide walks you through every step, from downloading the app to receiving your first delivery.

What Is the Cents App?

Cents is a mobile app used by laundromats across the country to offer pickup and delivery services. Think of it like ordering food delivery, but for your laundry. Spin It Up Laundry uses Cents as our ordering platform — when you open the app and search for laundromats near you, you'll find us listed as a provider in the Avondale area. The app handles scheduling, payment, and order tracking. You can also place orders through the Cents website on any browser without downloading the app.

Step 1: Download the App & Create Your Account

  • Open the App Store (iPhone) or Google Play Store (Android) on your phone.
  • Search for "Cents" — the app icon is a blue circle with a white "C".
  • Tap "Install" or "Get" to download the app. It's free.
  • Open the app and tap "Create Account."
  • Enter your name, email address, and create a password.
  • Enter your phone number for order notifications.
  • Add a payment method (credit or debit card). This is required to place orders.

The entire setup takes less than 2 minutes. You'll only need to do this once — your account saves your information for all future orders.

Step 2: Schedule Your First Pickup

1

Enter Your Address

Open the Cents app and enter your pickup address. The app will confirm whether you're in Spin It Up's delivery zone (Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, Albany Park).

2

Choose a Pickup Window

Select a date and time window for pickup. We offer windows throughout the day, seven days a week. Pick the time that works best for your schedule.

3

Add Special Instructions

Note any preferences: stain locations, fabric care requests, detergent preferences (fragrance-free, hypoallergenic), or sorting instructions. This step is optional but helps us give you the best results.

4

Review Your Order

The app shows your estimated price based on the per-pound rate ($2.00/lb next day, $2.25/lb same day) plus the $5.00 pickup fee. Confirm everything looks right.

5

Place Your Order

Tap "Confirm Order." Your payment method is charged after your laundry is weighed at our facility — not at the time of ordering. The app estimate is based on typical load weight.

6

Leave Your Bag Out

Place your laundry bag at your door, in your building lobby, or at the designated pickup spot before your window starts. You don't need to be home — the driver will collect it. You'll receive a notification when pickup is complete.

That's it. Your laundry is now in our hands. We'll wash, dry, and fold everything and deliver it back within 24 hours (or same day for express orders placed before 10 AM).

Step 3: Track Your Order & Receive Delivery

After your laundry is picked up, you can track the order status in the Cents app:

StatusWhat It Means
Pickup ConfirmedYour driver has collected your laundry bag.
In ProgressYour laundry is being washed, dried, and folded at our facility.
Ready for DeliveryYour clean laundry is packaged and waiting for the next delivery route.
Out for DeliveryYour driver is on the way to your address.
DeliveredYour clean laundry has been left at your door.

You'll receive push notifications at each stage. For future orders, the app saves your address, preferences, and payment method — reordering takes a single tap.

Tips for the Best Experience

Use a sturdy bagA drawstring laundry bag or large garbage bag works well. Make sure it's sealed so nothing falls out during pickup.
Note stain locationsIf you have items with stains, mention them in the special instructions. We treat all visible stains, but a heads-up helps us catch everything.
Set up recurring pickupsIf you order weekly, use the app's recurring pickup feature to schedule automatically. One less thing to remember.
Check your delivery areaAlbany Park is our extended zone. If you're at the edge of our area, the app will let you know at checkout.
Contact us if anything's offIf your order has any issues, call (773) 654-1275 or message us through the app. We'll make it right.

Troubleshooting

IssueSolution

I missed the pickup windowReschedule through the app for the next available window. No cancellation fee.
