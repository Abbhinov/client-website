Laundry Tips for Chicago Winters

By Spin It Up Laundry • Published May 24, 2026 • 6 min read

Chicago winters are hard on clothes. Between road salt, slush, static, and bulky layers, the season creates laundry problems you don't deal with in July. This guide covers the cold-weather issues we see most often at Spin It Up Laundry in Avondale — and how to handle each one so your winter wardrobe lasts.

Treat road salt stains quickly

The white crust that road salt leaves on boots, pant hems, and coats isn't just cosmetic — the salt is corrosive and can weaken fabric and leave permanent rings if it sets. As soon as you can, blot (don't rub) the area, then pre-treat with a mix of one part white vinegar to one part cool water before washing. Vinegar neutralizes the salt. Wash in the warmest water the care label allows.

If you bring salt-stained items in for wash & fold, point out the spots at drop-off — stain treatment is included on every order, and a heads-up helps our attendants catch everything.

Wash winter coats before you store them — and after

Down jackets, parkas, and wool coats hold body oils, salt, and grime that attract odor and break down insulation over time. Most puffer coats are machine washable on a gentle, cold cycle — check the label. The catch is drying: a home dryer often isn't big enough to let a down coat tumble freely, which is how the down re-lofts. Our large and commercial dryers give a coat the room it needs (toss in a couple of clean tennis balls or dryer balls to break up clumps).

Beat static cling without ruining your clothes

Winter air is dry, and dry air means static. A few fixes that actually work:

  • Don't over-dry. Static spikes when loads run bone-dry — pull items while just barely damp and hang the last bit.
  • Use wool dryer balls instead of disposable sheets; they cut drying time and reduce static.
  • Separate synthetics from natural fibers — mixing the two is what generates the most cling.

Never let jeans (or anything) freeze on the line

If you line-dry, bring laundry in before temperatures drop below freezing. Fabric that freezes while damp becomes stiff and the ice crystals can stress the fibers. In a Chicago winter, indoor drying — or our dryers — is simply more reliable.

Wash heavy bedding when the seasons turn

Comforters, duvets, and heavy blankets collect dust and allergens all winter. They rarely fit a home washer, which is the main reason people skip washing them. Our 130 lb commercial machines — the biggest washers in Chicago, you can't buy a larger washer — handle king-size comforters and multiple blankets in one load. Plan around free drying days

Winter loads are heavier and take longer to dry, so drying costs add up. At Spin It Up, drying is free every Wednesday and Thursday when you wash with us. Planning your big winter loads — coats, bedding, towels — around those days is the easiest way to cut your cost. See the full pricing page for details.

Quick winter laundry checklist

  • Pre-treat salt stains with vinegar and water before washing.
  • Wash coats on gentle/cold; dry with dryer balls in a large dryer.
  • Pull synthetics out slightly damp to avoid static.
  • Bring line-dried laundry in before it freezes.
  • Save bulky bedding for a free drying day (Wed–Thu).
