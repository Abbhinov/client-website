Self-Service Machine Pricing

Walk in and use our machines anytime, 6 AM–11 PM, seven days a week.

Spin It Up Laundry at 3710 N Kimball Ave in Avondale, Chicago offers transparent pricing across all laundry services. Self-service machines start at $4.75 per load for standard washers and go up to $23.75 for our 130 lb commercial-capacity machines. Wash-and-fold drop-off service starts at $1.50 per pound with a 10 lb minimum and next-day turnaround. Pickup and delivery through the Cents app starts at $2.00 per pound plus a $5.00 pickup fee. We accept cash, credit, debit, EBT, and our reloadable laundry card. Drying is free every Wednesday and Thursday when you wash with us.

Pricing FAQ

Standard wash-and-fold pricing at Spin It Up Laundry is $1.50 per pound with a 10 lb minimum. Same-day service is $1.75 per pound for orders dropped off before 10:00 AM. Stain treatment is included on every order at no extra charge.

Comforters are flat-rate for all services: Twin/Full $12, Queen $15, King $18.

Pickup and delivery includes a $5.00 pickup fee and a per-pound rate of $2.00 (vs. $1.50 for drop-off). The difference covers our driver's time and fuel to pick up and deliver to your door. The wash-and-fold process itself is identical.
