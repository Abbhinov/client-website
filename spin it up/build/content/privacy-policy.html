Introduction

Spin It Up Laundry ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website spinituplaundry.net and provides laundry services at 3710 N Kimball Ave, Chicago, IL 60618. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, submit a contact form, use our pickup and delivery service through the Cents app, or interact with us in person at our facility.

By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our website or services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you fill out a contact form, request a commercial quote, or communicate with us, you may provide: your name, email address, phone number, business name (for commercial inquiries), service preferences, and any message content you submit. When you use our pickup and delivery service through the Cents app, the information you provide is collected and processed by Cents (our third-party ordering platform) under their own privacy policy.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain information through cookies and analytics tools, including: your IP address, browser type, device type, operating system, pages visited, time spent on pages, referring website, and general geographic location. We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Tag Manager (GTM) for website analytics. These tools use cookies to collect anonymized usage data.

Payment Information

We do not collect, store, or process credit card, debit card, or EBT card numbers through our website. In-store payments (including EBT) are processed at the point of sale through our payment terminal. Online payments for pickup and delivery are processed through the Cents app, which handles payment information under their own privacy and security policies.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your contact form submissions and commercial inquiries
  • Provide and improve our laundry services
  • Send order confirmations and service-related communications
  • Analyze website usage to improve our site and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions and commercial inquiry data for as long as necessary to respond to your request and maintain our business records, typically up to 24 months. Website analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics configuration. You may request deletion of your personal information at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to: request access to the personal information we hold about you, request correction of inaccurate information, request deletion of your personal information, opt out of non-essential cookies through your browser settings, and unsubscribe from any marketing communications (if applicable). To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@spinituplaundry.net or (773) 654-1275.

Illinois-Specific Disclosures

Illinois is a two-party consent state for audio recording. If we implement call recording for quality assurance purposes in the future, all parties will be notified at the beginning of the call and recording will only proceed with consent.

We do not collect biometric information (fingerprints, facial recognition, etc.) through our website or at our facility. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and provide appropriate notice and consent mechanisms as required by the Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act (BIPA).

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
