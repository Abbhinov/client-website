How It Works

Spin It Up Laundry offers professional wash-and-fold drop-off service at 3710 N Kimball Ave in Avondale, Chicago. Bring your laundry to our facility and our experienced attendants will wash, dry, and fold everything for you using commercial-grade machines and professional detergent. Standard turnaround is next day, with same-day service available for orders dropped off before 10:00 AM. Pricing starts at $1.50 per pound with a 10 lb minimum. Stain treatment is included at no extra charge on every order. We also offer comforter and bedding wash-and-fold at flat-rate pricing.

01

Drop Off Your Laundry

Bring your laundry to 3710 N Kimball Ave during operating hours (6 AM–11 PM, 7 days). Our attendant weighs your bag, notes any special instructions, and gives you a receipt with your estimated pickup time.

02

We Wash, Dry & Fold

Our team washes your clothes using commercial-grade machines with professional-quality detergent. We treat stains before washing at no extra charge. Everything is dried at the appropriate temperature and hand-folded.

03

Quality Check

Before your order is bagged, we inspect each item for remaining stains, check that nothing was missed, and ensure everything is neatly folded. Garments that need special attention are flagged and handled separately.

04

Pick Up Clean & Ready

We call or text you when your order is ready. Standard turnaround is next day. Walk in, show your receipt, and take home your clean, folded laundry. Or upgrade to pickup and delivery through our Cents app.

What's Included in Every Wash & Fold Order

  • Professional Washing — Every load washed in commercial-grade machines with professional detergent. Hot, warm, or cold water based on fabric type.
  • Stain Treatment — Pre-wash stain treatment on all visible stains at no extra charge. We use commercial-grade stain removers appropriate for each fabric.
  • Full Drying — Tumble dried at the appropriate temperature for each fabric type. Delicates air-dried on request.
  • Hand Folding — Every item individually hand-folded by our attendants. Shirts, pants, towels, sheets — everything neatly organized.
  • Bagging — Your folded laundry is packaged in a clean bag, organized and ready to put away when you get home.
  • Ready Notification — We call or text you when your order is complete. No guessing, no wasted trips.

Optional Add-Ons

Fabric softener — included (standard on all loads unless you request unscented). Hypoallergenic / fragrance-free detergent — no extra charge, request at drop-off. Hangers for dress shirts & blouses — $0.50/item. Comforter / bedding wash & fold — flat rate, see pricing below.

What We Wash — And What Needs Special Handling

We Wash (Standard Service)

  • Everyday clothing (shirts, pants, shorts, socks, underwear)
  • Towels and washcloths
  • Bed sheets, pillowcases, and standard bedding
  • Comforters and duvets (flat-rate pricing)
  • Sleeping bags
  • Gym clothes and activewear
  • Children's clothing
  • Uniforms and work clothes
  • Table linens and kitchen towels

Special Handling / Not Accepted

  • Dry-clean-only garments (check labels — we cannot process these)
  • Leather, suede, or fur items
  • Heavily soiled items (grease, paint, chemical contamination) — call ahead
  • Items with bedbugs or pest contamination — not accepted for health/safety
  • Large area carpets — not accepted (washable rugs available at flat-rate pricing)
  • Formal wear requiring pressing — see our Ironing & Pressing service
