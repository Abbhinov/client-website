Laundry Services Across Chicago's Northwest Side

Self-service, wash & fold, and pickup & delivery serving Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, and Albany Park.

Our Service Areas

Spin It Up Laundry at 3710 N Kimball Ave provides laundry services to five neighborhoods on Chicago's Northwest Side: Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, and Albany Park. Walk-in self-service and wash-and-fold drop-off are available at our Avondale facility, open 6:00 AM to 11:00 PM daily. Pickup and delivery through the Cents app is available to all five neighborhoods, with most orders completed within 24 hours. Commercial laundry service with scheduled pickup is available throughout the delivery area.

Why a Neighborhood Laundromat Matters

A laundromat three blocks away is fundamentally different from one three miles away.
