Industries We Serve

Spin It Up Laundry provides commercial laundry services for businesses across Chicago, operating out of our facility at 3710 N Kimball Ave in Avondale. We serve Airbnb and vacation rental hosts, hair salons and barbershops, gyms and fitness studios, restaurants, small hotels, and property management companies that need reliable, high-volume laundry on a recurring schedule. Our facility includes two 130 lb commercial-capacity washing machines — the biggest washers in Chicago, you can't buy a larger washer — capable of handling bulk loads that standard laundromats cannot process. We offer volume-based pricing, consistent turnaround times, and pickup and delivery for commercial accounts.
