Frequently Asked Questions About Our Services

Wash-and-fold is a drop-off service: you bring your laundry to our location at 3710 N Kimball Ave, and we wash, dry, and fold it for you to pick up later — typically within 24 hours. Pickup and delivery adds door-to-door convenience: you schedule through the Cents app, and our driver picks up and delivers your laundry to your home or office. Both services use the same professional wash process and include stain treatment.

Our largest machines hold up to 130 pounds, making them the biggest washers in Chicago — you can't buy a larger washer. These commercial-capacity machines handle king-size comforters, sleeping bags, large blankets, restaurant linens, and commercial bulk loads that standard laundromat machines cannot fit.

For wash-and-fold drop-off, most orders dropped off before 10:00 AM are ready for same-day pickup by closing time. Standard turnaround is next-day. Pickup and delivery orders are typically completed within 24 hours of pickup. Contact us at (773) 654-1275 for rush service availability.

Commercial accounts receive volume-based pricing tailored to your business needs. Pricing depends on load frequency, volume, and service type. Airbnb hosts, salons, gyms, and restaurants typically save 15–25% compared to standard per-pound rates. Contact us for a custom quote.

EBT is accepted for self-service washing and drying at our facility. Wash-and-fold, pickup and delivery, and commercial services are paid via credit card, debit card, or cash. We are one of the few laundromats in Avondale that accepts EBT for any service.
