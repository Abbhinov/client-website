What We Press

Spin It Up Laundry offers professional ironing and garment pressing service at 3710 N Kimball Ave in Avondale, Chicago. Our experienced attendants press dress shirts, suits, blazers, slacks, uniforms, chef coats, and formal wear using commercial-grade equipment. Pricing starts at $3.00 per item with next-day turnaround. Drop off at our facility during operating hours (6:00 AM to 9:30 PM, seven days a week) and pick up your garments pressed, on hangers, and ready to wear. Combine with our wash-and-fold service for a complete laundry and pressing solution.

Items We Do Not Press

  • Leather, suede, or fur garments
  • Items labeled "do not iron"
  • Heavily soiled items (these must be washed first — see our Wash & Fold service)
  • Items requiring dry cleaning (we are not a dry cleaner)

How It Works

01

Drop Off Your Garments

Bring your items to 3710 N Kimball Ave during operating hours. Our attendant inspects each garment, notes any special instructions, and gives you a receipt with your estimated pickup time.

02

We Press & Finish

Each garment is professionally pressed using commercial equipment at the appropriate temperature for the fabric. Shirts get crisp collars and cuffs. Suits are shaped and finished. Everything goes on hangers.

03

Pick Up Ready to Wear

We call or text when your order is ready — typically next day. Walk in, show your receipt, and take home your pressed garments on hangers, protected in garment covers.

Drop Off Everything at Once

Bring your everyday laundry and your dress clothes in the same trip. We'll wash and fold your regular clothes and press your professional garments — everything ready for pickup at the same time. One stop, one visit, your entire week's laundry handled.
