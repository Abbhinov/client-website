How It Works — Four Simple Steps

Spin It Up Laundry offers laundry pickup and delivery service across Chicago's Northwest Side, including Avondale, Irving Park, Logan Square, Hermosa, and Albany Park. Customers schedule a pickup through the Cents app, and our driver collects their laundry from their door. We wash, dry, and fold everything at our facility at 3710 N Kimball Ave using commercial-grade machines and professional detergent, then deliver it back clean and ready — typically within 24 hours. Pricing is $2.00 per pound for next-day delivery and $2.25 per pound for same-day delivery. Stain treatment is included on every order at no additional charge. The service is available seven days a week.

01

Schedule Your Pickup

Open the Cents app on your phone (iOS or Android), enter your address, select a pickup window, and confirm your order. You can add special instructions for stain treatment, fabric preferences, or sorting requests.

First-time users: download the Cents app free from the App Store or Google Play. Create an account in under 2 minutes.

02

We Pick Up From Your Door

Our driver arrives during your selected pickup window and collects your laundry bag from your doorstep, lobby, or designated spot. No need to be home — just leave your bag in the agreed location.

Contactless pickup available. You'll receive a notification when your bag is collected.

03

Professional Wash, Dry & Fold

Your laundry is brought to our facility at 3710 N Kimball Ave. Our team sorts by color and fabric, pre-treats stains, washes in commercial-grade machines with professional detergent, dries at the appropriate temperature, and hand-folds every item.

Same professional process as our in-store wash-and-fold service. Your laundry is never mixed with another customer's.

04

Delivered Back to Your Door

Your clean, neatly folded laundry is delivered right back to your door in a fresh bag. Most orders are completed within 24 hours of pickup. You'll receive a notification when delivery is on the way.

Track your order status in the Cents app.
