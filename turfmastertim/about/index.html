How I Got Into This

I grew up in Ohio. Helped my dad cut the lawn from the time I could push a mower, and that was the start of it. Mowing was the first job I ever really loved — there's something honest about leaving a yard better than you found it.

I moved to the Brandon area and quickly figured out that what works on Ohio bluegrass has almost nothing to do with what works on Florida turf. Different grass, different pests, different soil, a totally different watering rhythm. So I rolled up my sleeves and learned. Talked to extension agents, read everything I could get my hands on, and put in the hours in real customer yards until I understood how Brandon lawns actually behave.

My family and I are members of Bell Shoals Church here in Brandon, and that's a big part of who I am and how I run this business. Show up. Tell the truth. Treat people right. Those aren't slogans on a website — they're how I was raised and how I expect my crew to operate every single day.

In 2019, I launched Edge 2 Hedges. My idea was simple: bring careful, thorough lawn care to regular homeowners at prices regular homeowners can afford. No franchise playbook. No call center. No rotating crews. Just me, my team, and a genuine obsession with making Brandon's lawns look their best.

Today I take care of hundreds of properties across Brandon, Valrico, Riverview, and surrounding communities. I still personally visit every new property. I still answer my phone. And I still get fired up every time I turn a struggling lawn into the best one on the block.

What I Stand For

Consistency

Same crew, same day, every week. I assign your team and they stay with you. They'll learn your lawn's quirks, remember where the sprinkler head is that sticks up, and know that you like the edging extra tight along the driveway. You'll know their names. That's how it should be.

Expertise

I didn't pick this up from a franchise training manual. Years of hands-on work in Florida's specific climate, ongoing education through UF/IFAS extension resources, and a long memory for what actually works in Brandon's soil. When I tell you your lawn needs something, it's because experience says so — not because I'm trying to upsell you.

Honesty

Flat prices on my website. No contracts. No hidden fees. If your lawn doesn't need something, I won't sell it to you. If I mess up, I'll fix it. If I can't help you, I'll tell you. I'd rather lose a sale than lose your trust.
