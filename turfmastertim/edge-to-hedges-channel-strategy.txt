2. Tier 1: Critical Foundation (Week 1)
These platforms must be set up first. They are the backbone of local search visibility and the primary channels homeowners use to find lawn service providers. Nothing else matters until these are live with accurate, complete information.


2.1 Google Business Profile
Type: Local business listing (free)
SEO Impact: Highest single factor for Map Pack ranking and local search visibility.
Backlink Value: Direct entity authority signal to Google. Not a traditional backlink but the most important local signal.
Google Business Profile is non-negotiable. It controls whether Edge to Hedges appears in the Local 3-Pack when homeowners search for lawn care in the service area. 46% of all Google searches have local intent, and the Map Pack appears above organic results.
Setup Requirements
* Primary category: Lawn care service
* Secondary categories: Landscaper, Garden service (if applicable)
* Complete NAP with exact business name, street address, phone number
* Service area defined by zip codes or city radius
* Business hours including seasonal variations
* All service types listed with keyword-aligned descriptions
* Minimum 10 original photos: crew at work, equipment, before/after results, team headshots
* Business description (750 characters) with primary keywords and service area
* Website URL linked to homepage
* Messaging enabled for direct inquiries
Ongoing Requirements
* 1–2 GBP Posts per week (offers, updates, service announcements)
* Respond to every review within 24–48 hours
* Add new photos monthly (job completions, seasonal work)
* Update seasonal services and hours proactively


2.2 Company Website
Type: Owned property (investment required)
SEO Impact: The central hub that every other channel links to. Controls organic ranking potential.
Backlink Value: Every directory and social profile links here. This is the destination for all citation authority.
Even as a free client, Edge to Hedges needs a website. It does not need to be complex. A well-built 5–7 page site is sufficient to start: Home, Services, About, Service Areas, Contact, and a blog for ongoing content. Without a website, every directory listing links to nothing and the GBP profile has no URL destination.
Minimum Pages
* Homepage with service overview, service area, and primary CTA
* Services page (or individual pages per service: mowing, edging, hedge trimming, cleanup, mulching)
* About page with owner story, team photos, and trust signals
* Service area page with city/neighborhood targeting
* Contact page with form, phone, email, and embedded map


2.3 Facebook Business Page
Type: Social media (free)
SEO Impact: High domain authority backlink. GBP and Facebook together create foundational entity signals.
Backlink Value: DA 96 backlink from profile URL. Facebook Business Pages are indexed and crawled.
Facebook remains the dominant social platform for local home service discovery. For lawn care specifically, Facebook is where homeowners ask for recommendations in neighborhood groups, share before/after photos, and leave reviews. The business page also powers Facebook Ads and Marketplace visibility.
Setup Requirements
* Business Page (not personal profile) with complete business info
* NAP matching GBP exactly (critical for citation consistency)
* Cover photo and profile photo (logo or branded image)
* All services listed in the Services tab
* CTA button configured: Call Now or Send Message
* Auto-responder enabled for after-hours messages
* Reviews tab enabled and seeded with initial customer reviews


2.4 Yelp Business Profile
Type: Review directory (free listing; paid advertising optional)
SEO Impact: Yelp pages rank independently for "[service] + [city]" searches. High domain authority.
Backlink Value: DA 93 backlink. Yelp listings frequently outrank local business websites in organic results.
Yelp is a Tier 1 priority because its pages rank organically in Google for lawn care searches and it appears in Apple Maps results. Even without paid advertising, a complete free Yelp profile with accurate NAP and a few reviews generates visibility. Yelp also feeds data to Apple Maps, Siri, and Alexa.
Setup Requirements
* Claim or create business listing with exact NAP
* Select primary category: Lawn Services
* Add secondary categories: Landscaping, Hedge Trimming
* Upload minimum 5 photos of completed work
* Write business description with keywords and service area
* Set service area and hours
* Enable quote requests

Instagram Priority Notes
Lawn care is one of the most visually rewarding trades on Instagram. Before/after transformations, satisfying edging videos, and lush lawn reveals perform extremely well. 5. Tier 4: Supplemental (Month 2+)
These are lower-priority directories and platforms that contribute to long-tail citation coverage. None are urgent, but collectively they fill gaps in the citation profile and provide marginal backlink value.


5.1 Local & Community Directories
* Local Chamber of Commerce (if available in service area) — High trust signal, DA varies by chamber
* City/county business directory (many municipalities maintain free listings)
* Local newspaper or community blog business directories
* Neighborhood HOA directories or community newsletters (not online but builds local recognition)
* Church, school, or community organization sponsor pages (backlink + community trust)


5.2 Review & Rating Platforms
* Birdeye — Review management platform; listing provides citation (DA 70+)
* Trustpilot — Less common for local services but provides a high-DA backlink (DA 93)
* Google Reviews (via GBP) — Already covered in Tier 1 but the review generation strategy is ongoing
* Facebook Reviews — Already covered; cross-promote review requests


5.3 Emerging & AI Search Platforms
AI search engines are increasingly sourcing local business data from structured platforms. While there is no direct way to submit to ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews, the following actions increase the likelihood of being referenced:
* Maintain a complete Thumbtack profile (partnership with ChatGPT for service recommendations)
* Publish structured FAQ content on the website (schema markup for AI extraction)
* Maintain consistent entity data across all platforms (name, services, location, phone)
* Create YouTube content with clear question-format titles for AEO
* Post on LinkedIn and Reddit (both are heavily indexed by AI models for training and retrieval)

6. Backlink Strategy Beyond Directories
Directory listings provide the citation foundation, but true backlink authority requires earned links from relevant, authoritative sources. These strategies are appropriate for a lawn care company at any stage.


6.1 Local Partnership Backlinks
* Real estate agents: Offer a preferred vendor relationship. Many agents maintain resource pages on their websites linking to trusted service providers.
* Property management companies: Same approach. Property managers need reliable lawn care and often list vendors on their sites.
* Complementary home services: Exchange referral links with non-competing contractors (pool service, pressure washing, fence companies, pest control).
* HOA management companies: Many HOAs maintain approved vendor lists on their websites.


6.2 Content-Driven Backlinks
* Seasonal lawn care guides: Publish original content (e.g., "Florida Lawn Care Calendar: Month-by-Month Guide") that local blogs and community sites will reference and link to.
* Before/after gallery: A well-organized project gallery becomes a linkable asset when shared in community groups and forums.
* Local expertise content: "Best Grass Types for [City] Soil" or "How Florida Humidity Affects Your Lawn" — hyper-local content that competitors in generic directories cannot replicate.


6.3 Community Involvement Backlinks
* Sponsor local events: 5K runs, school fundraisers, and little league teams. Many event pages link to sponsors.
* Volunteer for community beautification: Offer free lawn care for a community space and get mentioned in local press or community newsletters.
* Local press: Submit seasonal lawn care tips as a local expert to community newspapers. Many publish contributor content with a business link.

Complete this checklist in order. Each tier builds on the previous one. NAP consistency across all platforms is the single most important technical detail.

* Launch or verify company website (minimum 5 pages)

* Create TikTok Business account

* Create YouTube channel with branded banner and about section
* Create LinkedIn Company Page
* Create Pinterest Business account

* Audit all listings quarterly for NAP consistency
* Refresh hashtag sets and social posting cadence based on first-month analytics
* Evaluate paid lead-gen activation on Thumbtack or Angi based on inquiry volume

9. NAP Consistency Standards
NAP consistency is the single most important technical factor in local citation building. Google uses NAP data across all directories to verify that a business is real and that its contact information is trustworthy. Inconsistencies — even small ones — erode ranking authority.


Rules for Edge to Hedges
* Business Name: Use the exact same business name on every platform. If the legal name is "Edge to Hedges LLC," decide whether to use "Edge to Hedges" or "Edge to Hedges LLC" and stick with it everywhere. Do not alternate between variations.
* Address: Use the exact format from the USPS address lookup tool. If the USPS says "123 Main St" (not "Street"), use "St" everywhere. Include or exclude the suite/unit number consistently.
* Phone Number: One primary phone number across all platforms. Format it identically: (555) 555-5555 or 555-555-5555 — pick one and use it everywhere.
* Website URL: Use the same URL format everywhere. If the website is https://www.edgetohedges.com, do not link to https://edgetohedges.com (without www) on some platforms and with www on others.


Quarterly Audit Process
* Search for the business name on Google and review the first 3 pages of results
* Check every listed directory for NAP accuracy
* Update any inconsistencies immediately
* Document corrections in a tracking spreadsheet
