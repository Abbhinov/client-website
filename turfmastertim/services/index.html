Questions People Ask Tim

Can I combine multiple services?+
Absolutely. My Total Lawn Package ($89/month) bundles mowing with the health program and saves you about $30 a month vs. buying them separately. You can add pressure washing, irrigation work, or seasonal services anytime.
Do I need a contract?+
Never. I don't do contracts. You stay because you're happy, and you can cancel with one phone call. That's how it should be.
What's included in a mowing visit?+
Mowing at the correct height for your grass, precision edging along all hardscapes, string trimming around everything the mower can't reach, and a full blowdown of all paved surfaces. Plus a quick visual check for any turf health issues.
How fast can you start?+
I can usually get you a same-day estimate and start service within 3–5 business days. For sod and landscaping projects, 1–2 weeks depending on scope and material availability.
Are you licensed and insured?+
Yes. Edge 2 Hedges is a registered Florida LLC with general liability and workers' comp. I'm also a certified Florida-Friendly Landscaping practitioner through UF/IFAS. My license number is in the footer of every page.
