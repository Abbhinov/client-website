Pressure Washing FAQ — Tim’s Answers

How much for a driveway?+
$75–$200 for a standard two-car driveway. Larger or heavily stained driveways may cost more. I give free estimates on-site.
Will it damage my pavers or stucco?+
Not the way I do it. I adjust pressure for every surface. Concrete gets 3,000+ PSI. Stucco and screens get soft wash under 1,000. That’s the difference between someone who knows what they’re doing and someone who just bought a pressure washer.
How often should I pressure wash in Florida?+
Driveways and walkways once or twice a year. Pool decks quarterly if you want them perfect. Home exteriors annually. Our humidity grows stuff fast.
Do you use chemicals?+
For concrete, mostly high-pressure water. For soft wash (exteriors, screens), I use biodegradable, plant-safe solutions that kill algae at the root. Safe for your landscaping and pets.
Can you remove oil stains?+
Honestly, I can significantly reduce most oil stains with degreasing agents and hot water. Fresh stains respond best. Old deep ones lighten but may not fully disappear. I’ll be straight with you about what’s realistic during the estimate.
